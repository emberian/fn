## 2. What serves today, and what does not
Served: CAPABILITIES HELP QUIT MODE DATE AUTHINFO XREDEEM, three LIST variants; a POST reaches the transaction and is refused by word (no durable member). Interim (`*fn2-answer-interim*`, 23 rows): seventeen reader verbs (the cursor), POST's success (B-S2), STARTTLS, IHAVE/CHECK/TAKETHIS (peer path), NEWNEWS/NEWGROUPS (date map). No consumer verb serves. No peer, no TLS, no credential store, no operator verb. Every one of these is a declaration away once its brick has the word.

## 4. The approach
1. **The brick lane is the critical path and is measured by what it unlocks.** G2 unlocks the cursor, the transaction port and the consumer machine at once; P4 unlocks the connection port; G3 unlocks the sealer's `:sub` and per-path holds. The form seat lands G2 before anything else, and its READY names the three kinds it unlocked. No feature seat waits idle for a brick: it writes its declaration in the brick's syntax ahead of the brick (D's P.1-P.5 and E's E.P1 are the pattern), so the landing is a paste and a certify.
2. **A feature slice is named by the verbs it flips.** Its acceptance is the interim JSON shrinking by those rows and H's nightly families green on both carriers; its design is the layer record's section rewritten as built. A slice that flips no verb and lands no producer is design, not a slice, and stays in the document (D46; the wire-words rule).
3. **Vocabulary before bodies.** When a declaration cannot say what a kind needs, the ask goes to the brick's owner as an exact form (F1-F14 are the shape) and the brick grows; the feature never carries a hand body "for now". The owner places the ask in a named commit and the asker builds against it.
4. **Statement-first, then build in the same seat, in the same day.** A statement is a page of a layer record with its keystones and seams, read once by Astra, and the seat builds it immediately; designs that outrun their next served slice (models, witnesses, schemas for verbs that do not serve) stop at the statement.
5. **Order of the meat: M2's spine first, breadth behind it.** The cursor, durable POST, the article's octets, then the consumer machine serve M2 ("a consumer appends, reads, waits and acks on the laptop, and an NNTP reader sees the same article"). TLS, credentials, peering, BP, checkpoints and operator verbs come after, each by the same rule. A seat whose next spine piece waits on a brick does its layer's breadth that waits on nothing (C's §4a, B's envelope, D7's ledger ops) rather than new design.
6. **The seats stay long-lived and own their layer**; main keeps this table and §5's dependency order current as present state, carries seams as exact forms, reads the books the lanes name before ruling, and runs no gates. The flow is WORKFLOW-20261031.md.
