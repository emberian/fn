## 0. The answer in one page

**ACL2 is the right home for the instances and the wrong engineering home for the frame** (§1): every generated square, kit instance, functional instantiation and guard is a statement about named functions of one world and ACL2 proves it where the code is; the frame-level theorems quantify over declarations, theories and diagrams, and Lean with Mathlib holds them. Since ember's order of 2026-10-31 the Lean side is also **a model of the machine itself**, read from the world, never transcribed.

**What exists** (dev at cc3750e91; books at 24fce1ecd):
- **The metatheory** (§2, unchanged): the thin double category of squares over `RelCat`, generated-diagram coherence with its satisfiable instance and false-diamond teeth, conservativity of an `ext` revision, restricted-client parametricity.
- **The registry, aligned** (§3): one printer prints every declaration table of the world that includes `reference.lisp`, and the emitter writes `Fn2.Instances.LayerA`: 9 machines with their own case catalogues (93 cases), 51 columns tables, 51 core children, 425 squares, 151 declared costs, 3 wait kinds and 187 other rows (operation sets, commands, schemas, layouts, arm contracts). A capacity, an initialiser, a cost bound and a square's statement are terms ACL2 translated; structured payloads are ACL2 objects; `Opaque` is gone. The key rule holds: a row key neither consumed nor omitted by name stops the emission.
- **The embedding** (§4, §5): 758 ACL2 functions of fn2's closed subset (the closure of echo's and scribe's child-form arms through bodies, guards and measures) as Lean definitions over ACL2's universe, 567 guards as preconditions, ACL2's own definitions of the natives; the step language as a Lean inductive with a total definitional interpreter in child form. The refusal list on this world is empty but for the natives' own rational definitions and the core-form listener.
- **Proofs about the embedding**: eight natives satisfy ACL2's definitions (`Fn2.ACL2.Faithful`); the four recursive translations the arms reach are fuel-adequate (`Fn2.ACL2.Fuel`). 80 census entries, standard axioms only.
- **The measure** (§6): on 963 calls ACL2 executed while the A4 smoke's echo and scribe scenarios ran, the interpreter on the declarations, the translated generated functions and the translated guards all agree with ACL2; a generator-only mutation makes 139 of the generated ones disagree while the interpreter stays equal.
- **The pathway** (§9): a statement-first design for a Lean-native path to the served core's executable, five stages with statements, teeth and status, and the decision it needs from ember first (§12, decision 6).

**What it does not do** (§10): it proves no instance (no twin of an ACL2 proof), it does not yet prove the generator correct per arm (the measure is evidence; PW3 is the theorem), and nothing here gates a seat.

## 1. The decision rule: what is a Lean theorem, what is an ACL2 instance

*Astra's section A (A1), adopted with the Opus wording of the rule's first line.*

A seat classifies a new obligation by its **subject**, without asking:

1. **About named ACL2 functions, one generated representation, one concrete square or one admitted book: ACL2.** The applicability conditions of a functional instantiation, a converter's relation, guard obligations, arithmetic bounds, preservation of outputs and effects. "Quantifies over every execution" alone does not move a statement to Lean: ACL2 proves a named machine's theorem for every admitted execution.
2. **Quantifies over declaration presentations, theories, interpretations, clients of an abstract interface, or constructions of diagrams: Lean.** Its hypotheses are stated as the properties the ACL2 instances supply.
3. **About the correspondence between an exported row and its ACL2 subject: the bridge** (§8), neither an execution theorem nor a categorical one.
4. **About physical execution, environmental evidence or eventual progress: neither becomes a coherence theorem by being registered.** X rows, liveness and fairness keep their adapter, persistence and fairness premises explicit (COHERENCE §2.3).

|---|---|---|---|

## 4. The embedding: fn2's closed subset in Lean

### 4.1 The reader
`fn2/meta/emit/print-world.lisp` (raw Lisp, loaded into a world that includes `fn2/books/reference`; never a certifying event) prints one S-expression, `fn2/meta/emit/world.sexp`, in two parts. **The registry**: every declaration table row for row (§3). **The embedding**: for every kind declared in the step language, its arms **as the declaration writes them** (each entry after its tag, `:from`, `:on-refusal` and the steps, untouched: the Lean side reads the steps and their expressions itself, §5, so neither the generator's expression rewrite `fn2-arms-expr` nor a decoder in the printer or the emitter is on the interpreter's path, decision 7), each phase's value, each arm's narrowed signature (`fn2-arms-child-sigs`), the declared operations the arms call with their callees' formals and stobj signatures, every integer constant the arms name, and the **function closure**: every function reachable from the generated child-form arm functions, the operations' callees, the row readers and setters and the stop, park and wake operations, through bodies, guards and measures, each with its translated body (`unnormalized-body`), guard, measure and recursion. `emit_embed.py` reads that part and writes `Fn2/Instances/LayerAWorld.lean` (the functions) and `Fn2/Instances/LayerAKinds.lean` (the kinds, operations, wait kinds and constants as `Fn2.Arms.World`).

### 4.2 The semantics
`Fn2.ACL2.Val` is ACL2's universe on the subset: integers, symbols by printed name, strings, characters, conses. A stobj is its logical value (a true list of fields, an array field the list of its elements), so every stobj accessor and updater is translated from its own logical definition like any other function. A translated function is a Lean `def` over `Val` whose body is the translated term read construct for construct: a variable, a quoted object, `IF` (lazy), `RETURN-LAST` (its last argument, ACL2's logical meaning of `mbe` and `prog2$`), a call, a closed `LAMBDA` (a beta-redex). Its guard is a separate `def F.guard`, a precondition, never assumed by the body. A recursive function is `F.go` with fuel and `F x = F.go (measure x + 1) x`, its ACL2 measure translated; ACL2's admitted measure makes the fuel sufficient (PW1, §9; proved for the four recursive functions the arms' operations call, `Fn2.ACL2.Fuel`; 125 of the 758 translations are recursive, most of them recognizers reached through guards, and the other 121 are owed). Primitives (`Fn2.ACL2.Prim`) carry ACL2's completions (a non-number argument of arithmetic is 0, `CAR` of an atom is `NIL`).

### 4.3 The natives, and the measure of the subset
The closure is cut at the natives, the ACL2 built-ins implemented in Lean (`Fn2.ACL2.Native`, 35 names): the primitives with completions; `IF` and `RETURN-LAST` as forms; `NTH MV-NTH UPDATE-NTH LEN MEMBER-EQUAL` (stobj arrays are lists); the bitwise operations, `FLOOR MOD EVENP NONNEGATIVE-INTEGER-QUOTIENT EXPT ACL2-COUNT` (whose ACL2 definitions recurse through rationals or bits); `RATIONALP` and `COERCE` (reached only through guards). **The refusal list is the measure of the subset**, and today it reads:
- functions with no body outside the natives (the printer's `:refused`): **none** in the closure of the generated child-form arm functions of echo and scribe, the operations' callees, the row readers and setters and the stop, park and wake operations;
- functions dropped as callers of a refused function: **none**;
- mutually recursive functions, a recursion whose measure is not `(NFIX ...)` or `(ACL2-COUNT ...)`, a quoted rational or complex: **none** (each stops the emission by name);
- guards dropped for calling outside the subset (`guardOutside`): **none**;
- `UNARY-/`, `NUMERATOR`, `DENOMINATOR`, `COMPLEX`, `REALPART`, `IMAGPART`: outside, reached only inside the natives' own ACL2 definitions (`nativeOutside`);
- `EXPT` with a negative exponent and a base other than 0, 1, -1: outside (its value is a non-integer rational); the native returns 0 there;
- core-form kinds (a kind whose arms the world defines only over `fn2-core`): printed as terms, refused by the interpreter by name. Today: `FN2-LISTENER`, whose arms are registry arms (P4's open item).
On 24fce1ecd (the books; the Lean on dev at cc3750e91) the closure is 758 functions (567 with a stated guard), 2 kinds in child form (`FN2-ECHO`, `FN2-SCRIBE`; the listener printed in core form), 34 declared operations, 95 constants.

### 4.4 Faithfulness of the natives
`Fn2.ACL2.Faithful`: for each native whose ACL2 definition stays in the subset, the emitter prints that definition as `«F».acl2` with every native call (its own recursion included) going to the Lean natives, and `Faithful` says the native satisfies ACL2's defining equation, which ACL2's admitted measure makes unique. **Proved** (elaborated, standard axioms only): `NTH`, `MV-NTH`, `UPDATE-NTH`, `LEN`, `MEMBER-EQUAL`, `LOGNOT`, `MOD`, `NONNEGATIVE-INTEGER-QUOTIENT`. **Owed** (stated): `BINARY-LOGIOR`, `BINARY-LOGXOR`, `BINARY-LOGEQV`, `LOGORC1` (defined through `BINARY-LOGAND`). **Trusted as written**: `BINARY-LOGAND` (its definition calls `ZIP` and recurses through `FLOOR`), `FLOOR`, `EVENP`, `EXPT`, `ACL2-COUNT` (defined through rationals); `MOD`'s theorem is relative to `FLOOR`. Each statement is about the subset's representable values (integers): `EXPT 2 -1` has no `Val`. `UPDATE-NTH` runs through an iterative `implemented_by` (stobj arrays are long lists); the logical definition is the recursive one the theorem is about.

## 5. The step language and its definitional interpreter
`Fn2.Arms` holds MACHINE-FORM §6.2's step language as an inductive (`let set next do issue repeat wake-row if cond case` and the terminals `charge wait park stop halt idle yield wake nowake goto`) and `runArm`, a total (fuel) definitional interpreter in **child form**: the state is the kind's tuple of children as `Val`s; the result is the arm function's `mv` list. Expressions are the declaration's own S-expressions, read by `readExpr` into ACL2 terms and evaluated over the arm's bindings (`ID SLOT KIND ORDINAL` and `BUDGET` or the observation payload, every `ROW-F` read at entry from the kind's table by slot or the directory by id, `:let` and `:do` bindings, the children); operations are the translated functions. **The step reader** (`Fn2.Arms.readSteps`, `Arm.read`) reads each arm's steps from the declaration's S-expression as MACHINE-FORM §6.2 writes them (`:from` and `:on-refusal` in either order, as `fn2-arms-options`; a call's positional arguments until its first keyword; a literal `:when nil` as no condition, as the generator reads it; anything else refused by name). **The expression reader** (`Fn2.Arms.readExpr`, `readNext`; decision 7) is written from ACL2's definitions of the vocabulary's macros in `axioms.lisp` (`+ * - 1+ 1- < <= > >= = /= eq eql not null and or cond case logand logior mv prog2$ member-eq`, each with ACL2's arities and empty and unary cases; `case` with ACL2's clause shape, eqlable keys, a `nil` key clause dropped unread and a compound key bound once) and MACHINE-FORM §6.3, never from the generator's output: `ROW-SLOT` is the `SLOT` formal, `*K-KIND*` and `*K-ADMISSION-ORDINAL*` the `KIND` and `ORDINAL` formals (the child-form convention, not constant evaluation), another `*C*` its printed value, a phase keyword its state constant, `(:if ...)` and `(:case ...)` phase forms nested `IF`s; a function of the vocabulary is a call of the translated function at its arity; a declared pure operation is a call of its `:child` with its positional arguments and then the kind's children by name (its stobj formals must trail, as the generator appends them); anything else, an application with a dotted tail or a wrong arity is refused by name. The measure runs `readKind` over every arm and expression of every child-form kind before any arm runs (268 in echo and scribe, none refused), checks every arm's metadata (`ArmWF`) and that every vocabulary function resolves to a native or a translation (the printer roots them in the closure). Not a full ACL2 admission checker: binding legality and `mv` context are the generator's, already checked at certification. The control is the interpreter's own, written from MACHINE-FORM §6.2-6.10 and the generator `machine-arms.lisp` and checked by the measure, never by reading the generated code: writes are batched and written by one checked `PUT` (slot and id in range, every value within its column's width or enum count, else `:machine-write` and nothing written) before each operation call and each terminal, the phase last; a `:do` fills its callee's formals (positional, the stobjs from the tuple, the owner triple `okind oslot ogen` from the row, any other by `:FORMAL E`), binds its results, and routes its code (`:accept`, the default `(:ok)` exactly when the generator gives it, the first `:case` naming the code, `:otherwise`, the arm's `:on-refusal`, else `(:stop :machine-contract)`); an `:issue` routes `:ok`, `:exhausted` (refused steps, then a park on the operation FIFO) and any other answer (refused steps, then `:on-refusal` or `:halt`); `:goto` runs the sub-arm with fresh row reads; `:repeat` runs at most N passes, each reading the row afresh. Since 24fce1ecd a child-form function takes and returns only the children its paths touch (`fn2-arms-child-sigs`); the interpreter keeps the kind's whole tuple, and the measure maps each recorded call into it and projects the result back.

## 6. The measure
**Method.** `fn2/meta/emit/record-arms.lsp` loads the simulator (`fn2/tests/sim-layer-a.lisp`), wraps every child-form arm, handler and sub-arm function the world defines, and runs the A4 smoke's two scenarios: echo (seed 1234, 3 connections, the tiny profile with `:trace`, because the smoke's concrete profile carries a 7 MB pool on every call) and scribe (exactly as the smoke runs it: 19 cases, its 4 KiB-sector profile). Each sampled call (the first 24 per function, then every 41st, at most 48 more) is one line: the function, the logical values of its arguments and of its results (array fields run-length encoded). `lake exe fn2-measure RECORD` computes for each call (a) **interp**: `runArm` on the arm's declaration, (b) **generated**: the translation of the generated function itself, (c) **guard**: the translated guard on the recorded arguments, and (d) **frame**: since 24fce1ecd a child-form function takes and returns only the children its paths touch, while the interpreter runs on the kind's whole tuple, so every child the function does not return must leave the interpreter as it entered (a child it takes enters with its recorded value; a child it does not take enters as a sentinel symbol no operation accepts, so a declaration that reads it differs in (a)); (a) and (b) are compared with the recorded result.

## 7. How a Lean theorem licenses an ACL2 check

*Astra's section C (A1), adopted; the Lean names are the ones built.*

**The precise sentence.** For the stated frame and observation contract, if its local generators and compatibility obligations hold, every diagram constructed from those generators preserves that contract. "Every route agrees" adds a premise: the routes are connected by such a diagram (here, `TraceEq` under the declared independence). Showing that every intended interchangeable pair is so connected is completeness, a separate theorem (§10).

**Who supplies what.**

```text
Lean kernel:  coherence / q1_licensed       (the implication; parameterised by the premises)
ACL2:         one theorem per bound case     (a premise; admitted, or owed with check evidence)
the bridge:   binds that theorem's translated statement to that premise
```

Nothing is imported: no ACL2 proof object enters Lean, and no Lean axiom is generated from a proof-status row (A1). The Lean theorem stays parameterised by realisations of its premises, and the owed export records, per premise, an ACL2 proof, check evidence, or nothing.

**What crosses the boundary**, per bound obligation: the qualified owner and case identity; template, boundary and contract identity; the endpoint and helper function names (`FN2-TRANSITION`, `FN2-EXEC-ACCOUNT-WORK`, `FN2-REF-TRANSITION`, and DSL-METATHEORY §3.3's `fn2-q1-*` helpers once they exist); formals and calling convention; the source and translated formula; the proof reference (an admitted theorem whose formula is the translated one, never a matching name); the check reference (revision, profile, domain, horizon, counts, verdict); and the dependencies. A theorem proving only `fn2-core-wfp`, or the claim under an extra unestablished hypothesis, is not a match.

**When the ACL2 side is checked, not proved.** The row stays owed. A checked G1 instance is a fact about the recorded related states and transition; a checked G2 instance about the two orders exercised at that state; Lean's implication then yields a conclusion **about the runs the checks covered**, only where the intermediate states, interpretations and contracts actually join (A1's fused/unfused example: a shared recorded trajectory, never an intersection of seed numbers). A general Lean theorem never promotes finite observations to a universal premise.

**The licence for Q1, exactly.** `q1_licensed h R cases : ∀ w, (∀ c ∈ w, c ∈ q1Cases) → Sq R (run stepA w) (run stepB (expand h w)) R`, from `cases : ∀ c ∈ q1Cases, Sq R (stepA c) (run stepB (h c)) R`. With `stepA` the concrete transition selected by case, `stepB` the reference transition, `R` the normalised loop relation: if ACL2 establishes the 39 case squares, every turn word over them is a square. The entry, exit, termination, accounting and selector-coverage rows of DSL-METATHEORY §3.3 remain ACL2's, and `fn2-turn-refinement` is their composition, not a consequence of the licence alone.

## 8. The trust story, and the mutation controls

### 8.1 The one new assumption (A1, adopted)

Let `W` be the recorded ACL2 world, `E` the export (`print-registry.lsp` then `emit_lean.py`), `P = E(W)` the emitted presentation and `J` the interpretation a theorem takes as parameters. The assumption:

> For every declaration, case and obligation a claimed result uses, `P` identifies the corresponding subject in `W` without omission or conflation; and the premise `J` gives it is the property the recorded ACL2 formula states about that subject, with the same domains, boundaries, outputs, effects and observation contract.

Its clauses: **identity** (qualified names designate the same declarations, functions and events), **payload** (values and order retained; unsupported payloads explicit), **artifact** (the subjects are what was actually admitted, including assembly-section artifacts), **obligation** (catalogue entries become the intended case statements; coverage is never inferred from a catalogue's existence), **semantic** (the premise means what the formula states; opaque names do not discharge this clause), **evidence** (proof and check records belong to that subject and revision). This is the bridge's assumption, not the system's only one: ACL2's and Lean's kernels, the execution model's environment assumptions and native execution keep their own.

### 8.2 Control 1 (historical, at 137695dfc): a row-only mutation changes the model

Mutation: `defmachine`'s registry row records `:observes ,(cdr observes)` (`machines.lisp:236`), so each kind's row drops its first observed event. Run: `fn2/meta/emit/mutations/observes-row.sh` (re-certify, print, emit, restore, re-certify; its logs and mutant files landed in that worktree's `build/lean-meta-scratch/`; the scripts are kept, marked historical).

- **ACL2 refuses the mutant first**: `certify.sh books/reference.lisp` fails at `assembly.lisp`, in the guard proof of the generated router `FN2-MACHINE-OBSERVE` (`build/lean-meta-scratch/mut-certify.log:217`), because the router is generated from the row. The world can still be loaded without proofs, and the printer reads it.
- **The emitted model changes**: 17 changed lines (`emit/mutations/observes-row.diff`): three `observes` entries gone (`:accept-result`, `:receive-result`, `:write-result`), two symbols gone (376 to 374), events 9 to 6, machine-owned cases 31 to 28; **`q1_case_counts` (31, 39) no longer holds**, so the facts file stops typechecking by name. The diff also reorders `Sym`'s constructors (first-occurrence order), which a reviewer reads past.

**Control 1b, a field-width mutation ACL2 accepts (A1's control 1).** Mutation: the common `deadline` field narrows from `:u60` to `:u32` (`machines.lisp:8`). ACL2 certifies the mutant. The emitted model changes in exactly the 6 rows that carry the field, 12 changed lines (`emit/mutations/deadline-width.diff`): the three machine rows and the three machines' own `defcolumns` rows; `:layout` and `:packed`, which also change in the world, are omitted from the model by name. **No control fails**: the diff is the evidence, and a reviewer checks that both identities of each machine changed and nothing came from a hand-maintained fixture.

### 8.3 Control 2 (historical, at 137695dfc): a behaviour-only mutation did not change the table-only model

Mutation: `NAME-OBSERVES-P` stops excluding the `:retired` phase (`machines.lisp:230`); the row is untouched. Run: `fn2/meta/emit/mutations/retired-behaviour.sh`.

ACL2 certifies the mutant. **The printed registry is byte-identical to the unmutated one, so the emitted model is unchanged** (0-line diff; `build/lean-meta-scratch/mutation2.log`): a table-only exporter cannot see a change to generated behaviour. This was the measured limit A1 predicted, and the case for decision 3; the embedding now carries generated behaviour as translated bodies, so a behaviour-only change to a function in the closure changes `LayerAWorld`: the `:generated` manifest must bind each emitted name to its admitted body (opaque text with a fingerprint) before the export can claim artifact fidelity.

**What a reviewer looks for.** For a row-visible mutation: both identities that carry the changed field changed (a machine row and, where it shares the field, its `defcolumns` row), every affected row was emitted, nothing was copied from a hand-maintained Lean fixture, and a pinned control fails by name. For a behaviour-only mutation: the export must either show a changed artifact payload (once the `:generated` manifest of DSL-METATHEORY §2.2 binds names to admitted bodies, emitted as opaque text with fingerprints) or report the artifact as uncovered by name. A source-revision change alone invalidates freshness but shows no artifact was captured.

### 8.4 The embedding's trust, and its control
The embedding adds to §8.1's assumption, by name: **the printer** (world selection, the closure's completeness, the signatures, the constants, the declaration decoding; since decision 7 it no longer borrows the generator's expression rewrite, so a defect there is not shared, §6); **the readers** (`emit_lean.py`'s tokenizer and `val`, `emit_embed.py`'s shallow and deep translations, `Fn2.ACL2.Read`, and `Fn2.Arms.readExpr`, checked by the measure, not proved); **the semantics** (`Prim`'s completions, the natives not proved faithful, `UPDATE-NTH`'s `implemented_by` iteration, whose equivalence Lean does not check, `RETURN-LAST` read as its last argument); **the oracle** (the recorder's sampling, its stobj conversion through the stobjs' own accessors, raw-Lisp execution of guard-verified code). The interpreter's control is the generator mutation of §6: a generator-only change shows as generated ≠ recorded with interp = recorded. A defect in the generator's expression rewrite is caught the same way (§6, the rewrite mutation).

### 8.5 What Lean can never establish here

Soundness of ACL2 or of its implementation; the truth of an opaque formula; that a `defthm` named in a row has the advertised statement because the name exists (only a carried translated formula makes the comparison mechanical, and even then the semantic clause needs a justified interpretation); completeness of a case catalogue because every emitted row typechecks; universal validity from bounded checks; durability, fairness or environmental integrity from simulation squares; that every intended fn2 route equality follows from the toy's generators (A1).

## 9. The pathway: a Lean-native high-assurance path for the served core (statement-first)

*Opus draft written when the measure held for echo and scribe; read once by Astra (session 4, §11), whose corrections are adopted throughout.*

### 9.1 What it would replace, and what "higher assurance" has to mean
The planned path is ATC's: the forms generate ACL2 defuns; ATC, inside ACL2, generates C and, per instance, an ACL2 proof that the C computes the defuns under ATC's formal C semantics (with its execution-limit and memory and non-aliasing hypotheses); a C compiler builds it. ATC is already proof-generating, so **"higher assurance than ATC" is today an ambition, not an established comparison** (Astra). It becomes a claim only against a matched baseline: the Lean path must give at least ATC's executable correspondence and safety scope, and add what ATC does not give fn2:
1. **reach**: ATC accepts a flat subset (EXECUTION-MODEL §5) and every kind records its lowering as an unmet capability (DSL-METATHEORY §1.2); a lowering built for exactly fn2's closed subset (§4), whose edge is measured, can cover the served kinds;
2. **declaration-to-executable correctness**: nothing today proves a generated arm function is what its declaration says (ACL2 proves the generated code's shape and frame properties); PW3 states it;
3. **the bridge assumptions discharged or named**: the reader adds a trusted boundary (§8.4); a stronger path either proves the pieces (PW1, PW2) or keeps them as named assumptions beside ATC's.
"Two kernels" means different obligations checked in different systems (ACL2: the instances; Lean: the translation, the generator, the refinement, the lowering), not two independent proofs of one end-to-end claim.

### 9.2 The stages, each with its statement
Notation: `encode : ACL2 object → Val` (the reader's representation; the identity on the subset); `Supported_F` the arguments on which `F`'s guard holds and every intermediate value is representable.

| stage | statement | status | teeth (a counterexample, not a failed proof) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PW0** ACL2 | guard verification, the squares, `fn2-turn-refinement` | ACL2's, unchanged; no Lean twin | an uncertified book |
| **PW1** the reader | for each translated `F`: `∀ x ∈ Supported_F, encode (F_ACL2 x) = «F» (encode x)`, with the same for every function `F` calls. A bridge statement (§1 row 3): no Lean proof alone establishes it, since `F_ACL2` lives in ACL2. Its Lean-provable parts: the body is read construct for construct (by construction), and for a recursive `F` **fuel adequacy**, `∀ k x, F.go (measure x + 1 + k) x = F.go (measure x + 1) x` | adequacy **proved for the 4 recursive translations the arms' operations reach** (`Fn2.ACL2.Fuel`); owed for the other 121 recursive translations (mostly recognizers reached through guards); the correspondence itself is evidence only (§6), and agreement on recorded calls cannot establish it | a recorded call where the translation's value differs from ACL2's (the measure's `generated` column) |
| **PW2** the natives | `∀ x ∈ Val^n, Native.f x = «F».acl2 x` (the native satisfies ACL2's defining equation over the subset's representable values; with ACL2's admitted measure the equation has one solution) | **proved for 8** (`NTH MV-NTH UPDATE-NTH LEN MEMBER-EQUAL LOGNOT MOD NONNEGATIVE-INTEGER-QUOTIENT`; `MOD` relative to `FLOOR`); stated, owed for 4 (`BINARY-LOGIOR -LOGXOR -LOGEQV LOGORC1`); trusted as written for 5 (`BINARY-LOGAND FLOOR EVENP EXPT ACL2-COUNT`); `EXPT` off the integer domain is outside | arguments where the native and the definition's body differ |

The composed claim, once PW1-PW5b hold: every execution of the lowered C for a served kind implements the declaration and refines the ACL2 machine whose properties ACL2 proved, assuming the ACL2 and Lean kernels, the reader's remaining trusted parts (§8.4), the C compiler (as ATC also assumes) and EXECUTION-MODEL's environment assumptions (adapter, persistence, fairness), which no stage discharges.

### 9.3 PW3, stated (`Fn2.Pathway`, the next milestone by decision 6; Astra session 5's form)
**Statement.** For a printed world `w` (its kinds, operations, constants and the translated dispatch `w.fns`), its guard table `guard`, a kind `k` and one of its arms, handlers or sub-arms `a` whose generated child-form function is `FN = a.fn` with narrowed signature (`takes`, `returns`):

`ArmCorrect w guard k a observe :≡ ArmWF k a observe ∧ ∀ pre given, |pre| = nPre ∧ |given| = |takes| ∧ Guarded guard FN (pre ++ given) → ∃ v, w.fns FN (pre ++ given) = some v ∧ NarrowShape v ∧ ∀ rest, ∃ N, ∀ fuel ≥ N, runArm w k a observe (widen pre given rest) fuel = .ok (reconstruct (entering given rest) (normalize v))`

- `ArmWF` (decidable): a child-form kind, a duplicate-free tuple, a signature inside it without duplicates, its result count the arm's. A wrong signature makes `ArmCorrect` false, never vacuous.
- `Guarded`: a stated guard holds; a function with no stated guard has ACL2's guard `T`, so a missing table entry strengthens the statement instead of emptying it.
- `NarrowShape v`: `FN`'s value is a true list of exactly its results and returned children.
- `widen` places the taken children into the kind's whole tuple and fills the others from the arbitrary `rest`; `reconstruct` is the whole result `FN`'s value determines: its results, each returned child from `v` by name, every other child as it entered. Equality with it is the projection, the frame and the exact shape at once.
- `KindCorrect` is the conjunction over a kind's arms, handlers and sub-arms.

The interpreter reads the declaration through the independent reader (§5), so a proof of `KindCorrect` says: on every guarded input, the code the generator emitted computes what the declaration means under MACHINE-FORM's semantics as `Fn2.Arms` writes it. It is per instance: the generator is ACL2 code and is not quantified over. `N` is termination, not a work bound (bounded work is PW5b's).

**What the quantifiers buy, and what they do not.** `∃ v, w.fns FN … = some v` fails for a function the world does not define; the fuel quantifier needs termination, not agreement at one fuel. `rest` is universal, so the result may not depend on a child `FN` does not take: this is output independence, not absence of reads (a declaration that reads such a child and discards the value satisfies it).

**Evidence, satisfiability and teeth.** The guard premise is inhabited: the 963 recorded argument lists satisfy it. The measure computes the conclusion's equation on each recorded input with the sentinel `rest` at fuel 100,000 and finds it true 963 times: finite observations of the conclusion, not instances of the quantified statement. Each mutation of §6 gives a world in which `KindCorrect` is false, a recorded call the witness: the generator's wait default (echo's command, reply and closing arms), the frame's dropped return (scribe's `:RESET`), the expression-rewrite mutation (echo's draining arm).

**What remains shared or trusted** (Astra session 5): the declaration's decoding is no longer (the printer prints each arm as written and `Fn2.Arms.readSteps` reads it, §5); function naming, child-form detection, the whole tuple (`fn2-arms-kind-tuple`: a child missing from it is outside the frame) and the narrowed signatures; the phase constants, columns, operation table, waits, and the translated operations, accessors, setters and guards, which both sides call (their faithfulness is PW1's and PW2's, not PW3's). PW3 composed with PW1 says the same of ACL2's `FN`.

**Proof plan** (session 5's order). Total `readExpr`, `readNext` and `Term.eval` by structural recursion on the syntax (finite, so no fuel); per-name dispatch and guard equations generated beside the dispatch table, so a proof rewrites to the named callee without unfolding 758 entries; checked-write, accessor, updater and frame lemmas proved once over list-valued stobjs, operations left opaque where both sides call the same one (scribe's fill, patch and verify are such `.do_` callees, so their induction is PW1's adequacy, not each arm's); fuel stability of a successful run, with bounds built compositionally (the `:repeat` helper's zero fuel is now an error). Echo's eight arms first, then scribe's.

### 9.4 What stays ACL2's and what is Lean's (§1 applied)
ACL2 keeps every statement about named functions of one world: guards, the instance squares, the refinement theorem, the forms' expansion checks, certification; Lean never re-proves them. Lean takes the translation's Lean-side parts (PW1's adequacy, PW2), the generator's per-instance correctness (PW3, which quantifies over a declaration's presentation), the typed refinement (PW4) and the lowering (PW5b), which ACL2 could state but fn2 would have to build. An ACL2 theorem crosses to Lean only as a premise the bridge names (§7), never as an axiom.

### 9.5 The order
1. PW3 for echo and scribe as theorems (stated, §9.3): make `readExpr` and `Term.eval` total and the dispatch a `match`, then symbolic evaluation per arm; this turns the measure into proofs, and is the milestone decision 6 makes immediate.
2. PW1's adequacy for the remaining recursive translations (one schema, as `Fn2.ACL2.Fuel`), and PW2's four owed natives.
3. PW4 for one columns table and one kind's operations (scribe), generated, with the narrowed-width counterexample as teeth.
4. PW5b's statement once decision 6 names the C semantics; PW5a meanwhile as a third execution in the measure.
Each kind that lands in child form joins the measure by printing (the cursor, when its books join `reference.lisp`'s closure); nothing in this order gates a seat.

## 10. What is not claimed

- **Frame completeness** (COHERENCE §3.2: every pair of intended interchangeable routes is connected by generated diagrams plus declared route equations). Not stated in Lean: its "intended interchangeable" relation is a design decision per contract that no document has fixed for any fragment, and a Lean `Prop` over an invented relation would be a mirror. For the toy it would be Mazurkiewicz's projection characterisation (equal dependence-clique projections ⇒ `TraceEq`), a known theorem worth about two days; it becomes useful when a fragment (summary replacement with admitted suffixes, or one class of environment reorderings) has its relation declared.
