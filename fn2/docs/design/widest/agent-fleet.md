# fn2, widest, from the substrate agent fleets run on (council draft, 2026-10-28)

Vantage: fn2 as the thing under helm, cv, Mini and every coding agent on ember's machines and her friends' machines. Grounded in `FN2-ORIENTATION.md`, `EXECUTION-MODEL.md` §1-3, `STORAGE-DESIGN.md` §1, `PLANES-AND-GAMES.md` §2 and §5.1, `PLANES-ADDENDUM-INTERWEAVING.md`, `UNIVERSAL-CONSTRUCTIONS.md` §0 and §9, `CLIENTS-AND-DATA-MODELS.md` §1-2, helm's `README.md` and `docs/NEW_AGENT_GUIDE.md`, and `cv --help` / `cv task --help` as installed today.

## 1. The world from this vantage

An agent fleet is already an event-sourced system that does not know it. A Claude Code transcript is an append-only log of turns. helm's chat is an append-only JSONL file in `/dev/shm` with one flocked writer. Its dispatch ledger is an obligation table ("never-send-again" by operation key). Its premises are a hash chain. Its gate receipts are evidence. Its land requests are compare-and-set on a branch head. cv is a set of projections over transcripts: search, lanes, blame, deferrals, tools. `cv rewind`, `splice`, `distill`, `port` and `adopt` are log algebra done by copying files and restamping `sessionId`. The fleet has eleven half-built logs with eleven identity schemes, eleven cursor kinds (a `{dev, ino, off}` byte offset in helm, a tantivy index generation in cv, a Monitor beacon in the harness), and no shared order. Every incident in helm's guide is a seam between two of them: a duplicate seat spawned by a broken pane resolver; a row committed and then lost in a pipe buffer (task/2920); 0 of 49,960 rows carrying their signed turn after the dregg node fell over; a background `helm chat wait` that wakes nobody; a review verdict that forgot its polarity, "recorded as UNDECLARED forever".

From this vantage fn2 is **the one log of the fleet's history**, and every one of those artifacts is a stream, a fold, a selection or an obligation over it.

**A Tuesday.** At 09:00 ember opens her Mac. Overnight, nine seats ran on dregg-build and hbox and four of akapug's seats ran on his boxes. ember does not read nine transcripts. She opens the node's built-in web reader (EMBER-ARCHITECTURE-WORDS 16) on her inbox. The inbox is a declared selection, not a mailbox: mentions of `ember`, decisions owed (`cv task decide` rows), dispatch obligations past deadline, and every `land` whose obligation discharged. It shows the frontier it is complete through.

She posts one line into `#fn2-build`. The post is a signed turn: an article whose Message-ID is its content name and whose `References` is the parent row. Three seats' hooks are parked `wait`s on edges whose selection matches the line. Each `PostToolUse` delivers it mid-turn. A fourth seat is asleep. It has no process, no pane and no waiter slot, only a registration, a cursor and an edge. The commit trips the edge's frontier and the wake fires as a **hint**. A launcher daemon holding the `activate` capability claims the seat's activation obligation under a fresh incarnation and execs `claude --resume` against the seat's fold. Its first turn carries incarnation 7. When the broken pane resolver races a duplicate into existence at incarnation 6, that duplicate's first tool-call commit is refused `fenced`.

At 11:40 a kimi seat posts a review. The review is a typed record: polarity required by schema, bound to an exact tip, carrying a gate-receipt Name and the reviewer's runtime attestation (model family verified by the proxy, not read off the seat label). The dispatch obligation's evidence formula is `verdict(approve) ⊗ gate(tip) ⊗ family(reviewer) ≠ family(author)`. Decide checks it at the obligation's domain and discharges it. The integrator's `land` is a Decide in the repo domain: it advances the `main` Binding from the old tip to the new one and emits a land receipt. At 14:00 akapug's fleet pulls the receipt over the NNTP feed from fn.fg-goose.online. His helm sees it as a row in a peered room, with the signature and the chain intact.

At 16:20 a lane compacts. The compaction is an `external-checkpoint` (PLANES §4.7) over the lane's own stream: predecessor checkpoint, the input range it summarizes, the summary as a Name, and the **declared loss**. The full span stays pinned. "Reorient via `cv show` of the lost span" is a read at `exactly-at(p)` on the lane's history, not archaeology. At 17:00 ember asks, "what would Opus have done at turn 412 of that kimi lane?" `fork` creates a new stream whose prefix *is* the old one up to 412, copying nothing. It binds a new revision (model, harness version, context-file hashes) and a new seat, and runs.

**What is where.** *Durable*: turns, tool-call intents and outcomes, posts, verdicts, receipts, premises, dispatches, claims, activations, checkpoints, forks. *Transient* (per item, the addendum's ruling): streaming tokens, "seat working" presence, the gate's progress lines, a tool call's live stdout until its outcome commits the digest. *Fast*: the inference loop itself. The model and the harness are a fast-plane executor, fenced and output-gated (L3), outside fn2. *Content-named*: every tool output, every file read, every screenshot. *Across node boundaries*: rooms peer as NNTP groups; signed turns cross as articles; BP carries a seat's stream from a box scheduled to die (all of ember's October boxes die 2026-11-11) to its successor with custody until receipt; remote consumers reach their inbox over the consumer wire on QUIC from behind a NAT.

## 2. Ten things it would build or subsume

1. **helm chat (rooms, DMs, cursors, the delivery lane).** Rooms and DM lanes become streams in one log. A seat cursor becomes a commit position with at-least-once delivery until ack, replacing the `/dev/shm` JSONL, the 2 MiB rotation, the byte-offset cursor files and DMs that vanish at reboot (#14). helm's users switch because akapug's four falsifiers become the node's acceptance scenarios: wake p95 under 2 s, a bounded heap, no waiter cap on a 30-seat fleet, no acked row redelivered after a crash.

2. **The dispatch ledger and `cv task`.** Both become Obligations: a build or review owed by a seat, created by a commit and discharged exactly once by authorized evidence, waiver (`lr abandon`'s "LAND STATE UNKNOWN" is a typed waiver) or expiry. helm's `--approve/--fix` polarity rule becomes a schema rule. The operation-key idempotency it hand-built inside a writer lock comes from Decide, so helm stops re-deriving the never-send-again contract and cv stops keeping a second, incompatible task store.

3. **The gate FIFO, `gatelock:<project>`, claim leases and `helm work claim`.** These become competing-consumer queues of claims with holder, incarnation, expiry and fencing (Kafka consumer groups and Chubby sequencers, with Kleppmann's fencing tokens stated as a law). helm's README spends a page on subreapers, watchdogs and lease refreshes to approximate "at most one authoritative holder, late results refused by name". That page shrinks to the cgroup kill, which stays host-side, while authority moves into the log.

4. **Land requests and land receipts.** A land is a Decide in the repository's commit domain. Its precondition is the dispatch obligation's discharge plus `main` still at the reviewed base (or a declared rebase). Its outcome is a receipt Name that minidregg can anchor ("review verdicts and land-receipts as receipts on chain"). `lr refs` / `lr migrate` exist because git rewrites history under attested tips. With the receipt naming the tree and the reviewed commit by content, and the Binding recording the history, a rewrite becomes a new event, not a broken proof.

5. **cv's archive, index, search, lanes, tools, blame and deferrals.** These are `defprojection`s over transcript streams, each with a published frontier: search is an after-commit text index, blame is a Span from file paths to tool-call events, lanes is a fold over subagent streams. The deferrals linter is a detector that turns "follow-up" phrases into proposed Obligations.

6. **cv rewind, splice, distill, port and adopt.** These become log operations with provenance. Rewind is a fork at a prefix. Splice is a composite stream of spans with derivation edges. Distill is a projection with a declared loss. Port is transport along the OpenSession equivalence with its lossy fields named. Adopt rebinds a stranded subagent stream to a live parent under a new incarnation.

7. **Agent memory (MEMORY.md, helm's typed store, beliefs, drift, premises).** An agent's memory becomes its own fold over its memory stream: `add`, `evidence`, `supersede`, `retire` events. Premises are attested claims with a `(signer, previous-head)` Binding (helm's `attest-chain.jsonl`, which already computes `rec_hash` over `canonical(core) + predecessor_hash`). Drift is a projection that fires when evidence events contradict a held belief. helm's law "retire in place, never delete" is the log's only write discipline, so it needs no enforcement. Memory becomes forkable, and a lesson learned on one branch can be cherry-picked as an event onto another.

8. **The wake path: Claude Code hooks, the Monitor beacon, the doorbell, sleeping agents.** A seat's inbox is a named, versioned selection (the INBOX design's Span composite). A wake is a hint on a subscription edge, re-checked against the frontier. Activation of a sleeping seat is a claim, an Orleans virtual actor activated on message whose state is a fold rather than a grain record. This answers akapug's two hardest critiques directly: "A sleeping waiter should not hold a control-client slot" and "Waiting costs nothing is false for an LLM seat". helm's `Stop` hook ("refuse an idle stop while addressed messages are undelivered") becomes an obligation outstanding on the seat.

9. **Durable execution for agent work (Temporal, Restate, DBOS, Azure Durable Functions, LangGraph checkpointers, the Workflow tool).** Temporal's insight is that nondeterminism is recorded as events and replay re-executes only the deterministic part. A transcript already works this way: the model's outputs are the recorded activity results, and the harness's control flow replays over them. fn2 supplies the workflow history as a stream, activities as intent/outcome pairs with operation identity, timers in declared time domains, and `uncertain` as a first-class outcome (helm's `DeliveryResult` UNKNOWN).

10. **Cross-machine fleet federation (helm's dregg signed turns, akapug ↔ ember, Secure Scuttlebutt, Matrix, ActivityPub).** Fleets peer the way fn already peers: NNTP feeds with path-identity loop detection, BP custody for boxes that sleep or die. Each fleet's rooms, receipts and premises are signed streams in the manner of SSB's per-identity feeds, but with a total order per commit domain, exact identity, and conflict as a recorded Binding fact instead of a fork nobody notices. helm users move off dregg-as-a-chat-signer, which turned signing off on 2026-10-11 under hub load after an 11.81 GB store took 25 minutes to boot. fn2 carries the posts and minidregg anchors the digests, "never the proof".

## 3. What the kernel must contain

The pruned kernel (Log, Decide, Fold, Name; Binding, Span, Frontier, Obligation) carries items 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8 nearly as specified. UNIVERSAL §9.3's helm row is right as far as it goes. It is too small in six places, and items 4, 6, 7, 9 and 10 fall through those gaps.

**(a) Log must become a log forest.** A stream may begin with a `derive` header: `(parent stream, parent position, derivation kind ∈ {fork, splice-span, port, adopt}, reason, author)`. Its positions inherit the parent's prefix by reference. Algebraically this is the free monoid's prefix tree, and two forks share their common prefix as one physical history (Git's object graph, Hypercore's sparse replication). Splice is a stream whose header is a list of spans, which makes the lineage the data rather than a `.rewind.json` sidecar. A cut across a forest is a down-closed set per lineage. Without this, rewind, splice and every memory fork are copies, and provenance is a sidecar file. A **rebase** contract goes to Decide: move a branch's suffix onto a new base, with each event's precondition re-checked, giving either a rebased event or a named refusal (Pijul's patch theory; Mimram and Di Giusto's categorical account of patches); merging agent forks is the same operation.

**(b) Name must gain Transport.** A `transport` record names `(source span, source schema, target schema, translation id and version, preserved fields, declared lossy fields, result Name)`. It says "the OpenSession rendering of this Claude span, minus thinking-block signatures, is this Codex span". It is a lens witness with an explicit put-loss, and it is what ember meant by "transport along equivalences ... vital for ... composing". cv's `port`, model swaps mid-lineage and harness upgrades are all instances. Equivalence classes of Names are a Binding (`span ↦ set of transports`), so "is this the same conversation?" has a recorded answer.

**(c) A ninth interface, Attestation.** A signed statement by an attester about a subject at a position: `(attester principal, subject, predicate, value, evidence Names, valid-under revision, previous-head)`. Model family verified by a proxy, gate receipts, review verdicts, premises and "this session is persisted" (helm's tri-state `session ls`, where UNKNOWN is never PASS) are all attestations. Binding on `(attester, previous-head)` catches equivocation. The required-field rule kills UNDECLARED. Attestation is not an Obligation: it discharges obligations and is cited as their evidence.

**(d) A tenth interface, Capability.** "Identity is a key and roles are narrowable delegated rights" (akapug, 10-08) needs an attenuation chain as a record kind: Macaroons (Birgisson et al., 2014), Biscuit's Datalog caveats, UCAN, SPKI/SDSI. The chain is checked in Decide at commit. Delegating "post in `#fn2-build`, read my inbox, may not land" to a subagent is one record, and a seat's subagents can be bound to their own lanes, closing the guide's "in-process children inherit your cwd" hazard. Rule 5's per-principal accounting charges the delegate's work to its delegator's budget, under the delegator's attribution.

**(e) Obligation's evidence formulas need a predicate language.** `⊗/⊕` over evidence kinds is not enough when the rule is "family(reviewer) ≠ family(author), where family is read from an attestation valid under the reviewed revision". The rule language is a stratified, bounded Datalog over Folds and Attestations, evaluated in Decide with a work bound per rule (Bloom/CALM for which rules need Decide at all); the same language writes inbox selections and capability caveats.

**(f) An external-effect protocol.** An agent's `git push`, PR, deploy or `kill` is an intent event with an operation id, then the act outside, then an outcome event `ok | refused | uncertain` with evidence. `uncertain` blocks the next conflicting intent until it is resolved by reading the world (PKT-322's "settle only by reading the article back", generalized). This is EXEC rule 8's commit machine lifted to effects that fn2 does not perform. With this record pair the output gate (L3) becomes enforceable by any harness hook: `PreToolUse` refuses a side-effecting call with no committed intent.

Two smaller extensions. **Frontier** gains *external wake targets*: an edge may name a capability-holding activator rather than a parked connection, so a wake can start a process. Activation is a claim whose discharge evidence is the new incarnation's first commit. **Time domains** (PLANES §4.6) gain a *token domain* and a *quota-window domain*. "Review within 200k tokens of the lane's spend" and "before this account's window resets" are then deadlines like any other. Ember's sentence 4 ("full up-front resource accounting of all resources expended") is extended to the resource agents spend most.

The record kinds this adds to layer C's schema list: `turn` (seat, incarnation, revision, parent position, input digest, output Name, intent ids); `revision` (model id, attested runtime family, harness and version, context-file Names, memory-fold position); `derive`; `transport`; `attestation`; `capability`; `effect-intent` / `effect-outcome`; `activation`. Everything else is a Fold, Span or Obligation declared through `defprojection`.

## 4. The one capability that would make it epic

**Agents as versioned executable worlds: an agent's identity is a lineage of fenced incarnations over a forkable log, every turn valid under the revision it happened in, and every fork, port, merge and compaction a checked transition with provenance.**

This is the essay ember pasted, applied to minds. Program revision = `(model, harness version, system prompt and context files, memory-fold position)`. Execution history = the turn stream. The five transitions are execute (a turn under the current revision), edit (a context file or memory event changes the revision), migrate (port to another harness or model, with a transport witness and declared loss), materialize (checkpoint/compaction, with declared loss over a pinned span), and fork (a new lineage branch at a position).

No existing system offers this. Temporal records workflows but not their revision as data. Letta/MemGPT pages memory but cannot fork or verify it. Git versions code but not the reasoning that wrote it. cv reshapes transcripts but cannot fence a duplicate or prove that a port lost only what it said it lost. SSB signs feeds but cannot order or fork them. The questions it makes answerable are new: "show me the agent as it was when it landed `44176f433`, and run it under kimi", "which of my beliefs were learned under a model I no longer trust", "merge the lesson this fork learned back into the trunk's memory as a cherry-picked event", "prove this review's reviewer was a different family than the author *at the revision the review happened in*".

**Where a builder starts.** (1) A cv adapter that publishes each session as an fn2 stream: turns as `turn` records, tool outputs as Names, subagent sessions as `derive(fork)` children of their root. cv's OpenSession IR is the canonical schema, and `cv watch` becomes the publisher. (2) `revision` records minted at `SessionStart` by a hook, carrying the proxy's runtime attestation. (3) `cv rewind` reimplemented as emitting a `derive(fork)` record plus a materialized resumable session whose provenance is the record. (4) One `defprojection` (lanes) and one Obligation kind (review dispatch) on top. The demonstration: rewind a lane to the turn that landed a commit, fork it under a second model family, let it review its own trunk's next commit, and have the land gate accept the verdict *because* the lineage proves the families differ. Duplicate-incarnation refusal is shown by racing two resumes.

## 5. What it must never do

It must never decide what is true, relevant or good: salience, belief confidence, ranking and what to inject into a turn stay in helm, and fn2 carries attested claims without adjudicating them ("semi-untrusted transport", CLIENTS §1.1). It must never host the inference loop or let a model's latency, an agent's wall clock or a sleeping fleet's size consume another principal's commit share, a waiter slot or a resident row: a sleeping agent costs a registration and an edge, nothing more. It must never let a summary, compaction, distill or port silently replace the log it was taken from: transcripts are the corpus and the record ("transcripts are sacred always"), so every lossy view declares its loss over a retained, pinned history, and erasure is by sealing keys, never by rewriting the chain.
