# The widest fn2, from composition across nodes (2026-10-28; one voice in the widest-role council)

Vantage: composition is the primary act. A node is an open system with an interface; a fleet or federation is a wiring diagram of nodes; projections and obligations cross node boundaries; a world moves between nodes and between versions by transport along an equivalence that is itself a recorded fact. Read beside UNIVERSAL §9, PLANES §2 with its addendum, and the "versioned executable world" essay.

## 1. The world from this vantage

Every system ember has named (helm's fleet, Mini, breadstuffs, Wild Earth, a Robigalia node, a PDS someday) is already a composite: several nodes, several programs, several versions, glued by protocols nobody wrote down. The glue is where they break: Third Room died at the seam between Matrix and WebRTC (two identities, two orders, no shared position); the transactional outbox, Debezium, Kafka Connect, sagas and "exactly-once" connectors exist because Postgres, Kafka and the application cannot see one another's history; Kubernetes controllers fight over leases whose fencing token never reaches the actuator. The widest fn2 is **the medium in which systems are composed**: the place where the interface of an open system is declared, the wiring between systems is a durable versioned fact, the obligations one system owes another are linear resources with receipts, and a running world can be moved to another node or another revision along an equivalence that everyone downstream can check.

The mathematics is already chosen for us. In Spivak and Niu's *Polynomial Functors: A Mathematical Theory of Interaction*, an interface is a polynomial `p = Σ_{i ∈ I} y^{A_i}`: positions `I` (what the system exposes: its protocol state and its installed frontier) and, at each position, directions `A_i` (the commands it will accept there, each with its Decide, retry identity and refusal words). That is UNIVERSAL §6.2's shared protocol table, read as a mode-dependent interface: `claim` is a direction only at positions where the entry is unclaimed; `ack(cut)` only where the cut is below the frontier. A wiring diagram is a morphism `p_1 ⊗ … ⊗ p_n → q` in Poly (Spivak, *The operad of wiring diagrams*; Vagner, Spivak and Lerman, *Algebras of open dynamical systems on the operad of wiring diagrams*). An open node is a structured cospan `L(a) → X ← L(b)` (Baez and Courser, *Structured cospans*; Fong, *Decorated cospans*): its inner domains `X`, and two legs naming the streams, frontiers and obligations it shares on each boundary; two nodes compose by pushout along the shared boundary, which is exactly "identify the stream they both carry". Myers' *Categorical Systems Theory* gives the double category: objects are boundaries, horizontal cells open nodes and machines, vertical cells interface maps (renamings, migrations, version changes), squares the statement that a migrated node still plugs into the migrated diagram. Revisions are paths: Angiuli, Morehouse, Licata and Harper's *Homotopical Patch Theory* models a repository as a higher inductive type whose points are states and whose paths are patches, with 2-paths saying that independent patches commute; Mimram and Di Giusto's *A Categorical Theory of Patches* (the basis of Pijul) gets merges as pushouts. UNIVERSAL §1.2 already has the 2-paths: Mazurkiewicz independence is the homotopy between two interleavings of commuting events. And transport along equivalences (Univalent Foundations; the Cubical Agda `transport`) is what lets every structure defined on one version (projections, cursors, obligations, proofs) be carried to an equivalent one without rebuilding it by hand.

**A Tuesday.** At 09:00 a helm operator adds a fourth box. She does not configure peering; she appends a `wire` event to the fleet's topology domain: node `d` exposes port `rooms/v3`, connected to `a`, `b`, `c` along the shared streams of rooms `#lanes` and `#review`. Every node folds the topology domain, sees the new pushout, and starts carrying those streams. At 11:00 a CI runner (an ordinary process) claims job 4412 from fn2's queue with incarnation 7, builds in a Nix sandbox, and appends the result carrying a `Name` for each artifact and its incarnation; the dispatch obligation "reply owed for 4412" is discharged by that result's receipt, which helm on another node verifies with an inclusion proof against a signed checkpoint, not by trusting the runner. At 14:00 the room schema gains a `thread` field. No migration script: the lane appends an `equiv` record between `rooms/v3` and `rooms/v4` (a Δ/Σ migration in Spivak's *Functorial Data Migration*, with its round-trip law checked by the simulator on the latest checkpoint), and the room domain is **transported**: its observations before 14:00 stay valid under v3 and are readable through v4 by the forward map; every seat's cursor and every outstanding owed reply is carried across. At 16:00 the box hosting Wild Earth's tile 77 is overloaded; the tile's fast executor checkpoints, its zone lease is fenced at incarnation 12, and the world moves to box `b` at incarnation 13 by the same `transport` transition, with the position mapping recorded as an event (PLANES law L5). At 18:00 a scientist forks the Wild Earth catalog at position 1,904,221 under revision r52, runs another species model, and merges back by pushout, conflicts recorded in a Binding.

**On the wire**: the existing consumer protocol and transactions, plus four record kinds that cross node boundaries: ports (interface declarations, content-named), wires (diagram edits), receipts (a signed checkpoint claim plus a Merkle inclusion proof that an event sits at a position in a named domain), and transports (a world's move along a named equivalence). **Durable**: every domain's journal; the topology domain; every port, equivalence and transport. **Live**: presence, which side of a wire is reachable, a transport in progress. **Fast**: nothing here; fast executors are open systems wired to fn2, and their move is a transport like any other. **Across boundaries**: positions only as cuts with their domain named; facts only with receipts; obligations whole; worlds only along equivalences.

## 2. Ten things it would build or subsume

1. **Federation and peering as declared wiring** (subsumes NNTP feed configuration, Matrix federation, Kafka MirrorMaker/Cluster Linking, Postgres logical replication slots). The topology is a domain whose events are wiring-diagram edits, so "which node carries what" is history with a frontier rather than config files that drift; operators switch because the diagram is checkable (every boundary stream has a declared owner domain) and rewiring is an append, not a restart.
2. **Change-data capture without Debezium** (composes with Postgres logical decoding, SQLite's session extension changesets, MySQL binlog). A database presents a port whose positions are its LSN and whose directions are idempotent commands; fn2 records its changes with receipts and replaces the transactional-outbox pattern with an Obligation discharged by the database's commit evidence, so users drop the outbox table and the connector cluster.
3. **Durable execution** (subsumes Temporal, Cadence, Restate, Azure Durable Functions). A workflow is an open machine whose journal is fn2's Log and whose activities are Obligations with retry identity; Temporal's versioning API (`GetVersion` patches), the part its users fight most, becomes the revision dimension and transport.
4. **CI and build provenance** (composes with Buildkite, GitHub Actions runners, Bazel remote execution, Nix; subsumes in-toto/SLSA attestation stores). Jobs are claims with incarnations, artifacts are Names, results are receipts, and the provenance chain is a Span from source revision to artifact that any node verifies; users switch because a zombie runner's late result is refused `fenced` and the attestation is the history itself rather than a sidecar document.
5. **Control planes** (subsumes etcd as used by Kubernetes, Consul, ZooKeeper leader election). Desired state is a domain, controllers are open machines wired to it, and a lease's fencing incarnation travels in every command to the actuator's port (Kleppmann's fencing token, enforced end to end); reconcile loops become replayable and split-brain a named refusal.
6. **Schema and code migration** (subsumes Flyway/Liquibase, Rails migrations, Erlang/OTP `code_change`, Unison's content-addressed upgrades). A migration is an `equiv` record with forward and backward maps and laws, and transport carries every projection, cursor and obligation; users switch because old observations remain readable under their revision and a migration that loses information must say so as a declared loss rather than discover it in production.
7. **Revision journals: version control of running worlds** (composes with Git and Pijul; subsumes Datomic's as-of, Dolt, lakeFS branches). The world is two-dimensional (revision × position); fork, patch, commute and merge are paths and 2-paths per *Homotopical Patch Theory*, and merge is a pushout per Mimram–Di Giusto; Dolt and lakeFS users switch because a branch carries its executions and obligations, not only its rows.
8. **Scientific pipelines and lineage** (composes with Snakemake, Nextflow, DVC, MLflow; subsumes OpenLineage/Marquez). Each step is an open machine with a port whose inputs and outputs are Names; the pipeline is a wiring diagram, and a rerun under a new code revision is a transport whose equivalence says which outputs are unchanged; scientists switch because "which result came from which code and data" is an inclusion proof, not a lab notebook entry.
9. **World handoff for games and shared spaces** (subsumes SpatialOS-style zone migration, Croquet/TeaTime's reflectors, the Third Room seam). A zone's fast executor moves between nodes by transport under a fenced lease, with its subscribers' cuts mapped; a world's content and rules upgrade by equivalence while players are inside; studios switch because zone handoff stops being bespoke netcode.
10. **Orthogonal persistence for processes** (composes with CRIU, rr, seL4/Robigalia, in the line of KeyKOS and EROS). A process is an open system whose port is its syscall and capability boundary; fn2 stores its checkpoints as Names and its nondeterministic inputs as a Log (rr's recording made durable), so a process can be restored, replayed, audited or moved to another node; Robigalia gets ember's "foundational orthogonal persistence" as wiring, not a kernel feature.

Across all ten, what makes a system **worth composing with** is concrete: the port grade it can present. **G0 opaque**: no events, no identity (a Jupyter kernel with hidden state); fn2 can only wrap it behind an output-gated proxy. **G1 observed**: it emits an ordered change stream (Postgres logical decoding, SQLite changesets, eBPF syscall traces). **G2 commanded**: its commands accept an operation identity and return a durable outcome (Stripe idempotency keys, Postgres `INSERT … ON CONFLICT`). **G3 fenced**: it rejects a superseded incarnation (a fencing token checked at the actuator). **G4 replayable**: its state is a function of a checkpoint plus recorded inputs (lockstep games, Nix derivations, rr, deterministic simulation). **G5 transportable**: its schema has declared equivalences to its other versions (Unison, Cambria's lenses at Ink & Switch). In return fn2 gives history and receipts from G1, exactly-once outcomes from G2, fenced exclusive authority from G3, audit, fork and migration from G4, live cross-node upgrade from G5. Worth composing with means G2 or above, or one adapter away.

## 3. What the kernel must contain

The pruned eight describe one node's algebra well. Composition across nodes needs three additions that are irreducible at the composition level, and changes to five of the eight.

**New: Port.** A content-named declaration of a polynomial interface: positions (protocol state × frontier type), and at each position the legal directions, each with its Decide signature, retry identity, admission, outcome evidence, refusal words and grade (G0–G5). It is UNIVERSAL §10.1's protocol table published as data, so the parser, the checker, a remote client and a wiring validator read one object. DSL form: `defport` generated from the command grammar, never hand-written beside it. Law: a node implements a port iff its machine is a coalgebra for that polynomial (a per-transition simulation, the same local proof shape as UNIVERSAL §2.2).

**New: Wire.** The composition record: a structured cospan naming inner domains and the boundary legs, appended to a topology domain. Composing two open nodes is a pushout along shared boundary streams; the kernel checks that each identified stream has exactly one owner domain (the one Decide that may append to it), that port types agree on both legs, and that every obligation crossing the wire names the domain whose receipt discharges it. Law: wiring is associative and unital up to the recorded isomorphism (the operad laws), so a fleet can be composed in any bracketing and checked piecewise.

**New: Equiv and Transport.** An `equiv` record names two schemas or revisions, a forward and a backward map (Δ/Σ migrations over `defcolumns` C-sets, or lenses with lifting laws per UNIVERSAL §7), the observations it preserves, and its declared loss. A `transport` is a Decide transition that moves a domain from (node, revision, incarnation) to (node′, revision′, incarnation + 1) along an `equiv`, recording the position mapping, translating every outstanding Obligation, mapping every registered cut, and marking each projection as transported or to-rebuild. Law (the square in Myers' double category): for every declared observation `o` and every position `p` of the old world, `o′(map(p)) = forward(o(p))`, checked by the simulator on the transported checkpoint and its suffix. Equivalences compose, and the composite of recorded transports is itself a path, so a world's lineage is a path in the version groupoid.

**What the eight must become.**
- **Log** gains a second index: the world is `Revision × Position`, with domain identity independent of the hosting node, so a domain can move. A position outside its domain is always written `(domain, revision, position)`; L5 extends to revisions.
- **Name** gains its positional sibling, the **Receipt**: `(domain, checkpoint claim, inclusion proof of event e at position p)`. A content name says "these bytes"; a receipt says "this happened there, then". Every cross-node fact is a receipt; STORAGE's signed Merkle checkpoint claim already supplies the root.
- **Decide** accepts receipts as preconditions ("commit this only if domain D shows event e at or below p"). This is how composition replaces two-phase commit: one atomic mutation stays one domain (UNIVERSAL §1.1), and cross-domain agreement is a chain of Decides linked by receipts and escrow Obligations (sagas, made checkable).
- **Obligation** becomes cross-node: created in one domain, discharged by a receipt from another, translated by transport. The owed reply from helm to a seat on another box, BP custody, and a CI dispatch are one kind.
- **Frontier** of a wired system is the limit of its parts: a down-closed cut across the diagram's domains. A wait on a composite frontier is a threshold read on each leg.
- **Binding** also binds domain identities to their current host and incarnation, and port names to port contents, merged by union with conflicts recorded: two nodes both claiming to host a world is an equivocation fact, not a race.
- **Span** across nodes is what a wire's legs are; the inbox composite generalizes to a cross-node inbox along the diagram.
- **Fold** is functorial in its schema: a fold declared on `rooms/v3` is transported to `rooms/v4` along the equivalence (`Σ_F` of the fold's state), or declared to rebuild.

So the kernel is eleven named interfaces over six irreducible ones (Log, Decide, Fold, Name, Port, Equiv), with Wire, Receipt and Transport as their named compositions.

## 4. The one capability that would make it epic: live transport of a world along a recorded equivalence

No system moves a running world to another machine *and* another version in one checked step while keeping every promise it made. Erlang hot upgrade checks nothing; CRIU cannot cross a schema change; Postgres logical replication loses consumers' positions and owes nobody anything; Unison leaves live state to the user; Kubernetes reschedules a pod by killing it. fn2 already has the pieces: one Decide per domain, fenced leases, signed checkpoints, cuts, obligations, declared folds.

**`transport(domain, to: (node′, revision′), along: equiv)`**, as a builder would start it:
1. **Declare.** Append the `equiv` record (forward/backward Δ/Σ maps over the domain's C-set schema, preserved observations, declared loss). The simulator checks the square on the current checkpoint and a bounded suffix; a failure is a named refusal before anything moves.
2. **Fence.** The source's Decide appends `transport-begin` at position `p₀`, which supersedes the domain lease at incarnation `k`; later commands to the old incarnation are refused `fenced` with a forwarding hint (the domain's new Binding).
3. **Carry.** The destination takes the checkpoint Name and the receipt for `p₀`, applies the forward map, and installs at position `p₀′` under incarnation `k + 1`, recording the position map `p₀ ↦ p₀′` as an event (law L5).
4. **Translate.** Every outstanding Obligation is re-created on the destination with its evidence formula pushed through the forward map; every registered cut that included the domain is mapped; each projection is either transported (`Σ_F` of its state, frontier mapped) or marked to rebuild through `catch-up`.
5. **Commit.** The destination appends `transport-complete` with a receipt for `transport-begin`; the source's Binding moves the domain to the new host by union, and an obligation "source retains history through p₀ until the destination's checkpoint is witnessed by an independent node" gates reclamation (`A-INDEPENDENT-FRONTIER`).
6. **Keep the past.** Every observation made before `p₀` stays valid under revision r and is readable under revision′ through the forward map; a reader can ask for either.

The first instance to build is a helm room moved from node `a` (schema v3) to node `d` (v4, adding `thread`) with three seats parked on waits and two owed replies outstanding; the gate is a new simulator checker, **TRANSPORT** (for every declared observation, old reads equal translated new reads; no obligation lost or duplicated; no cut regressed; the old incarnation fenced at every interleaving), run under death cuts at every step above. The same transition then moves a Wild Earth tile, upgrades a workflow mid-flight, migrates a Robigalia process: the world moves, and nothing it promised is lost.

## 5. What it must never do

fn2 must never offer atomic commit across domains or nodes as a composition primitive: composition is pushouts, receipts and obligations, and a hidden two-phase commit would reintroduce the coordinator whose failure every federated system in §2 has paid for. It must never move, rename or reinterpret a world without a recorded equivalence and a fenced transport, because an unrecorded migration is the one change that silently invalidates every downstream receipt, cursor and proof. It must never treat a wired system's word as fn2's fact beyond the grade its port declares: what another node or an external database says enters fn2 only as a receipt checked against a signed checkpoint, or as a claim labelled with who made it.
