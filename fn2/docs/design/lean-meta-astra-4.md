1. **The stages need stronger statements; their proposed teeth are uneven.**

- **PW1:** state `∀ x ∈ Supported_F, encode(F_ACL2 x) = F_Lean(encode x)`, including closure of intermediate values and faithful dependencies. Satisfying an equation emitted by the same reader does not validate that reader; fuel stability alone also survives a consistently mistranslated body. Moreover, the export has **125 fuelled definitions**, not four: `MEMBER-EQ-EXEC` and recursive guard recognizers are examples ([LayerAWorld:718](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/lanes/fn2/lean/fn2/meta/Fn2/Instances/LayerAWorld.lean:718), [LayerAWorld:1466](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/lanes/fn2/lean/fn2/meta/Fn2/Instances/LayerAWorld.lean:1466)). Four adequacy lemmas already appear in [Fuel:20](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/lanes/fn2/lean/fn2/meta/Fn2/ACL2/Fuel.lean:20). Recorded discrepancies can falsify faithfulness at those inputs; agreement cannot establish it.
- **PW2:** the defining-equation statements are useful, conditional on faithful dependencies and well-founded uniqueness. `MOD` still depends on trusted `FLOOR`. **`BINARY-LOGAND` is an additional trusted native**, beyond the four named in the draft ([Faithful:14](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/lanes/fn2/lean/fn2/meta/Fn2/ACL2/Faithful.lean:14)). State native correspondence on an explicit representable domain; `EXPT 2 (-1)` cannot satisfy unrestricted correspondence ([Val:199](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/lanes/fn2/lean/fn2/meta/Fn2/ACL2/Val.lean:199)). “Fails to prove” is not a tooth: exhibit inputs satisfying the retained hypotheses and unequal results.
- **PW3:** quantify over each fixed, successfully generated declaration and **all guarded arguments**, with signature reconstruction and output normalization explicit: `∀ args, Guard args → ∃ N, ∀ fuel ≥ N, project <$> runArm … fuel = .ok (normalize(F args))`. Include preservation of children omitted from the narrowed outputs. The current measure supplies missing children as `nil` and projects them away ([Measure:108](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/lanes/fn2/lean/fn2/meta/Fn2/Measure.lean:108)). The mutation is a useful counterexample to the **mutated instance**, provided its guard holds. Changing the dispatch to `match` is insufficient: `Term.eval` is `partial`, hence opaque to kernel reduction ([Val:319](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/lanes/fn2/lean/fn2/meta/Fn2/ACL2/Val.lean:319); [Lean’s semantics](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Definitions/Recursive-Definitions/)).
- **PW4:** require `Inv s ∧ InputOK i → Inv s' ∧ ResultRelated(F_typed(s,i), F_ACL2(α s, encode i))`, covering **results, charges, stops and every child**, plus initialization and representation coverage for the supported profile. The draft’s state-only equation omits these ([draft:18](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/coordinator/fn2/lean-meta-pathway-draft.md:18)). A u60→u32 mutation need not break its square: it can merely remove large states from the typed domain. Keep the reference fixed and exhibit a legal write of, say, `2^40`; test either preservation or representability.
- **PW5a:** honestly a trusted execution route, not a correctness theorem. Name compiler, runtime and replacement implementations.
- **PW5b:** `⊑` needs defined observations, memory/ABI relation, legal initial states, and **absence of undefined behavior plus termination of each bounded operation**. Trace inclusion alone can admit a diverging implementation. A failed lowering proof is not itself a counterexample; demonstrate a changed result/effect or newly possible UB under valid premises. Per-operation proofs also need composition through initialization, dispatch and the served entry point before licensing [draft:22](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/coordinator/fn2/lean-meta-pathway-draft.md:22).

2. **“Higher assurance than ATC” is currently an ambition, not an established comparison.**

ATC already provides per-instance correspondence between ACL2 functions and generated C under its formal C semantics, including execution-limit and applicable memory/nonaliasing hypotheses. This path currently has neither that C theorem nor its memory boundary. ([ATC](https://acl2.org/doc/index-seo.php?xkey=C____ATC), [generated events](https://acl2.org/doc/index-seo.php?xkey=C____ATC-TUTORIAL-EVENTS))

“Reach” is broader applicability. “Two kernels” presently means **different obligations checked in different systems**, not independent verification of the same end-to-end claim. PW3 could add a substantive guarantee ATC alone lacks: generated arms implement declaration semantics. But its expressions already reuse the generator’s rewrite ([print-world:104](/Users/ember/dev/fn/build/lanes/fn2/lean/fn2/meta/emit/print-world.lisp:104)).

Strictly stronger needs a matched baseline: preserve ATC’s executable correspondence and safety scope, add declaration-to-executable correctness, and discharge or explicitly retain the additional bridge assumptions. Merely adding Lean adds another trusted boundary.
