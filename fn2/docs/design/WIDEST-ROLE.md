# The widest role of fn2: the council's synthesis (the coordinator, 2026-10-28; present state 2026-10-31)

Twelve independent drafts in `fn2/widest/`: versioned executable world, OS persistence substrate, agent-fleet substrate, federation of worlds, composition across nodes, the sovereign node, the substrate under other systems, the economy of obligations, the transclusive commons, the physical world and off-planet, and two free-vantage drafts from Astra and Sol that independently chose the same thing: fn2 as the shared instrument of collective inquiry. Plus the reading of minidregg and nanodregg (`REALMS-FROM-DREGG.md`). Ember's correction that started it: "you've contracted into describing the smallest version of what fn2 would be"; "a signal messenger substrate ... that's not a very expansive view". No draft was pruned; this is the synthesis, with the kernel re-derived from what the widest fn2 needs.

## 1. The widest fn2, in one page
fn2 is **the thing a world is kept in**: a world being any history that has promises, identity and meaning, whether it belongs to a person, a community, a laboratory, a robot, a process, a market, a document, a game, or an agent. A world lives on a node; it is durable, witnessed so no host can rewrite it, metered so no client can exhaust it, and portable so no host can keep it. Its history is the authority, its state is folds over that history, its meaning is content-named programs that travel with the data, and its promises are obligations that are discharged by evidence or carried to whoever inherits them. Worlds compose: a node is an open system with declared ports, a fleet or a federation is a wiring of worlds, and a world moves between nodes, bodies and versions by transport along a recorded equivalence, carrying every outstanding promise and keeping every past observation valid under the revision it was made in.

What this makes possible, from the twelve, is one family of capabilities that no existing system has, because each needs all of the above at once:
- **Fork and counterfactual replay**: fork a world at a cut, re-execute its recorded commands under a candidate revision with every external effect held, and get a signed behavioural diff as the evidence for promotion (versioned world; the agent-fleet's "run this agent as it was, under another model"; the substrate's "a git branch of Postgres, search and S3 at one instant").
- **Transport of a living world**: move a world to another node, body or version in one checked step, fenced, with obligations and cursors carried and old observations still valid (federation; composition; the OS substrate's forkable machine with linear obligations; the physical world's "the rover is gone, its world is not"; the sovereign node's lineage that no host can keep).
- **Reconsider and consequence propagation**: when a fact, calibration, statute or dataset changes, find everything built on it, wake every dependent with a typed delta, and return an account of what changed, what stands, and who accepted responsibility for the rest (commons; Astra; Sol).
- **Settlement without a shared chain**: holds, witnessed foreign evidence, typed uncertainty, and equivocation turned into portable debt (economy).
- **A machine, a person, an agent or a robot whose whole world survives the destruction of its host** (OS substrate; sovereign; physical; agent).

These are not five features. They are one engine seen from five sides: a cut, a fork by reference, a revision with a migration, a witness with a receipt, an obligation with a disposition, and a transport. Everything the pruned algebra said about one node at one revision stays true; it was describing the fragment of this that 5.1.1 serves.

## 2. The kernel, re-derived from the widest role
The pruned eight (Log, Decide, Fold, Name; Binding, Span, Frontier, Obligation) were chosen by attacking for vacuity. The twelve were asked instead what the kernel must contain for their world to be a composition rather than a bolt-on, and they converged. Counting what four or more vantages independently demanded:

**Irreducibles, now nine.**
- **Log → World**: a forest of commit domains with lineage; domains cheap enough to number in the millions (a row, never a file); fork by reference (a child's prefix is the parent's, shared); each event stamped with the revision it was decided under, its occurrence identity (distinct from its content identity: two identical readings are two observations), its author key, its payer principal and the grant it acted under; items graded durable or transient; declared independence per event kind so trace equivalence is computable across nodes; foreign parents carried as a Span; exportable as **Segments** (self-verifying runs with predecessor links) that attach or wait as `pending-predecessor`.
- **Decide**: a content-named, bounded, total program installed in a domain by an event and amendable only by its own rule (so an old contract is judged by its old rules); typed by **occasion** (birth, adopt, write, relaw, invoke, import, fork, promote, transport); preconditions in order: grant chain valid at head, meter reservation available, receipts named, then the domain predicate; every outcome records the Decide's Name; one atomic mutation per domain stays, and cross-domain agreement is a chain of Decides linked by receipts and escrow, with one controlled sequenced multi-domain form for the remainder.
- **Fold**: indexed by revision, with migrations between members; **declared loss** as state that travels with every read (a covariance is state); conservation laws certified per event kind; a derivation contract (inputs, method, environment, output, checker, dependency extraction) so a fold's result is a portable, re-runnable fact; relations as bounded keyed places with limits, caps, retraction policy and a guard, never values.
- **Name**: algorithm-tagged (foreign hashes as validated aliases); structured (`defshape`: names whose children are names); delta names sharing structure with a parent, so a checkpoint costs its dirty pages; dependency closure (a node may not fold an event whose code it cannot fetch); range challenges so custody is checkable. Its positional sibling is the **Receipt**: "this event sits at this position in this domain under this checkpoint".
- **Revision** (new): a content-named closed bundle of schemas, Decide programs, fold and machine definitions, protocol tables and projections; a fifth position type, never mixed with the others.
- **Equiv / Transport** (new): a migration `r1 → r2` with a transfer per changed fold, a relation, the observations it preserves and which it changes on purpose, and evidence (replay certificate, per-event-kind homomorphism, functorial schema map, or a named waiver); migrations compose and so does their evidence; when the relation is an equivalence on the declared observations, everything stated over them transports; a transport is the Decide that moves a domain to (node', revision', incarnation + 1) along one, recording the position map.
- **Port** (new): an interface as data (`defport`), generated from the command grammar and read by parser, machine, client, checker and wiring validator alike; a translation to a foreign protocol carries a loss signature in a lattice of losses, so composing ports computes the loss of a bridge; files, directories, mailboxes, tables, NNTP, 9P, RESP, PG wire are ports of one grammar machinery.
- **Meter** (new, inside the algebra, not beside it): a tree of principals; every resource a dimension (bytes, seconds, visits, joules, link budget per contact, tokens, custody held); reserve before work; conservation; no laundering; protected shares as a theorem; liability when one principal vouches for another; admission without CAPTCHAs composed from it.
- **Grant** (new): a capability with issuer, holder, scope as a predicate over Spans, caveats that only narrow, a derivation edge to its parent, an incarnation, an expiry in a named time domain and a revocation handle that is a threshold read; a Decide's first precondition; visibility is the pullback along the reader's grant, never a filter afterwards; confinement checkable from the derivation Span.

**Named compositions, now sixteen.** Binding (unchanged, and it is the one equivocation detector for every signed chain, double-spend included); Span (plus **Anchor with transport**: a position region inside a version with a functorial law of motion along edits, the literal Xanadu span; plus a transclusion's opening, mode, disclosure scope and capability ceiling from minidregg; plus derivation hyperedges and negative dependencies, "searched this collection and found nothing"); Frontier (plus the **Cut**, a durable named consistent vector of positions across domains, the unit of restore, fork, migrate, backup and read-your-writes across a whole stack; plus witnessed frontiers co-signed by k of a declared set; plus the predicted frontier, **Contact**, for store-and-forward; plus interval time with two firing senses); Obligation (two parties separately transferable; quantity in a unit with split and merge; succession; k-of-n and foreign evidence; continuation into the next obligation; effect classes with every external effect held inside a fork; holder transfer by receipt; deadline-triggered activation; inherited obligations that travel along references; a disposition at every fork so exactly one side holds each promise); **Claim / Attestation** (signed, supersedable, bitemporal with valid time separate from commit position, read through a named trust-policy fold; disagreement is a Binding conflict, never an overwrite); **Receipt**; **Wire** (a structured cospan of worlds along shared boundary streams, each with one owner); **Segment**; **Incarnation** (World versus Body: a lineage continues on a new body under a recovery quorum, the old body fenced, its late writes recorded as equivocation and graftable as observations, never decisions); **Continuation** (a suspended machine as a durable value with an upgrade policy); **Derivation**; **Declared loss**; **Witness** (the co-sign exchange as a served protocol, with recovery states `rolled-back (witnessed)` and `repairing (from peers)`); **Lineage** (key events with pre-rotation, succession, escrow as a dead-man obligation, move); **Image** (a process checkpoint with a chosen nondeterminism mode).

The reader from Kafka, Postgres, Matrix or Redis still sees ten operations; they gain `fork`, `transport`, `reconsider`, `witness` and `grant`, and every one of the ten gains a revision and a cut.

**How the kernel is held now.** Ember, reading this section: "I notice this is starting to sound like (modal) logics and theories. Which is good. because that's the right direction." And on composition: "we do need to be careful that we are composing these things correctly and not just suffering a proliferation of conceptual linkages." MODAL-KERNEL.md therefore holds the nine and the sixteen as six judgment families over the base theory, with merges that keep them small: Revision, Equiv, Port and Wire are one mapping mechanism and Transport a fenced Decide over it; Witness, Receipt, Claim, Grant and lease are one evidence infrastructure; Incarnation is position uniqueness plus a lease; Meter shares the obligations' accounting. Read the lists above as what those families must be able to say, not as sixteen constructions to build.

## 3. What this means for the build now
Layers A and B are unaffected in substance: the byte pool, the host primitives, the simulator, the journal, the commit machine and the formats are the floor all of this stands on. What the widest role asks of them now is the "mechanisms now, policies later" rule applied once more, as **envelope fields and record kinds**, not subsystems: the event envelope carries a revision stamp, an occurrence identity, an author key, a payer principal and a grant reference (B2's layouts); domains are rows with lineage and a `fork-of` genesis (B4's allocator and C's schemas); segments are the export unit (B's format, E's bundle); the checkpoint claim's origin is `(lineage, domain, incarnation)` (B5); claims, derivations, declared-loss, anchors and grants are schema entries (C); ports are what D's grammar DSL emits and witness and transport are consumer-protocol verb pairs (E). The first slice still serves NNTP; the kernel it serves from is this one.

**Where the tree is against §3 (origin/dev 747afb64c).** Built: Meter's first form (the principal ledger with reserve-before-work and run charges, layer D's D7); Port's seed (the NNTP grammar table as an interface-as-data, layer D's D2). Owed, and ruled to go in now while a field is cheap: the origin fields (lineage, commit domain, incarnation, position) and the revision stamp in the event envelope (IDENTITY-ACROSS-PEERS ruling 2), with an occurrence identity (REGROUND §4 item 3); every layout frozen without them is a rewrite later. Owed pending decision 2: the author key, payer principal and grant reference fields; domains as rows with lineage and a `fork-of` genesis (REGROUND §4 item 10, the transport demonstration's first rung); segments; claims, derivations, declared loss, anchors and grants as schema entries; `defport`; Grant (REGROUND §4 item 6).

## 4. The one demonstration that proves the widest role
**Transport of a living world.** A helm room on node A at schema v3, with seats parked on waits and owed replies outstanding, moves to node B at schema v4 along a recorded equivalence: fenced, obligations and cursors carried, old reads equal to translated new reads, the old incarnation refused by name, under crashes at every step, with a witness that saw A's checkpoint accepting B's. It exercises the cut, fork by reference, revision and migration, witness and receipt, obligation disposition and transport at once; the same transition then moves a Wild Earth tile, reincarnates a robot, forks a machine, and runs an agent as it was under another model. It is the first thing after 5.1.1.
