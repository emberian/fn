## The claim to make precise
The kernel is a multi-modal logic over a frame of worlds `(domain, position, revision, incarnation, node)`; each widening the council made is a modality: durable/transient as a grading with declared loss as its dual; "valid under revision r" as truth at a Kripke world with migrations as accessibility and transport-along-equivalence as the isomorphism case; `at-least p` / `exactly-at p` / cuts as temporal modalities (since, until, as-of; interval time with two firing senses as Halpern-Shoham); `witnessed by k` as an epistemic modality nearer common knowledge than belief; obligations, discharge, waiver and inheritance as deontic modalities with evidence as the discharge; grants, delegation, attenuation and "speaks for" as authorization logic (Abadi-Burrows-Lampson-Plotkin's calculus of access control, Garg-Pfenning's constructive authorization logic, DKAL, NAL/Nexus); "held in a fork" as counterfactual; occasion-typed Decide as dynamic logic ([α]φ). And the other half: a world, a port and a protocol table are theories; composition of worlds is a theory morphism; the colimit of a wiring diagram is the composed theory (Goguen-Burstall institutions; Kestrel's Specware: specs, morphisms, colimits, refinement; the same house as APT and ATC, hence the lineage that reaches ACL2 directly); transport along an equivalence is an isomorphism of theories.
