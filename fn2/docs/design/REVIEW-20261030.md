# fn2 review, 2026-10-30 (a Fable reviewer at ember's request; origin/dev 9dd58fa89; verbatim below the rulings)

## Main's rulings on the five changes (2026-10-30)
1. Recovery and the sealer go to the head of every queue: B5 (with GC2 and B-S2) is B's only work; C1c (the sealer) is C's next after C-S3, ahead of C4b; nothing on the served path is durable until they exist, and the tree had inverted RELEASE-PLAN §5.5's order (B5 before C2).
2. sim-layer-d runs under A4's instrumentation (TRACE lockstep, the own-read hook, the q1 case hook) and is D's per-commit gate before D4 2/n lands: the DSL seat's DM3 2/n tally hook is exactly this and moves first.
3. fn2-commit's arms leave :unchecked and every assert$? in commit.lisp and alloc.lisp becomes an fn2-core-stop with a named reason, in B5 before B-S2: the executable and the logic must not disagree on the impossible branches.
4. The ROUNDTRIP/CANONICAL rows are reclassified :representation with :shared, and fn2-square-rowp refuses a :semantic row whose sides share a declaration (ember's decision 1 made enforceable): the DSL seat, with DM3.
5. A5r (the native adapter, the socket, the peer address) gets its own seat now; OWED.md stays tracked (it is the assurance ledger and a release artifact) and is regenerated only in a commit that changes rows; the design mirror refreshes once per milestone, not per batch.
Also ruled: C strengthens fn2-identity-binding-converges to compare accepted/evidence state, or states the served choice as its own row; runs-schema.lisp's :program fold compiler is a numbered departure in the layer doc with its remedy; machines.lisp's silent over-width drop (:1278) and the clamp (:1397) become refusals.

### 1. Correctness

**The durable path has no root, and nobody is seated on it.** No `recovery.lisp`, `checkpoint.lisp`, `cleaner.lisp` exist (b-durability.md §1 marks B5/B6 owed); C1c's sealer waits on B's GC2 member, which lives inside B5. So identity facts never leave resident overlays (c-ordered-runs §7 C-SUCCESSOR: "until C1c's sealer seals frozen overlays into runs, the overlays accumulate"), and served POST capacity is overlay rows × arenas. D5 2/n (one durable POST) needs B-S2, which needs GC2, which needs B5. B's continuation was last rewritten 10-29 09:54; every C and D quest since built over B5's absence. RELEASE-PLAN §5.5 puts B5 before C2 on the M1 path; the tree inverted it.

**Evidence stops at the echo.** The 53 checked rows are the A4 echo/scribe run. `fn2/tests/sim-layer-d.lsp:14-18` includes owed-conn, echo, check-wire and check-account only, and calls `fn2-turn` directly (`:139`): no TRACE lockstep, no own-read-hook, no q1-case-hook. The reference transition system has never been compared against the machine that will serve NNTP; fn2-conn's 23 rows and fn2-commit's 13 have zero check records; 5 of Q1's 73 catalogued cases are unbound. The run-time certificates do stop the core (good), but WIRE and ACCOUNT are the only independent checks on D.

**B's commit machine is outside the checked language.** Every arm of `fn2-commit` is `:language :unchecked` (`machines-b.lisp:158-166`): no frame rows, no stale-read or undeclared-write refusal, holds unchecked. `commit.lisp` uses `assert$?` 22 times (`alloc.lisp` 5): ACL2's `er hard?` aborts the process at run time while the logic returns the absorbed value (`:119` `(mv 0 0 0 0 0)`, `:154`, `:181`), so the executable and the proved model disagree on exactly the impossible branches, and nothing stops the core with a named reason. D5 will bind transaction members to this machine.

**A keystone weaker than its name.** `fn2-identity-binding-converges` (`identity.lisp:753-758`) compares key sets; the IDENTITY checker's `:binding` event (`check-identity.lisp:50-54, 89-91`) drops accepted/evidence state. Two nodes offered (m,a),(m,b) in opposite orders "converge" yet answer the same query `:duplicate` on one and `:evidence` on the other (POST 441 text differs). The layer doc calls the served choice "a separate statement"; no row states it. E4 peering meets this first.

**The ledger misdescribes itself.** 405 of 411 rows claim `:semantic`; the ~140 `*-ROUNDTRIP`/`*-CANONICAL` pairs are generated-codec-vs-list-codec, which DSL-METATHEORY decision 1 names representation refinement over a shared declaration. With `:shared nil` the table guard's independent-contract rule (`owed.lisp:49-55`) never fires, and 210 rows sit on the migration default `:assertion`.

**Smaller.** S1 as built admits a refused booking unbooked and 400s it (`machines-a-run.lisp:155-180`): the ledger hole closes but the pool still spends a row, RX and TX per refused flood connection; FAIR is absent and every machine runs on slot 0. `runs-schema.lisp:100-143` is `:mode :program` (the fold compiler), against the brief's and both layer docs' "no :program" claim. `machines.lisp:1278` still drops an over-width scalar write silently; `:1397` clamps the active count at zero.

### 2. The method

Five seats on one branch produced 16K net lines in a day, and the shape of the churn is the concern. The 11 `wip` commits (21:12-21:58 on 10-29) landed R6a1's range primitives and the D 4.5 admission partition straight on dev in 1-to-298-line slices, touching `machines.lisp` (+243), `core.lisp`, `machines-directory.lisp`; the worktree-plus-whole-tree-gate rule for exactly those files was written at 02:33 the next morning. Fifteen commits touched foundation files in the window. Nineteen of 76 commits are regeneration (11 OWED.md, 8 design mirror): a 3,032-line generated table and a ~20K-line mirror of the coordinator docs are committed artifacts, and the mirror's own ORIENTATION still says "branch fn2/main, at most two building seats". dev's tip is a merge "into fn2/c-found", local dev is three commits ahead of origin, and lean-meta, squares and sprint continuations hang off a `fn2/main` that no longer integrates anything.

Unowned: A5r (the native adapter is parked on `fn2/a5-native-wip`; `fn2/host` holds three files and no socket), B5/B6, the C1c sealer, FAIR, the ENVELOPE floor measurements, and the mirror's staleness. D9 says "a node serves plaintext NNTP on the laptop"; no seat owns the host that would do it.

### 3. The macros

They are pulling weight: `defcolumns` (every table), `defmachine` with contracts, certificates, frame rows and case catalogues, `defcost`, `deflayout` (~70 layouts, generated codecs), `defrunschema :fold` (one declaration, step compiled twice, keystones generated per fold), `defsquare`/DM1. Hand-written beside them: the whole commit machine (above); three per-byte loops on the article path (`commit.lisp:364-373`, `upload-feed.lisp` reading and writing each octet through checked exports, the identity bind copy pending A-S8) while `pool-ranges` has scan/compare/drop-prefix and no copy; `accounts.lisp:56-101` hand-rolls six dimension-major arrays and their facts because the DSL has no 2-D column; `conn-answer.lisp` is a 375-line decision table next to the grammar DSL. 0 proved is ember's ruling and is fine; 358 rows with neither proof nor check is the number to watch.

### 4. Stretch marks

The pool's one-state-per-slot costs two slots per connection and two 64 KiB chunk windows per upload; articles over two chunks need D4 3/n extents through storage jobs under an allocator that has no recovery. The 32-value `mv` ceiling was moved from rows to read sets (fn2-conn: 30 fields, widest read 17); upload and txn will press it again. B's `fn2-fields` is now shared scratch for B format stripes, C decision stripes and D offers (C-DECISION-STRIPES): the B4 sin across three layers. The identity decision visits every overlay and every live run linearly (`fn2-id-next-overlay`, `fn2-id-next-run`), filters skipping reads not rows: O(#runs) until C5, and no sealer to make runs. Two timer idioms coexist: the commit fence busy-polls `fn2-commit-now` while runnable (`commit.lisp:709-713`) against D7 5/n's coalesced ArmWake, not built. The 4 KiB quantum is both conn's cap and the thing BH1 measured as 262K yields/s at device speed. The next machines break: upload needs A-S7 (the admission partition generalised a third time), txn needs B-S2, cursor needs C1c's cold leg, multi-range Send (A has one), and a varying holding dimension the ledger has no row shape for.

### 6. Five changes now

1. Seat B (or hand B5 and C1c to C) and put recovery plus the sealer at the head of every queue; nothing on the served path is durable until they exist.
2. Run `sim-layer-d` under the A4 instrumentation (TRACE lockstep, own-read-hook, q1-case-hook) and make it D's per-commit gate before D4 2/n lands.
3. Port fn2-commit's arms out of `:unchecked` and replace `assert$?` with `fn2-core-stop` reasons before D5 binds members to it.
4. Reclassify the ROUNDTRIP/CANONICAL rows `:representation` with `:shared`, and make `fn2-square-rowp` refuse a `:semantic` row whose sides share a declaration.
5. Stop committing generated artifacts (OWED.md from the export into `build/`, the mirror once per milestone) and give A5r an owner this session.
