# Programmable transclusionary object realms: what minidregg and nanodregg explored, and what fn2 inherits (the coordinator, 2026-10-28; read from the trees, cited by path)

## What the two trees built (minidregg main; nanodregg main)
**Hyperdreggmedia (minidregg).** A hyperdocument is a `Store` over typed namespaces (documents, atoms, runs, elements, fields, conflicts, links, transclusions, marks, annotations, version events, operation intents), each identifier a versioned, domain-separated preimage; absence is the primitive read result (`Theory/Hyperdocument.lean` header). The element tree is `container | runs | embed(transclusion)`: a transclusion is placed by an embed leaf naming a `TransclusionRecord`, and the record is the one durable object (`Hyperdocument.lean:350-359`). The reference it stores, `StoredTransclusionRef` (`:520-531`), carries: the source identity; an **opening** (`OpeningDescriptor`, `:506-512`: a committed, codec-identified relation naming what part or view of the source is taken, bound by a commitment under the deployment); a **mode** `snapshot | live` (`:345-348`); a **disclosure scope** and a **capability ceiling**, both finite sets of `DisclosureAtom`s (`:515-518`), i.e. what the transclusion may reveal and the most authority it can confer. A transclude writes the reference and its forward link in one guarded patch; backlinks are derived history (`HyperdocumentOperations.lean:52-62`). Every content effect is a first-order declaration (create, edit, link, transclude, mark, annotate) that derives one patch with every write guarded by its exact expected prior value, a footprint, an effect digest and a canonical post; no callback supplies a post-state (`HyperdocumentOperations.lean` header). History is a `CausalVersionDag`: content-addressed events binding schema and semantic versions, exact pre/post roots and a parent frontier; `ValidAppend` refuses replay, missing or duplicate parents, and version rollback; parents need not be tips (offline siblings arrive late); replay is relational; consensus and finality are a separate layer (`Theory/CausalVersionDag.lean` header). Authority: a content cell has `body` and `annotations` fields and capabilities over sub-fields (`TypedAuthorization.lean:538-546`); a room is a cell and `under R` is the parent-projection scope, realm over area over room, with every admitted capability's lineage tracing to a root that reaches the cell the same way (`Theory/RoomAuthorization.lean:4-14`). Realms issue assets through **wells** (an account born in a realm; mint and burn under the well's own law; conservation per batch; `Kernel/RealmWellReceiver.lean`). Kinds carry **executable methods**: a method table in ROM, programs by content id, outputs mapped to fields, laws still judging every write (`docs/design/world-kind-executable-methods.md`). The MUD templates (sheet, room, item, quest, org, market, the Tidewrack realm) are the worked example of laws composed as predicates over numbered fields (`native/resource-client/templates/mud/README.md`). The planned Deos layer names the rest: DocMerge as LUB / pushout, fog-of-war, an affordance algebra, "the coalgebraic side enumerates observations: lenses, views, fog-of-war" (`ATLAS.md:137,280`; `docs/KERNEL-NECESSITY.md:178`). The RECEIVER lane measured 33 hand-written receiving families with 58 copies of `Reject` and 56 of `Result` across 149K lines of Kernel (`docs/design/RECEIVER.md`): fn1's sin, in Lean.

**nanodregg.** A World is a finite map of typed cells; it advances only by Turns; one function, `World.admit`, is the whole admission path and its properties are theorems; one writer per World, followers re-execute every Turn and refuse the log by name if the host lied; a fork is a new World; shape changes are re-genesis from a checked export (state carried, history never); "Lean decides, Rust moves bytes" (`CHARTER.md`, `README.md`). A **package** is a checked term with an interface, a state type, methods `State → Args → Req → State`, laws `State → State → Req → Verdict`, **relations** that are bounded keyed places under the object (never values; limits, sum caps, retraction policy, a guard), a doc line, and **holes**: typed contextual candidates whose selections are resolved at invocation against authenticated rows and pinned supports, and whose refusal names the justification the caller failed to pin (`Lang/Package.lean`, `Lang/LiveHoles.lean`). Laws judge by **occasion** (birth, adopt, write, relaw, invoke). On top: **activities** (generated methods that keep a plan, pending and log rows, and resume with a typed answer, patience measured in World heights), **offers and fusion** (parties post terms; fusion judges a named deal and consumes the offers atomically) with **escrow** (posting funds a value leg; fusion pays both legs), **generators** (a method emits package bytes through install), **claims** (`guardedBy` with a soundness theorem), and **foreign receipts** (a receipt justifies an action relative to a trusted head object: admission at a height, never arbitrary outside content) (`docs/OBJECTS.md`). Two demonstrations were approved: documents and escrow.

## The phrase, defined from what they built
A **programmable transclusionary object realm** is: objects that are typed cells with member-authored laws and methods (nanodregg's packages), living under authority scopes that are parent projections with their own asset wells (minidregg's realms), where any object can take an **opening** into another, by snapshot or live, carrying with it what it may disclose and the most authority it can pass on (minidregg's transclusion), over a causal history whose admission is one function and whose replay is relational, and where reads name the supports they relied on and are refused by name when those supports moved (nanodregg's holes).

## What fn2 inherits, and where its pruned kernel is too small
fn2 is the substrate these semantics ran out of room for: the journal, replication, witnessing, obligations, bytes and faults. The dregg lineage is the object, law and transclusion layer above it. Read together:
1. **Decide is `World.admit`, by occasion.** nanodregg's one admission function with laws judging birth, adopt, write, relaw and invoke is the shape fn2's Decide should take per domain: the occasion is part of the command, and "relaw" (replacing an object's law) is the versioned-world's "evolve the activity" as a first-class act. fn2's kernel lacks the occasion and lacks laws as member-authored, installed, content-named programs.
2. **Relations as places are fn2's ordered-run schemas**, with declared limits, sum caps, retraction policy and a guard: exactly what the C brief's folds need and do not yet declare. "A relation is a place, never a value" is the rule that keeps a fold from being materialized whole into a reply.
3. **A transclusion is a Span with an opening, a mode, a disclosure scope and a capability ceiling.** fn2's Span (a keyed relation between two streams or objects) has no opening (which committed view of the source), no mode (snapshot pins a version; live follows a frontier), and no disclosure or ceiling. Those four are what make a reference across realms or nodes safe to follow: they are the fog-of-war and the attenuation. The pruned kernel's Span must grow them, or transclusion across worlds is a bolt-on.
4. **Holes with pinned supports are the read contract done right.** A nanodregg read at invocation names every row and cell it relied on, at their versions; the refusal lists what moved. That is fn2's `exactly-at(p)` read with its supports made explicit, and it is what a projection's `read-fallback` should return when it cannot answer: the named supports, not a bare gap.
5. **Activities with patience in heights are obligations with deadlines in a declared time domain**, and offers-fusion-escrow are `⊗` obligations consumed atomically by Decide. fn2 has the obligation; it lacks the "plan with pending and log rows that resumes with a typed answer", which is the durable continuation the versioned-world vantage also asks for.
6. **Generators are "materialize"**: a method that emits a content-named package installed by the same check as a member's install. fn2's Name plus a Decide that installs code is the versioned-world's materialize; the kernel lacks "code is an installed, content-named object with an interface".
7. **Foreign receipts are cross-node admission at a height against a trusted head**: fn2's witnessed checkpoint claims and its peer protocol, made into a first-class object a World holds. nanodregg refuses cross-World atomic commitment and merges of divergent histories; fn2's answer is transport: a fork is a new domain, and a migration between domains carries its equivalence or its declared loss (TRANSPORT.md, in flight).
8. **Realms are authority scopes as parent projections, with wells.** fn2's principals and accounts have no scope tree; `under R` with capability lineage to a root is the authorization model a federation of worlds needs, and wells (assets born in a scope, conserved per batch) are the ledger vantage's primitive.
9. **Re-genesis versus history.** nanodregg carries state and never history across a shape change. fn2's whole point is the history; so fn2 must give nanodregg-style Worlds what they refused themselves: a migration that carries history under a recorded equivalence. That is the transport experiment, and it is the thing minidregg's `CausalVersionDag` (schema and semantic versions bound into every event, rollback refused) already prepared for.
10. **The RECEIVER lesson, carried.** 33 receiving families, 58 `Reject`s: the one-receiver collapse minidregg attempted is what fn2's DSLs exist to prevent; a second shape of an existing thing is a defect (nanodregg law 1).

## For the widest-role synthesis
The council's vantages (versioned world, OS substrate, agent fleet, federation, composition, sovereign node, substrate-under-systems, economy of obligations, transclusive commons, physical and off-planet) all land on this layer: objects with laws, realms with wells, transclusions with openings and ceilings, holes with pinned supports, activities and offers, generators and receipts. The kernel fn2 should be derived from is the one on which THAT layer is a composition. Minimum additions to the pruned eight, from this reading alone: occasion-typed Decide with installed laws; Span with opening, mode, disclosure, ceiling; reads that name their supports; obligations as resumable activities; code as content-named installed objects; scope trees with lineage and wells; history-carrying migration.
