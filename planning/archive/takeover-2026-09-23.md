## What the source changes about the handoff

- **Do not re-prove hybrid subject injectivity.**
  `fn-hsig-subject-body-injective` already exists in
  `books/hybrid-signature-invariants.lisp`, with
  [certification evidence](../evidence/hybrid-injectivity-certification-2026-09-21.md).

  `fn-stxe-profile-supportedp` in `books/stx-evidence-records.lisp` still
  refuses every profile.
- **The acceptance stamp is still a schema change to implement.** Records
  remain schema 0; NEWNEWS still derives its date from article fields.
  [The stamp specification](../../specs/acceptance-stamp.md) owns the new source
  of time, legacy handling, and every prepare caller.
- **The duplicate/conflict twin remains real.**
  `fn-store-article-match` in `host/store-host.lisp` decides this outside
  ACL2, with callers in `host/store-node-host.lisp` and `host/owner-host.lisp`.
  T3 must move the decision to the executable core and connect those callers.
- **T4 is not just another timing repair.**
  `fn-sn-finish-preserves-indexedp` in `books/store-node-invariants.lisp`
  excludes the accepted-statement arm. The unconditional invariant and
  meaningful witnesses remain work even though its current proof is fast.
- **An outbound BP machine is not the proposed complete node machine.**
  `host/native/bp-service.lisp` calls `fn-bpn-step` for outbound events.
  Native reception in `host/native/tcpcl.lisp` and `host/native/bp-app.lisp`
  still follows the older callback/receipt path. The article-and-receipt gate
  needs A1's service loop, A2's replay/crash argument, and A3's durable receipt
  outbox/Store join together.
- **Frozen launchers are not yet self-contained.** The image runbook copies
  launchers whose core path still points outside the image directory. The
  campaign hashed both core copies and found agreement for that run; that
  does not make future use of the launcher immutable. The deploy runbook
  also refuses an existing store, so the successful node upgrade was manual.

## Next joins, in dependency order

**The Store schema and semantics sequence.** Once the first validation and
carrier edits are reconciled, give T2a a shared interface and an integration
lead; collaborators can handle codec, caller and proof work. Its scope is the explicit
constructor/caller list in acceptance-stamp §6: record shape and codecs,
acceptance/node, replay/checkpoint, Store, the four ingress paths, and every
changed test fixture. This touches too many callers to split casually by
feature. Preserve schema-0 bytes and legacy reads; establish schema-1 round
trips, the observation/prepare boundary, and carry-over-store recovery before
claiming the stamp is usable. Use current incremental tooling, not the old
`--closure` recipe still printed in that spec.

T2b then replaces article-date NEWNEWS behavior. T3 removes the host match
decision, and T4 covers all finish arms. Those shared Store changes run in
sequence. T8b then adopts domain/capacity changes in the live owner without
breaking existing reader pins; its gate is live `GROUP` before a restart,
including existing-reader/new-reader observations. T9b's inbound octet and
peer-row work follows the stamp edits to `peer-inbound`.

**Signed messages, end to end.** After T2/T4 and the carrier, connect native
verification to durable verdict acceptance, expose the recorded verdict to
readers, and finish the native signing CLI. Exercise two independent
correspondents, both signatures required, altered source and keys refused,
reopen preserving the same verdict and source, and relay projections not
changing the signed source. A working cryptographic primitive or carrier is
not this gate. Key custody/succession remains an explicit policy question
when its workflow is reached.

**BP schema to a real disconnected conversation.** A2 starts after A1's
schema is fixed; separate the record/replay files from the machine files.
Its byte publisher relation and recovery proofs include inherited history,
zero new events, uncertain publication, and a second crash. A1's native
receive/ACK loop co-lands with those proofs. A3 then joins the durable receipt
outbox and actual owner Store: lost contacts/replies, restart/idempotence,
and release of only the matching obligation. Do not acknowledge final TCPCL
acceptance on a mere normal callback return. The combined gate exchanges an
article and application receipt through two native processes, preserves a
second unrelated pin, and repeats the named crash/contact interruptions.

Fragment refinement C1 and the bounded status-report codec D1a are independent
packages for the next free slot. They do not delay the whole-bundle foundation.
Contact scheduling, limits, backpressure and the production DTN selector gate
must be exercised before the DTN release gate. The selector work coordinates shared host edits with the other contributors
and checks the resulting combined entry profile.

**Matrix and qualification.** Give the next free tooling slot the matrix
contract/witness work: separate obsolete configuration subjects from missing
commands, inspect the owner queue for feed-once, and drive protected peering,
reader pin concurrency, crash and signature rows as their implementations
land. Do not convert observations inferred from arrival into independent
witnesses. Reuse the INN lab; extend it to protected exchange and a second
relay when those properties are claimed. Two nodes on one box are the fast
first gate; follow with hbox-to-persvati interruption/restart evidence on
isolated stores. The existing node is not a disposable test store.

## Integration discipline

Keep the newly cheap iteration loop. A lane uses existing logs, then a bounded
live proof session, then an incremental affected-root run. Run a fresh
ordinary closure for the frozen image. Preserve theorem statements/guards
when optimizing; a new statement or assumption is a contract change with
its own evidence, not a performance fix. Remaining slow books deserve a
measured diagnosis, but they are not a reason to postpone independent
protocol implementation. ITER's named benchmark and the proof-cost reporter
are still proposed tools, not installed capabilities.

Use both hosts without oversubscribing them: proof searches are bounded;
lanes start with small job allocations inside the shared pool; root's wide
integration run gets capacity after lane jobs release it. An agent submits a
run and reports its identifier rather than spending repeated turns polling.
No mid-flight rebase that silently expands a lane's scope. Root keeps the
source revision, toolchain, include-closure digests and certificate manifest
with each landing, and commits named files frequently.

The September 23 workflow correction is now reflected in AGENTS.md and
how-we-work: wider peer coordination, incremental combined checks and
provisional triage only for hidden failures. Local defaults in
`tools/acl2_slots.py` are eight slots on Darwin; respect any configured lower
limit. No new machine capacity is allocated by widening the agent roster.

The handoff's `--affected-by` warning is still reproducible: running
`python3 tools/certify_books.py --affected-by books/store-node-resolution.lisp --dry-run`
exits 2 because it selects `host/native-*-host` roots which the runner then
rejects. Fix that selection defect in the tooling slot; meanwhile use an
explicit affected set of valid book/test roots, including dependent invariant
and test books, rather than silently narrowing the evidence to the edited
book alone.

Independent review is for a specific seam: actual caller, model/physical cut,
receipt release authority, source/projection preservation. Root reviews these
in coherent batches and sends concrete defects back to their owner. No
indefinite whole-project review queue and no model-tier names as job roles.

## Decisions and goal boundary

The user selected developer-only raw `store post`, with production posting
through the normal submission path. The decision and pending implementation
are recorded in the decision register, host contract, HST-001 and SCN-015.
The image lane owns the profile guard and negative production witnesses;
the crash lane retains the raw path only on developer images. A future
import/repair command requires its own explicit contract.

The OpenSSL build and network exposure are separate from algorithmic
correctness. Record the exact crypto artifacts and native tests in the
qualification profile. Do not change hbox's network exposure as part of a
proof or local integration run. Any later live upgrade should use the
reviewable, tested upgrade result and the user's deployment scope.

Suggested next autonomous goal:

> Bring fn to a qualified v0 release candidate for humans and agents: native
> NNTP peering, exact-source Ed25519 plus ML-DSA-65 authorship, and durable
> BP/DTN article-and-receipt exchange in one reproducible native image; close
> the required Store and served-path correspondence obligations, demonstrate
> interrupted two-node operation and recovery, and publish source-pinned
> evidence and explicit remaining trust assumptions. Use Sol implementation
> lanes and periodic root convergence while preserving the fast proof loop.

The stopping point is the required v0 behaviors and their arguments, not a
green scaffolding command or an arbitrary number of waves. Process-death and
modeled durability claims remain distinct from physical power-loss
qualification. The new goal has not been activated by this planning work.

After v0's gate, v1 proceeds along the selected trajectory: LTP against the
established BP node, served Message-ID/per-group indexes with correspondence
and scaling measurements, policy/membership epochs, retention-aware
compaction/reclamation and history pruning, then BPSec. Human web/9p clients
and private groups remain later work; the private-group cryptosystem is not
chosen by this plan. V1 performance/data-layout designs can be prepared in
free slots, but do not change the shared v0 Store beneath its qualification.
