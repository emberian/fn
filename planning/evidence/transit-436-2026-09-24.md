# transit-436: a durable transit into a node with a Path identity answered uncertain (2026-09-24)

**Result.** Fixed on `lane/transit-436` (fix `a3e10e74`, arm lemmas and
teeth `156c2aca`, logging `000c6b6e`; native test `afe11b5c`). On a
developer image built from `000c6b6e`, the matrix lane's hand repro answers
`335`/`235` and the service stays up. The new native case passes, and so do
`tests.test_native_peering` (5/5) and `tests.test_native_protected_peering`
(5/5). Two `test_bp_node_native` cases that native-subsets-6c0626c5 found
failing for this cause now pass. The same new native case fails on the frozen
6c0626c5 developer image with `436 transfer failed; the outcome is
uncertain`.

## Cause

The wire word for an article completion comes from
`fn-own-finish` (`books/owner-served-invariants.lisp`). Since the p2-wire merge,
`host/owner-host.lisp:515` `fn-owner-finish-submission` calls it, and
`host/native/owner.lisp:767` installs it as the completion callback of
`fnn-owner-attempt`. A carrier-absent transit takes that path from
`fnn-owner-attempt-transit`. `fn-own-finish` returned `:durable` only when
`fn-own-completion-names-submission-p` held, which required:

    books/owner-served-invariants.lisp:56 (at fc2e1941)
      (equal (fn-record-payload record) (fn-own-sub-octets sub))

For a transit submission, `fn-own-sub-octets` is `fn-peer-submission-octets`,
the octets as received. The owner does not store those octets. In
`fn-owner-take` (`host/owner-host.lisp:558-564` at fc2e1941), the P7 lane
stages `fn-peer-relayed-octets` instead: the received article with
`<identity>!<diagnostic>!` prepended to its Path and its Xref removed. The
store completes that record. Once a Path identity is set, the two sides always
differ. `fn-own-finish` then answers `:fault`, `host/native/io.lisp:1651`
raises `fnn-indeterminate "ACL2 rejected durable completion after
publication"`, and the attempt handler (`host/native/owner.lisp:732`)
turns that into `:uncertain` without writing a line. The drain answers
`436 ... uncertain` and stops the service with exit 3. Without a Path
identity the relayed octets equal the received ones, which is why the
existing peering tests stayed green. Hypothesis (b) of the brief is correct,
with one difference: the comparison was made in ACL2 (the keystone's own
predicate), not in the host. The P2 keystone `fn-own-240-follows-consumed-completion`
stated the same received-octet comparison. That statement was true, but it
described a word that no durable transit could ever reach.

The BP transit path (`fnn-owner-complete-bp-transit-submission`) stores the
same relayed octets, and its fixture sets `path-identity`. That explains the
native-subsets record's 9 `test_bp_node_native` failures ("owner BP transit
Store outcome is uncertain").

## Fix

- `fn-own-sub-stored-octets (cfg sub)` (books/owner-served-invariants.lisp)
  is the one definition of the octets the owner hands the Store. A local or
  control submission's are its injected octets. A transit submission's are
  `fn-peer-relayed-octets` of the received octets under the live
  configuration.
- `fn-owner-take` stages `fn-own-sub-stored-octets (fn-owner-config state) sub`.
  It replaces the host-side `if transitp` that computed the same value.
- `fn-own-completion-names-submission-p (o cfg)` and `fn-own-finish (o cfg)`
  compare the completed record with that same function. The host passes
  `(fn-ocfg-config oc)`: the configuration `fn-owner-take` read, because no
  configuration record can be staged while a submission is in flight, and a
  staged one makes the finish answer `:fault`. If the configuration did
  change between take and finish, the comparison fails in the uncertain
  direction, never toward 235.
- The keystone `fn-own-240-follows-consumed-completion` now concludes
  `(equal (fn-record-payload record) (fn-own-sub-stored-octets cfg sub))`.
  `fn-own-sub-stored-octets-of-a-local-submission-by-definition` and
  `-of-a-transit-submission-by-definition` name the two arms. They hold by
  definition and are not registry events. For served POST the keystone
  therefore says what it said before.
- The three outcomes stay distinct. Durable plus a matching record gives
  235/239, and the service stays up. A mismatching or unconsumed completion
  is still `:fault`, which becomes 436/441 uncertain plus the recovery stop,
  and the reason is now logged. Nothing becomes a refusal.
- Logging: `fnn-owner-attempt-handlers` and `fnn-owner-bound-commit-word`
  now write one stderr line when an indeterminate Store condition becomes
  `:uncertain`:
  `Store outcome uncertain; the store needs recovery: <reason>`.

## Teeth (tests/acl2/owner-served-invariants-tests.lisp)

The transit submission comes from peer `p` and is in flight on connection 4 of
the reachable `*own-taken*`. It is installed with owner-tests'
`own-with-inflight`, so it is not reached by an IHAVE through `fn-own-read`.
The configuration is `*own-peer-cfg*`, with path-identity `own.example`.

- Witness: the staged octets differ from the received ones, begin with
  `Path: own.example!`, and keep the received Path contents as their
  suffix. With the record carrying the staged octets, the owner relation
  holds, the completion is enabled, the finish is `:durable`, the reply is
  the 240 expression, and the keystone conclusion holds. The old comparison
  (record payload = received octets) is asserted false there, and a
  `must-fail` closes it.
- Negative, unstaged: the record carries the received octets. The finish
  is `:fault`, no 240 is rendered, and the conclusion is a `must-fail`.
- Negative, different article: the record carries the staged octets under
  `<other@example.invalid>`. The finish is `:fault` and the conclusion is a
  `must-fail`.
- The local-POST witnesses and the per-hypothesis teeth are unchanged.
  They now pass the same configuration.

## What this does not establish

- No production or frozen image was built from this branch, and no matrix
  run has used one. The 22 `V0-TRANSIT-*` rows, `V0-CFG-LIVE*`,
  `V0-POST-CONCURRENT` and the INN rows remain to be rerun on a frozen image.
- Of native-subsets' `test_bp_node_native` failure 1, two of the nine cases
  were rerun. The other seven, and the MRU case (c), were not.
- The ACL2 transit witness is constructed with `own-with-inflight`. It is not
  reached from an IHAVE through the owner's read path.
- `fn-own-sub-feed-groups` / `owner-feed-subject` still read
  `fn-own-sub-octets` (the received octets) for a transit's feed groups and
  loop exclusion. That choice is unchanged here. The stale comment in
  `books/owner.lisp` above `fn-own-sub-origin` ("the peer's own octets ...
  which is what fn-peer-injection-arguments stages") was left, so that
  `books/owner` did not have to be recertified.
- `planning/proof-events.json` still gives
  `fn-own-240-follows-consumed-completion` a `pending_subject` that says the
  host does not call `fn-own-finish`. That text has been stale since p2-wire.
  This lane did not reclassify it.
