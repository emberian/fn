### The revoked composite and replay (books/hybrid-store.lisp, books/replay.lisp)

- `fn-hsig-article-event-revoked-bindsp`: the carried composite's structural
  bindings at a positive keyring generation, verdict token `:revoked`, the
  verdict's generation the composite's, its detail the carrier's principal,
  the carrier's signatures well formed.
  `fn-hsig-article-event-revoked-bindsp-facts` (the analogue of
  `fn-hsig-article-event-carried-bindsp-facts`), `-is-not-carried`,
  `-binds-no-snapshot` (replay's three kind-4 branches are disjoint).
- `fn-hsig-revoked-tombstone-bindsp E KEYS SNAPSHOTS`: the generation E names
  is a snapshot of profile `fn-hybrid-revoked-v1` whose payload is exactly
  E's principal, and KEYS were enrolled for that principal by a snapshot of
  SNAPSHOTS. Replay's revoked branch (`fn-replay-apply-revoked-verdict`,
  called by `fn-replay-identity-step`) faults `:composite-binding` otherwise.
- `fn-osp-replay-admits-a-revoked-composite-exactly-at-its-tombstone`
  (books/owner-signed-post.lisp): with the verdict at the cursor, the step is
  `:ok` iff the tombstone binding holds over the replayed snapshots, and then
  it records exactly that verdict and leaves the snapshots unchanged.
  `fn-osp-carried-composite-keeps-the-keyring` and
  `fn-osp-revoked-composite-keeps-the-keyring`: neither composite changes a
  keyring.
- `fn-hsig-keyring-snapshot-value-of-keyring-event`: the keyring snapshot
  codec round trip (the kind-3 event decodes, through the selector every
  acceptance reads, to exactly the principal and keys it was built from).
  The succession keystones rest on it.

### Evidence is never rewritten

A kind-3 completion keeps every stored verdict
(`fn-hls-finish-snapshot-keeps-historical-verdicts`, existing, over
`fn-sn-finish`), and a kind-4 completion conses its own verdict onto the
list (`fn-hls-finish-kind4-verdicts`, existing). A succession or revocation
is a kind-3 event, so verdicts stored before it are unchanged over the kind-4
replay projection; a revoked or carried composite changes no snapshot
(`fn-osp-*-composite-keeps-the-keyring`).
