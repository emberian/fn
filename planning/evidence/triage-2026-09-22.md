**Nothing here is a certification claim.** A triage run is evidence of the
list of reds it prints and of nothing else: its certificates are published
to no cache, its manifest is not archived, and its certify run ids are
deliberately absent. `tools/triage.py` produced the report below on
persvati; this file records it together with the measurement that decided
how triage works.

The substitution round is the part worth reading twice. `green_check` said
`books/store-node-traces` was last green on 2026-09-21 at digest
`22285aaea66b`, from commit `8b9aa2520225`; triage wrote exactly those bytes
into the box's tree (the worktree kept `2e899f69093b`, checked on the box)
and ran again. The book failed anyway, on `FN-SNT-STATE-RECONSTRUCTION`
rather than `FN-SNT-RECORD-DIRECTORY-PRESERVES-RELATION`: those bytes were
green over dependencies this tree no longer has. Substituting a book's own
source answers only when the book's own bytes are the problem, and here the
cause is drift underneath it. Two rounds, twenty-two minutes, one red.

ACL2's provisional certification answers it properly (`:DOC
provisional-certification`). Create skips proofs and writes each book's
`.pcert0`; Convert does the proofs given only its own `.pcert0` and a
`.cert`, `.pcert0` or `.pcert1` per included book, so **the Converts have no
dependencies on each other**; Complete renames `.pcert1` to `.cert` in
dependency order. Measured by hand on the same closure before the runner
carried it: Create 32.9 s for 63 books, Convert 301.4 s at 8 jobs — its
floor is the slowest single proof, `books/store-node-invariants` at
289.4 s — and Complete 2.6 s.

What the certificates are. Complete wrote 47 of them (the other 16 books are
the 4 reds and the 12 above them). ACL2 accepts them at `include-book`: a
plain `(include-book "books/store-node-invariants")` over that whole chain
returned in 0.49 s with no warning. `tools/certs.py` accepts them, because
the only thing it reads out of a certificate file is the serializer magic
`valid_looking` checks and its content hash. And `books/store-node.cert` made
this way is byte-identical to the one an ordinary run made for the same book
apart from the absolute paths it embeds and their length prefixes — it
carries no provisional marker at all.

Two cautions, both ACL2's own, and both the reason `--pcert` is the
*discovery* mode and not the certification mode: Complete checks that each
included book has a `.cert` at least as recent as the book, not that book's
hash; and the documentation says that for maximum trust a project's books
are best certified from scratch without provisional certification. A claim
about this tree still comes from an ordinary run.

## The four reds

`books/store-prepare-correspondence` — were cascades behind it and said

and they are waiting on a certificate.
