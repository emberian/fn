# Heap census of a loaded owner, and the first memory change, 2026-09-25

Lane `lane/rep-heap`, from dev `087f7213`. Host commit `66089fd9` (the
collection trigger); the evidence commit carries this record and
`rep-heap-2026-09-25/`. Design context:
`planning/design-2026-09-25-representation.md` §3 ("needs a heap census"),
boundaries 4 and 6. D27.

## Method

- **Images** (`rep-heap-2026-09-25/*-image.sha256`): before = dev
  `087f7213`, census image core `a1a381f5…`; after = lane `66089fd9`, core
  `7a5e4edd…`. Each is the developer image built by `build.sh`/`setup.sh`
  (default-profile roots certified in place from `/tank/fn/certcache`,
  `proof_artifacts acquire`/`validate` 290 books, `tools/build_native_host.sh`
  under `swarm-build`, OpenSSL 3.5.8) with one scratch-only change,
  `census-hook.lisp`: its `fn-native-entry` is the real one plus
  `(load $FN_CENSUS_LOAD)` before `fnn-main`. With the variable unset the
  image is the developer image. Scratch `/tank/fn/scratch/rep-heap/`.
- **The census** (`census.lisp`, loaded at run time): it wraps
  `fnn-owner-install` to keep the service object, and on a trigger file,
  holding the owner mutex, runs `(sb-ext:gc :full t)`, prints
  `sb-kernel:dynamic-usage`, `(room t)` (bytes per SBCL type over the whole
  dynamic space) and `sb-vm:instance-usage`, then walks, with one `eq`
  visited table so that shared structure is counted once, (1) the ACL2
  global `fn-owner`, broken down positionally to depth 6 (the owner record,
  its Store, the Store's files/node/indexes, the view, the connections),
  (2) every other ACL2 state global except the world, (3) the host service
  structure and everything it reaches (store struct, feeds, clients,
  workers). Sizes are `sb-ext:primitive-object-size`. Each depth-1 and
  Store/view/connection slot is also walked alone with a fresh table, which
  shows the sharing.
- **Load** (`census_run.py`): `operator CONFIG init --profile scale fn.test`
  on tmpfs (`/dev/shm`), then POSTs over one NNTP connection, censuses at
  N = 0 and at the checkpoint. Articles: `msgid_measure.article` (155
  octets, the "160-octet" article of earlier records) and a 16,379-octet
  article (the same headers, 76-character body lines). Every run under
  `systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=40G`. RSS is `VmRSS` read before
  the census (the census allocates its visited table, so no RSS after it is
  quoted).

N = 1000 with 155-octet articles; 282.2 MB and 516.2 MB with 16,379-octet
articles. The 250 MB floor is the image (ACL2 world, code, symbols: 10.2M
conses of 163 MB at N = 120, unchanged by N within 0.4%). Everything that
grows with N is in the owner:

- **One copy of each payload.** Walked alone, the records, the node, the
  event index, the view's archive, the trie and the connection's pinned view
  each reach the same 263 MB at N = 1000 (16 KiB): they share the one
  octet list per article. There is no second copy of the history anywhere:
  not in the host (the service reaches 3.6 KB; `fnn-owner-install`'s
  `(mapcar #'fnn-octet-list records)` is garbage once recovery returns), not
  in another ACL2 global (the store bridge's globals are empty after
  `fnn-bridge-reset`).
- **Slope from the census.** Live heap (whole dynamic space):
  (516.2 − 282.2) MB / 880 = 265.9 KB per 16 KiB article; owner walk 265.8 KB.
  155-octet: 6.2 KB per article (walk: 6.26 KB). Exponent 1.00 for every
  row, both sizes (log ratio of N = 1000 to N = 120).
- **Against the RSS slope.** Before the change, RSS at N = 120 / 1000:
  697 / 1,897 MB (155 octets), 2,232 / 2,488 MB (16 KiB): 1,363 KiB per
  155-octet article and 291 KiB per 16 KiB article, against a live slope
  of 6.1 KiB and 260 KiB. The 2 MiB-per-article RSS slope the design
  quotes, and the "15 MB per article" of `m5-capacity`, were never data:
  the census found `bytes-consed-between-gcs` = 1,677,721,600 in the owner
  (SBCL's default, 5% of the launcher's 32,000 MB `--dynamic-space-size`;
  ACL2's own 1 GiB setting is made only on first LP entry, which the
  native image never does). Up to 1,600 MiB of dead objects accumulate
  between collections, and the dynamic usage before each census was
  0.89 to 1.70 GB against the 0.25 to 0.52 GB live after it. RSS climbs
  toward live + 1.6 GB at a rate set by allocation per POST, not by the
  data kept.

## The two largest holders, and what was done

1. **Largest term of RSS at every N measured: the garbage headroom** (up
   to 1,600 MiB; 1.26 to 1.55 GiB of the before-image RSS at N = 1000).
   A host setting, not a copy and not an ACL2 value. **Landed:**
   `host/native/owner.lisp:1773` `+fnn-owner-gc-nursery-octets+` = 64 MiB,
   set at `:1779`, the first form of `fnn-owner-run`, so recovery and
   service both run under it. It bounds dead memory and collection work,
   not data: the live heap is not capped by it and grows with the store.
   Chosen from a sweep on the before image (`FN_GC_NURSERY`, 64 MiB and
   256 MiB, table below); 64 MiB costs no load time.
2. **Largest live holder: the payload as an octet list**, 16 octets per
   payload octet, held once, O(N·L). An ACL2 value in the logical record
   (`fn-record-payload`, books/records-shape.lisp). **Not landed; the
   exact change, which is the design's boundary 4 and a freeze decision:**
   - Representation: an abstract stobj `fn-payloads` whose logical view is
     the list of payload octet lists by history position and whose `:exec`
     is one resizable `(unsigned-byte 8)` arena with an offset/length
     vector per record (1 octet per payload octet, 16 per record). The
     history record keeps every other field; its payload position holds
     the record's index into the arena in the concrete store and the octet
     list in the logical one.
   - Correspondence obligations: the abstract stobj's `{:logic,:exec}`
     preservation for `append-payload` (the commit), `payload-len`,
     `payload-byte` and `payload-octets` (which returns the list; used only
     where a caller still needs the list), and one keystone per host-called
     reader: `fn-record-payloadp` over the arena equals it over the list;
     the served ARTICLE/BODY writer's octets from the arena equal
     `fn-article` body octets; the frame and identity digests read the
     arena through the same equality. Recovery establishes the stobj
     invariant from the replayed records; each commit preserves it.
   - Dependents: `books/records-shape` is included by 4 books directly and
     `tools/certify_books.py --affected-by books/records-shape` selects
     **624 roots**, i.e. the tree. Every owner/store/node book that
     threads the store would take the stobj as a formal. That is a
     coordinated freeze, not a lane edit.
   - What it buys, by the census: 262 KB of the 266 KB per 16 KiB article
     becomes 16 KB; at the scale ceiling (below) 12.9 GB becomes 0.8 GB.
   - Not a string: an ACL2 string on SBCL is a `simple-character-string`
     (`(coerce ... 'string)` never makes a base string), 4 octets per
     character, measured here (65 characters, 280 bytes). A payload string
     is 4 L + 16, a quarter of the list, not the L + 16 the design's §3
     assumed (corrected there).

The second live holder at small articles is the four record strings (1.1 KB
of 6.3 KB per 155-octet article, 18%): the same SBCL fact, and the same
leaf (records-shape). Third is the trie (611 B per article, 10%), which is
per-character conses of the Message-ID key. Both are O(N) with small
constants; neither matters at 16 KiB (0.7% together).

## The pessimistic memory sentence, scale profile, after the change

For a Store under the scale profile (at most 4,096 transactions, at most
196,608 payload octets per record), the owner's live heap is about
250 MB + N × (16 L + 3.7 KB) for N articles of L payload octets, exponent 1
in N and 1 in L (every census row: exponent 1.00 between N = 120 and
N = 1000; the 3.7 KB is the non-payload per-article cost at these headers
and grows with header length at 4 octets per character), so at most
4,096 × 16 × 196,608 = 12.9 GB of payload conses plus 15 MB at the ceiling
with every record full, and its resident set adds the 64 MiB trigger and
SBCL's copying reserve for a full collection (measured at N = 1000,
L = 16 KiB: RSS 0.86 GB against 0.52 GB live, RSS slope 273 KiB per
article against the census's 260 KiB). The 12.9 GB is 40% of the launcher's
32,000 MB dynamic space, which is the hard ceiling (heap exhaustion) and
itself a constant that bounds data under D27: a full collection of a 12.9 GB
generation needs copy space beside it. The payload arena (holder 2) makes
the payload term 1 L, 0.8 GB at the same ceiling. This is a figure for the
ACL2 owner's heap from the definitions and the census; it does not include
the kernel page cache of the store's files, the feed journals, or a peer
feed's per-peer state (none configured in these runs).
