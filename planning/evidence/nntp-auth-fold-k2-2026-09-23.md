# K2 local posting exclusion on the served read

The former OB-AUTH-FOLD sentence, “no submission of any kind under a no-posters policy,” was false. An initial reader with a nonposting credential can authenticate into a separately configured peer role, then submit TAKETHIS transit through one `fn-served-step` read. `tests/acl2/nntp-auth-fold-tests.lisp` executes that reachable sequence and checks that the result is `fn-peer-submissionp`, not `fn-inj-injectedp`.

`books/nntp-auth-fold.lisp` proves the actual local-authority guarantee. `fn-auth-fold-step-has-no-local-submission` excludes every injected `:submit` effect in a whole read when the connection is valid, the pinned credential policy grants posting to nobody, and no local POST body was already pending. `fn-auth-fold-step-preserves-safe-connp` carries this premise to the next read. The factor chain establishes POST-command origin through the pinned NNTP, POST, peer, and auth transitions; auth refuses that command under the no-posters policy; the result is folded through `fn-served-dispatch`, framed event lists, bytes, and `fn-served-feed`. It does not constrain authorized transit submissions. The nonempty no-posting credential, AUTHINFO login, peer transit, refused local POST, and a complete accepted POST when the no-posters premise is removed are executable teeth. The final `must-fail` uses that accepted POST to refute removal of the keystone's safe-connection premise.
