# Maintained topic projection across Store publication and recovery

The new `fn-sti-livep` relates the carried topic projection to the exact committed Store prefix at `fn-sn-identity-next`, checks a staged topic candidate before publication, and checks the physical last event during `:completed`. These are proof-side scans, not served validation. `fn-thr-*` selector lemmas show that actual Store crash erases the topic projection and successful recovery or observed open rebuilds it from the physical history. The prefix book supplies append and rollback facts used by that relation.

The manifests contain exact source digests, toolchain identity, and root results; they do not certify this combined checkpoint.
