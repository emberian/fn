# Control messages C1: evidence (2026-09-25)

Packet C1 of [the control-message design](../design-2026-09-25-control-messages.md)
(§5): recognize a control article and file it, execute nothing.
Requirement NNT-010, proof target PRF-078, scenario SCN-041; the
specification is [peering §8, "Filing"](../../specs/peering.md#8-control-messages-filing-and-authority-implemented-cancel-decided-served-withdrawal-open-group-control-deferred).

## What changed

- `books/control-classify.lisp` (new, prefix `fn-ctl-`): `fn-ctl-classify-octets`
  parses the received octets with `fn-article-parse` (no Lisp reader) and
  classifies on the `Control` field alone: `:ordinary`, `(:control VERB ARGS)`,
  or `(:malformed REASON)` for two Control fields, Control beside Supersedes
  (RFC 5536 §3.2.3 MUST NOT) or a command outside `verb *(1*WSP argument)`.
  The filing group comes from a fixed table, never from article octets:
  `control.<verb>` for cancel, newgroup, rmgroup, checkgroups, ihave, sendme;
  `control` for obsolete (RFC 5537 §5.6) and unknown verbs (local policy:
  verb compared in ASCII lower case).
- `books/peer-authored-accept.lisp` `fn-pa-filing-plan RECEIVED GROUPS DOMAIN`:
  `(:file GROUPS)` for an ordinary article, `(:file (control.<verb>))` for a
  control article whose group the operator created, else `(:refused
  :control-not-filed)`, `:control-malformed`, or `:control-signed` (open,
  below). Stored bytes are the received bytes; only membership changes.
- `books/nntp-post.lisp`: three 441 lines, one per reason.
- The plan change in the host: `host/owner-host.lisp:1282`
  `fn-owner-control-filing` calls `fn-pa-filing-plan` over the owner's
  allocation domain (`fn-state-groups`), and `host/native/owner.lisp:821`
  calls it first in `fnn-owner-attempt-transit`, before the carrier form and
  any Store call; a refusal returns through `fnn-owner-transit-refused`
  (detail recorded for the log line and the 441 word), a filing replaces the
  ingress's groups.

## Theorems (all in the certified books below)

- `fn-ctl-control-article-is-filed-only-in-control` (KEYSTONE,
  `books/peer-authored-accept.lisp`): for received octets classified
  `:control`, the plan files in exactly the article's filing group, which is
  `control` or `control.*` and in DOMAIN, or refuses `:control-not-filed` /
  `:control-signed`. Subject is the host-called `fn-pa-filing-plan`
  (`host/owner-host.lisp:1284`, called from `host/native/owner.lisp:821`).
- `fn-ctl-cmsg-subject-is-ordinary` (KEYSTONE, same book): with no Control
  field the plan returns `(:file GROUPS)` unchanged, whatever the Subject
  (`cmsg ...`), Newsgroups (`.ctl`) or Also-Control say (RFC 5537 §5).
- `fn-ctl-classify-reads-only-the-control-field` (KEYSTONE,
  `books/control-classify.lisp`): inserting any field that is neither
  Control nor Supersedes anywhere leaves the classification unchanged.
- Supporting: `fn-ctl-unsigned-control-filing-by-definition`,
  `fn-ctl-classify-without-control-is-ordinary`,
  `fn-ctl-filing-group-is-a-control-group`, `fn-ctl-filing-group-is-a-filing-group`.

Teeth (`tests/acl2/control-tests.lisp`): reachable witnesses (a cancel naming
fn.test with control.cancel in the domain is filed in control.cancel; without
it refused; unknown verb filed in `control`; two Control fields malformed;
signed refused; cmsg Subject and `.ctl`/Also-Control articles keep fn.test);
one `must-fail` per hypothesis (an ordinary article is not filed in a control
group; the ingress's own groups are not the answer for a control article; a
signed control article is not filed; inserting a Control field, or a
Supersedes field, changes the classification). The host stub test
`tests/native_peer_authored_accept_raw.lisp` checks the refusal detail
reaches `fnn-owner-transit-refused` and a filing replaces the groups.

## Findings

1. IHAVE's refusal code is 437 (RFC 3977 §6.3.2 "transfer rejected"); 439
   is TAKETHIS's (§6.3.4 streaming, RFC 4644). The brief said 439 for IHAVE;
   the image and test use 437, which is the RFC's code. TAKETHIS was not
   exercised natively.
2. The reason reaches the IHAVE peer only through the operator log: the
   437 line says `refused by acceptance`, not `control-not-filed`. POST
   carries the reason on the wire. Naming the reason on the 437 text is a
   small follow-up in the transit reply rendering.
3. OPEN (`:control-signed`): a control article with an FN-Authorship carrier
   is refused, not filed, because a signed article's Store record must list
   exactly its source's Newsgroups (`books/hybrid-store.lisp`). Refused
   rather than stored in the named groups, so RFC 5537 §3.7 holds.
4. Found and fixed here: the local signed-author path bypassed the filing
   step (above). "Every ingress" held only for the callers of
   `fnn-owner-attempt-transit`; `fnn-owner-complete-bound-submission` with a
   commit callback is a second route to the Store, and any future caller of
   it must file first.
5. BP transit takes the same `fnn-owner-attempt-transit` step (line 1370)
   and the theorem covers it, but no native case drives a control bundle.
6. D27: `fn-pa-filing-plan` runs `fn-article-parse` over the received
   article as an octet list (`fnn-octet-list payload`, like the existing
   carrier-form call beside it). It is per article, bounded by the ingress's
   article limit, but it has no concrete twin with a correspondence theorem;
   it belongs on the representation plan's list of boundaries.
7. Nothing is executed: a filed cancel withdraws nothing (C2 to C4 are
   proposals in the design).
