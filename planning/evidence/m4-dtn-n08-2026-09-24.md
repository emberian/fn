**What the lab cannot express:**
- **Application acceptance and receipts.** The DTN image has no application
  receiver, `bp-app` or `bp-node`. Its `bp receive` refuses every bundle. The
  application receipt therefore travels only on the Python host
  (`run_fn_exchange_lab.py`: lost receipt, distinct-BID retry, receipt
  regenerated and returned through BP into durable sender state, all 8
  assertions). That run's restarts use SIGTERM, and no fn native process
  is involved.
- **A kill mid-transfer.** On loopback a 149-octet bundle completes before a
  kill can land. The interruption is therefore a byte-level cut by the relay,
  and the kill hits a carrier that holds the bundle.
- **An fn-to-fn exchange through dtn7-rs.** It would need a DTN-image
  receiver that admits the bundle.
- **Four nodes on a real BPA.** The four-node lab's dtn7 mode raises
  `NotImplementedError` (`run_four_node_lab.py:462`).

## Findings

1. **The DTN image carries the kind-8 books but not their host driver.**
   `host/native/build-dtn.lisp` includes `books/bp-node-machine` and the rest
   of the kind-8 closure, but its raw loads (lines 101 to 113) stop at
   `bp-contact.lisp`. Only `host/native/build.lisp:224` loads
   `host/native/bp-node.lisp` (the `bp-node` verb and the
   `FN_BP_NODE_TEST_PAUSE_AFTER_KIND_EIGHT_SENT` pause at `bp-node.lisp:255`),
   and only `:214` loads `bp-obligation.lisp`.
   - The kind-8 retry is therefore observable only on the default image.
   - `test_bp_node_native` reads `FN_NATIVE_DEVELOPER_HOST`
     (`tests/test_bp_node_native.py:25`), not a DTN variable.
   - The 47bdb9a4 record ran that module against the default developer image
     too, so "on a DTN image" was never its setting.
2. **Every inbound bundle on the DTN image is refused at the receive
   boundary (a).** `bp receive` installs `fnn-bp-deliver-node … nil nil`
   (`host/native/bp.lisp:562-564`), and `fnn-bps-tcpcl-ingress` returns NIL
   without an owner and channel (`host/native/bp-service.lisp:675-685`).
   `fn-bpnf-receive-wire-event` then refuses `:receive-boundary`
   (`books/bp-node-receive-boundary.lisp:39`).
   - This dates from `76567b87` (2026-09-23, "Admit BP principals from
     durable observed-channel profiles").
   - Since then the DTN image can author and forward bundles but cannot
     accept any. The receive-integrity module passes because it asserts
     refusals and staging, not acceptance.
   - The relay fixture's "missing boundary trust" is this defect. It is not a
     fixture gap that a test can close on the DTN image.
3. **`bp send` classifies a severed contact as a fault (a).**
   `fnn-command-bp-send` (`host/native/bp.lisp:503-522`) has no handler for
   `fnn-os-error` or `sb-bsd-sockets:socket-error`. The `ECONNRESET` escapes
   as `store: [Errno 104] …` with exit 4. `bp receive` maps the same
   conditions to uncertain (exit 3, `bp.lisp:585-587`), and `bp-service run`
   under the same relay cut exits 3 with `reason=uncertain`.
   - This breaks the uncertain/refused/accepted rule at the CLI boundary.
   - The durable authored wire (`authored-0.wire`) exists, and it is
     unresolved rather than faulted.
4. **The relay test's expiry step does not observe expiry.** It is
   undiagnosed. This is the first run to reach the step.
   - `bp-contact tick` drives `:clock` through `fnn-bpc-advance-clock`
     (`host/native/bp-contact.lisp:15-20`), and the machine has an expired
     transition (`books/bp-node-machine.lisp:555`).
   - A tick at wall 3600001, after `work-to-expire` was sent with wall 0,
     answers only `BP queue recovered jobs=2` and `BP contact closed`.
   - It may be a test error about which clock ages the bundle, or an fn
     defect. That is left open.
5. **A `bp send` retry is a new bundle.** Step 2 carried `sequence=1`, not
   step 1's `sequence=0` (the comment at `bp.lisp:499-500` says so). No
   duplicate reached dtn7d only because the first bundle never left fn. If a
   cut came after the peer's XFER_ACK, the far BPA would hold two bundle IDs
   for one ADU. N08's same-identity retry belongs to `bp-node`, not to `bp
   send`.
6. **Lab defects, fixed in the tests:**
   - `run_fn_exchange_lab.py:57` called `git rev-parse` unguarded. It now
     reads `FN_GATE_REVISION` first, as the four-node lab does.
   - The same file rebound `report` to a `BundleReport` inside the receipt
     step, so its `finally` raised `TypeError` and hid the outcome. The
     variable is now `identified`.
7. **The four-node lab (mock BPA) fails at relay-a's recovery after its
   SIGKILL.** `run_four_node_lab.py:511` resolves the durable intent as
   `committed` without inspecting the Store, and ACL2 rejects that history
   (`tools/workflow_journal.py:215`). This is a Python-host lab on the retiring
   host. It was not diagnosed further.

## M4's exit clause

*Interrupted exchange and restart preserve accepted responsibilities;
application acceptance remains distinguishable from transport delivery.*

- **Has:**
  - On the default developer image, a death after a durable kind 8 is
    retried with the original identity, delivered once, and held once, and
    it is not re-attempted (N08, theorems of `bp-kind8-retry`).
  - The whole BP node module passes 17/17.
  - On the DTN image, the send side, contact windows, the queue and the
    journals survive restart (bp_service, bp_contact, app_journal).
  - Against a real BPA, an fn-authored bundle crosses a severed contact and a
    SIGKILLed carrier and is delivered once.
  - Transport acceptance (carrier XFER_ACK) and application outcome stay
    apart in every line.
  - On the Python host, a lost application receipt is regenerated and
    returned through BP.
- **Lacks:**
  - Any application acceptance on a DTN image (finding 2).
  - A native fn application receipt through a real BPA.
  - The uncertain class for a severed `bp send` (finding 3).
  - Observed expiry through `bp-contact` (finding 4).
  - A four-node run on a real BPA.
  - The native N08 on the image the plan names. It passes on the default
    image, which carries the driver.
