### 2. Link partition and outcomes, as the native runs showed them

- **Links are 4 MiB-bound for 2 KiB articles.** 4,500 records make three links
  (about 1,775 records a link at 2,363 octets a record). The tests had computed
  `ceil(N/4096)`, which is a second owner of ACL2's partition (`fn-ccc-fit`). They
  now read the chain's length from `status`, from the compaction line, or from an
  uncut reference compaction. 4096 events a link is kept only as a lower bound.
- **EIO outcomes.** An EIO before a syscall that cannot publish the name is
  refused: candidate `stage` and `file-barrier`, and `selection-file`. An EIO of a
  `link` or a `rename` leaves the name's visibility unknown, and a failed
  directory barrier leaves durability unknown. Both are uncertain (exit 3). The
  host does not inspect a visible name after an error
  (host/native/immutable-publish.lisp), so this is the three-outcome rule, not a
  defect. The first draft of the test expected `link` and `selection-replace` to
  be refused. The native run corrected that, and the scenario says it.
- **Finding: the owner's greeting waits for its open-time checkpoint.** On a
  20,000-record store with no checkpoint, `operator CONFIG run` prints `LISTENING`,
  then runs `CHECKPOINT auto sequence=20000 suffix=20000 octets=101646645
  ms=49542` before it answers the greeting. A reader that connects on `LISTENING`
  waits 50 s. The tests now wait the class's `served_timeout`; the owner is
  unchanged.

### The theorem over that call

`fn-ccc-chain-reconstructs-the-history` (books/checkpoint-pack-chain.lisp) is
generalized in place: the observation holds, for every sequence from some N at
or below the chain's boundary to the end of H, the record H holds there
(N = 0 before a reclaim, the boundary after a complete one, anything between at
a reclaim cut). With the chain ok, its records a prefix of H, H a true list and
the suffix valid under the final frontier, the answer is `(:ok H frontier)`. No
hypothesis mentions a file below N, so no reclaimed file is needed. The earlier
statement (a complete observation from 0) is the case N = 0. N need not be
stated natural: a local lemma proves the natural case and the keystone
instantiates it at `(nfix n)`.

Teeth (tests/acl2/checkpoint-pack-chain-tests.lisp): the full antecedent and the
conclusion at N = 3 and at N = 5 (the empty observation) on the two-link chain;
must-fails without N within the boundary (a one-link chain of boundary 3, a
history with a record at sequence 3 whose file is gone, N = 4: every other
hypothesis asserted), without a true-list history, without the files matching H
(record 4 differs), plus the earlier ones (integrity, link, prefix, the count,
valid suffix). Not toothed: the observation's contiguity (below the boundary a
gap is harmless, above it the valid suffix refuses one; not proved redundant).

Assurance chain for the slice:
native entry (`store recover`, `operator CONFIG run`, `store compact`: all open
through `fnn-open-live-store`) -> `fnn-pack-selected-raw-and-coverage`
(`fnn-pack-walk`, `fn-store-checkpoint-chain-coverage`) and
`fnn-pack-recover-records` -> executed ACL2 subject `fn-ccc-observe-chain`
over octet lists (the representation is the logical one; the open holds every
link as octet lists, D27 debt) -> maintained relation: the selected chain's
records are a prefix of the history and the files present run from some N at
or below its boundary. It is established by the first link's capture
(`fn-ccc-capture-link-succeeds`), preserved by each link
(`fn-ccc-capture-extends-the-chain`), by the reclaim at every cut
(`fn-ccc-reclaim-preserves-reconstructed-history`, and directly by the
generalized keystone), by retire (`fn-ccc-retire-plan-keeps-the-chain`), and
across a publication cut under the pack program's two facts
(`fn-ccc-publication-crash-walks-old-or-new-chain`; not composed by a theorem)
-> behavioural theorem `fn-ccc-chain-reconstructs-the-history` -> observed
result: the replay serves the same records, so the served answers are the same
bytes (test_scale_store_compacts_into_a_chain compares every ARTICLE of the
group and the sampled Message-IDs before and after).

### The resolution

**books/store-compact-verb.lisp.** The chain's uncapped compaction unit stays:
no `*fn-cc-max-events*` / `*fn-cc-max-octets*` refusal on data (D27). The
temporary-space refusal is dev's disk check, over the next link instead of
the whole history: `fn-cverb-link-octets records lower` = the 128-octet link
header bound + `fn-cc-event-octets-size` of the records one quantum takes
above the chain's boundary (`fn-ccc-fit`, the capture's own choice) + the
frame trailer; `fn-cverb-decide` refuses `:temporary-space` when
`fn-cverb-disk-admitsp disk-free (fn-cverb-link-octets records lower)`
fails. The keystone keeps its name and its one hypothesis, restated over the
link the host seals:

    fn-cverb-pack-fits-the-disk:
      (fn-cverb-decide profile records lower names generations selected disk-free)
        = (:compact *fn-cverb-pack-steps*)
      => (len (fn-ccc-encode-link (cadr (fn-ccc-capture-link records lower lf gen digest))))
         + *fn-frame-trailer-octets*  <=  disk-free

for every lower frontier, predecessor generation and digest. A hypothesis
`(<= (len digest) 32)` was first needed and then proved redundant (the
bounded encoder writes nothing for a longer digest; the weakened theorem is
the one in the book). `fn-cverb-temporary-space-is-the-disk-by-definition`
lost the two unit hypotheses (no size refuses). One decision function, and
among its refusals by name the disk's.

Deviation from the brief, stated: the brief asked for two refusals, "the
profile's space budget is short OR the disk cannot hold the link". The
chain side's `(< (fn-cverb-space-budget profile) (+ footprint pack))` was the
base's history-bound check, not a chain feature; PKT-169 (decided
2026-09-26) replaced it by the disk because it refused every store past half
its history bound (reclaim-lifecycle F2), and names halving the bound the
rejected alternative. Reintroducing it would re-open F2 and break dev's
tight-store result. It is not reintroduced; PKT-332 (2) records this.

**host/native/checkpoint.lisp.** One `fnn-pack-publish-generation (store
records &key chain coverage selected summary)` for both callers: the chain
(`fnn-pack-extend-chain`, the capture of the next link through
`fn-store-checkpoint-chain-capture`) and the reclaiming pack
(`fnn-reclaim-steps`, `:summary` = the octets `fn-store-reclaim-decide`
captured). `fnn-compact-decide` is the observation (chain coverage,
namespaces, `fnn-disk-free-octets`) and the one call of
`fn-store-compact-decide`; `fnn-compact-steps` asks it once and, through
`fnn-pack-extend-chain`'s `:admit`, again over a fresh observation before
every further link, so the disk is never asked for more than one link; a
refusal there says `links=K`, keeps the K selected links, and a rerun
continues. `fnn-pack-retirable-generations` (the chain side's list: ACL2's
retire plan outside the selected chain, plus the selected head) is handed to
both the compact and the reclaim decision, so a chain's own older links never
count as "older" (without it a keep-forever `store reclaim` on a chain would
answer `:resume-retire`). `fnn-compact-footprint` is gone (dev removed the
footprint).

**host/checkpoint-host.lisp.** Both include-books.

**The reclaiming pack of a chained history** (books/store-reclaim-pack.lisp).
`fn-rclp-reclaiming-pack-is-a-first-link` (PRF-085): when `fn-rclp-decide`
answers `:reclaim`, the summary it hands the host, read as
`fn-ccc-link-of-summary` (how the chain decodes a version-0 pack), is a valid
link (`fn-ccc-linkp`) with lower 0 and no predecessor whose boundary is LOWER,
the selected chain's coverage, and the whole committed history. Its selection
makes a one-link chain and `fn-ccc-retire-plan` retires every old link. It
cannot be a successor of the old chain's head: a link's lower is its
predecessor's boundary, and the reclaim rewrites records below it. Nothing
materializes the links beyond what the open already holds. A rewritten
history past one quantum (4096 events or 4 MiB) is refused `:spans-links` by
name, nothing written (before the disk check). Why it cannot be yet: it needs
the rewritten records captured as a new chain of links, all published
unselected, then one selection (a partial selection would point the open at
transaction files the compaction already removed), with a keystone over that
chain and a disk figure over all its links (PKT-332 (1)).
