## Findings

1. Pack generation names stay below 4096 (`fn-cprt-next-generation`). A chain holds its
   names, so a store can be compacted at most 4096 times.
2. The link codec round trip is executed, not proved (as for the version-0 pack).
3. The chain publication crash keystone is stated under the two facts the pack program's
   keystones give. It is not composed with them by a theorem, and there is no K0 over the
   chain cuts.
4. The compact verb's space budget still counts one pack of the whole history, and the
   per-link header is not in `fn-cverb-capture-within-pack-octets`.
5. `checkpoint pack` with nothing uncovered is refused (exit 1) rather than reported as a
   no-op.

## Fix: empty pack (2026-09-25, continuation)

Finding 5 is fixed. `checkpoint pack` with nothing uncovered now returns a named
no-op: exit 0. It no longer refuses.

- **ACL2 decides.** `fn-ccc-capture-link` (books/checkpoint-pack-chain.lisp) returns
  `(:nothing-uncovered LOWER)` when `LOWER = (len RECORDS)`. The host calls it through
  `fn-store-checkpoint-chain-capture` (host/checkpoint-host.lisp). That function
  also renders the line `packed nothing-uncovered boundary=B records=R`
  (`fn-store-checkpoint-pack-nothing-line`). `fnn-pack-publish-generation`
  (host/native/checkpoint.lisp) checks for the no-op before any directory
  operation and returns ACL2's line. `fnn-pack-extend-chain` (used by `pack select`
  and by `operator store compact`) stops its loop on the same answer, so the host
  no longer makes its own boundary comparison. `fnn-checkpoint-command-pack` prints
  the line and exits 0.
- **Theorem.** `fn-ccc-nothing-uncovered-leaves-files-and-marker-by-definition`:
  `(equal lower (len records))` ⇒ the capture is `(:nothing-uncovered lower)`, and
  `fn-ccc-pack-effect` leaves both the files alist and the marker unchanged, selected
  or not. The theorem follows from the capture's branch, so it carries the
  `-by-definition` name and is not a PRF-085 event. `fn-ccc-pack-effect` is the model
  of the effect, not a host function. The host's correspondence is the early return
  cited above.
- **Teeth** (tests/acl2/checkpoint-pack-chain-tests.lisp):
  - Witness: the two-link chain over the five-event history. Capture at 5 gives
    the no-op, and the effect on `*ct-image*` is the identity with select.
  - One uncovered record (lower 4) gives `:ok`. That link has boundary 5 and adds
    generation 2, and select moves the marker.
  - Must-fail: the conclusion without its one hypothesis, and the concrete lower-4
    instance.
- **Test** (tests/test_native_checkpoint.py,
  `test_retiring_old_pack_generations_keeps_exact_retained_sources`):
  - `pack` and `pack select` with nothing uncovered each exit 0 and print the
    no-op. They print no `generation=`, the generation-file list is unchanged and
    `selected.fncp` is unchanged byte for byte.
  - Then one post, and `pack` gives `generation=2`.
  - The native helper's 30 s timeout can now be overridden per class
    (`native_timeout`). The pack-chain class sets it to 1800 s.
- **Registry.** Added the spec line `STO-012:` under its heading. STO-007 now lists
