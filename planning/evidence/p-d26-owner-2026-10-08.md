## What was found

   (27k enabled runes in the world; osr has 124 hints, nop 63). Measured on a trivial theorem:
   `(enable f)` hint 30 ms, with `(set-inhibit-warnings "Theory")` 10 ms, with `'(f)` or
   minimal-theory 0 ms. Each touched book now carries `(local (set-inhibit-warnings "Theory"))`.
   This is worth doing in the certify driver for every book (proposal to Deputy P, not done here).
2. Hints of the form `(e/d (defs) (disables))` run on top of the whole default theory. Every defthm
   costing 20 ms or more was rewritten to `(union-theories (theory 'minimal-theory) '(runes))`
   where the runes are exactly those the previous proof's Summary reported. Program transformation:
   `planning/evidence/p-d26-owner-minthy.py BOOK LOG OUT --min-ms N [--skip names]` (log from
   `proof_repl.py send-range --full`); defuns without hints got local deftheory hints from

   and the farm with 0 refused forms.
