## Step 2: records decided at each article's own txid

Before: `fn-own-refresh` decided every record under
`fn-ctl-config-at (next txid of the archive)` over the configuration journal.
At a restart (a discontinuous refresh) that is the configuration after every
later record, so a grant revoked after an authority cancel executed would
undo the withdrawal across a restart (and a later grant would execute a
cancel that was declined). Now:

- `books/control-visible.lisp` `fn-ctl-record-txid MSGID RECORDS` (the txid of
  the article's acceptance record in the Store's records) and
  `fn-ctl-article-withdrawals A VERDICTS RECORDS CONFIGS`, which decides under
  `fn-ctl-config-at (fn-ctl-record-txid ...) CONFIGS`. A configuration record
  at the same txid precedes the event (`books/config-physical-replay.lisp`),
  so this is the configuration the commit saw.
- `books/owner.lisp` `fn-own-refresh` passes `(fn-sf-records (fn-sn-files s))`
  and `(fn-sn-config-history s)`; `fn-own-refresh-config` is removed.
- Journal entries carry the target: `(TXID CAUSE VERDICT TARGET)`.

| Theorem | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| `fn-ctl-articles-withdrawals-is-the-journal` | the rebuild the refresh runs at a discontinuity (start, reopen: recovery) equals `fn-ctl-journal-withdrawals (fn-ctl-archive-entries ARTS VERDICTS RECORDS) CONFIGS` |
| `fn-ctl-refresh-withdrawals-is-the-journal` (keystone) | if WS = the journal of OLD's entries under (V0, R0, C0), every withdrawing article of OLD has its record in R0 (`fn-ctl-all-recorded-p`), VERDICTS is V0 or V0 with one pair for a Message-ID outside OLD, and the first appended configuration record's txid is above every OLD entry's (`fn-ctl-entries-below-p`), then `fn-ctl-refresh-withdrawals NEW OLD WS VERDICTS (append R0 MORE-R) (append C0 MORE-C)` = the journal of NEW's entries under the grown inputs |
| supporting | `fn-ctl-journal-of-old-entries-is-stable`, `fn-ctl-journal-of-consed-entries`, `fn-ctl-archive-entries-of-appended-records`, `fn-ctl-archive-entries-of-new-verdict`, `fn-ctl-record-txid-of-append`, `fn-ctl-journal-withdrawals-of-append` |

**The txid of a signed article** (found natively, fixed at `e8173157`): a
signed article is stored as a kind-4 acceptance composite, not a plain
article record, so the first `fn-ctl-record-txid` found no txid for it and an
authority cancel was decided under the empty configuration (declined
`:no-grant`; the two-node run showed U still served on B). `fn-ctl-event-msgid`
now reads the article record inside the composite with
`fn-replay-composite-record`, as replay does. Pessimistic cost: one walk of
the Store records, decoding each signed composite's article record before
the match, per withdrawing article the refresh first publishes (and per
withdrawing article at recovery); a Message-ID-to-txid index beside the trie
is the follow-up. No ACL2 tooth covers a composite record (the teeth use
plain records); the native authority case does.

Host line: `fn-own-refresh` (every owner step at an idle store; the host
reaches it through `fn-owner-step`, `host/owner-host.lisp`) calls
`fn-ctl-refresh-withdrawals`; recovery is `fn-own-start`, whose refresh takes
the discontinuity arm.

Teeth (`tests/acl2/control-visible-tests.lisp`, end): T at txid 4, P's cancel
C2 at txid 5, O at 6, a grant (P, cancel, fn.mod.*) at txid 1, a revoke

## Supersedes

`books/control-authority.lisp`: `fn-ctl-withdrawal-plan CAUSE VERDICT TARGET CFG`
(the decision; `fn-ctl-cancel-plan` is it over the Control argument),
`fn-ctl-supersedes-target` (an ordinary article, exactly one `Supersedes`
field whose value is one bracketed Message-ID), `fn-ctl-article-target`,
`fn-ctl-target-octets` (one parse). The superseding article stays visible
and is filed in its Newsgroups; its target is withdrawn only for its author
or a covering authority, as for a cancel. Keystone
`fn-ctl-withdrawal-plan-record-is-bound`. Teeth: S of P's with `Supersedes:
<t@...>` withdraws T and stays visible; two Supersedes fields, or Supersedes
beside Control, name no target; an unverified superseder withdraws nothing.

## Step 1 (partial): the reader kernel, no caller yet

`books/control-served.lisp` (new). The view's withdrawn list W
(`fn-ctl-withdrawn-articles`), carried by `fn-ctl-refresh-withdrawn` (O(1) for
an ordinary visible arrival, one merge walk otherwise: O(N) per executed
cancel, the class of `fn-ctl-visible-add`'s pass), and the three answers.

| Theorem | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| `fn-ctl-withdrawn-is-the-complement` | for X in RAW: X in W iff X not served |
| `fn-ctl-refresh-withdrawn-is-withdrawn` | carried W was the merge, the new visible list is the view's, RAW a true list: the refresh's W is RAW's withdrawn list (no distinctness needed: identical articles share a status) |
| `fn-ctl-number-withdrawn-is-a-withdrawn-holder` (423 withdrawn) | a hit for (GROUP . N) in W is an article of RAW holding N that the view does not serve; converse `fn-ctl-withdrawn-holder-is-found-by-number` |
| `fn-ctl-msgid-withdrawn-is-a-withdrawn-article` (430 withdrawn) | the same by Message-ID; converse `fn-ctl-withdrawn-article-is-found-by-msgid` |
| `fn-ctl-executed-status-means-withdrawn` (HDR :fn-control) | C in RAW with status executed: the target is held in RAW, not served, C's record is in WS and the stated basis is its effect |
| `fn-ctl-owed-status-means-not-held` | owed: RAW holds no article with the target's Message-ID |
