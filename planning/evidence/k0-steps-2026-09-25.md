## PKT-087: the K4 node correspondence at the host's reopen

The host opens every process through `fn-cpo-open-observed`
(`host/store-node-host.lisp:180`, in `fn-store-sn-recover`;
`host/owner-host.lisp:178`, in `fn-owner-recover`). Its node is the
configured replay's: `fn-cpr-replay` interleaves the configuration journal
with the Store journal. The K4 node statements are stated over
`fn-sn-open-observed`, whose node is the fixed-table `fn-replay` under one
group table and capacity. `b5724fce` carried the kernel conjuncts and left
the node conjunct, `fn-snt-relation`, and the BP receipt theorem.

### What is proved (`books/store-open-node-bridge.lisp`)

- **Keystone, `fn-sonb-configured-replay-is-fixed-table-replay`.** If every
  configuration record names txid 0 (`fn-sonb-configured-before-eventsp`, a
  true list whose every record has `fn-cfg-record-txid` 0) and
  `fn-cpr-replay configs events` answers `:ok` with configured node `cn`, then
  `fn-replay` under `cn`'s allocation domain and capacity is `fn-replay-okp`,
  and its node is `(fn-cnode-node cn)`. A fresh store's initial record is at
  txid 0 (`host/config-host.lisp` `fn-cfg-host-initial-octets`), so the
  hypothesis means the store was configured before its first Store event
  and never reconfigured after one. It stands on three lemmas:
  - `fn-sonb-config-step-keeps-initial-cnode`: applying a record at txid 0
    to an initial configured node gives the initial node of the new
    configuration. `fn-cnode-extend-nexts` of initial watermarks is the
    initial watermark list.
  - `fn-sonb-cpr-events-loop-is-replay-loop`: with no configuration left,
    each `fn-cpr-loop` event step is the `fn-replay-loop` step on the same
    node. The served-group check only adds refusals.
  - `fn-sonb-cpr-loop-is-fixed-table-replay-loop`: the loop-level
    composition, by induction over the configuration list.
- `fn-sonb-configured-node-is-fixed-table-node`: from the keystone,
  `fn-cst-replay-node configs events frontier` equals
  `fn-sf-replay-node` under the final domain and capacity.
- **At the host's call** (each over `fn-cpo-open-observed`):
  - `fn-sn-open-observed-success-configured-node-of-host-open`: the node
    conjunct with no reconfiguration hypothesis. The opened node is
    `fn-cst-replay-node configs events frontier`.
  - `fn-sn-open-observed-success-exact-history-node-of-host-open`: the node
    conjunct of `fn-sn-open-observed-success-exact-history`, against
    `fn-sf-replay-node` under the opened state's own groups and capacity.
    Hypotheses: `fn-sonb-configured-before-eventsp` and host success.
  - `fn-sn-open-observed-success-has-live-history-relation-of-host-open`:
    `fn-snt-relation` of the host's opened state, under the same two
    hypotheses. So `fn-snrt-mixed-trace-preserves-live-history-relation` and
    its consequences hold from the state the host starts in.
  - `fn-bprv-observed-reopen-facts-of-host-open` and
    `fn-bpr-live-receipt-regenerated-after-restart-of-host-open`: the
    receipt theorem with the restart reopening through
    `fn-cpo-open-observed`. It adds two hypotheses,
    `fn-sob-configured-openp` and `fn-sonb-configured-before-eventsp`. The
    argument is factored as `fn-sonb-live-receipt-regenerated-from-related-open`,
    which holds for any opened Store where the relation holds and whose
    history extends the crashed one. That factoring took the proof from
    2.9 s to 0.01 s.

### What is not claimed

The correspondence is not claimed for a store reconfigured after an event,
and for some such stores it is false.
`tests/acl2/store-open-node-bridge-tests` (following `config-observed-tests`)
has one: an undertaking of 10, its release, and a capacity reduction to 1 at
txid 7. The host opens this store, but the fixed-table replay under the
final capacity 1 refuses the undertaking. So the correspondence, the node
conjunct and `fn-snt-relation` all fail there. For a reconfigured store the
host-side relation is `fn-cst-relation`
(`fn-cst-open-success-has-historical-relation`, `books/config-store-traces`),
and the BP receipt theorem is not stated over it. That is open. The
hypothesis is sufficient, not necessary: a reconfiguration that only adds
groups or raises capacity would probably keep the correspondence (not
proved). Whether the deployed node's configuration journal satisfies it was
not checked.

### Teeth (`tests/acl2/store-open-node-bridge-tests.lisp`)

- Witness: the host's open of a crash image of the K5 fixture's second
  publication (two records, frontier 2), under a fresh store's one initial
  record. Every hypothesis and conclusion of the keystone and of the three
  host statements holds. The witness is non-degenerate: the node is not the
  initial node.
- `fn-sonb-configured-before-eventsp`: the release-then-shrink history.
  The configured replay succeeds and the host opens. There are three
  `must-fail`s: the correspondence, the node conjunct and `fn-snt-relation`.
- Configured-replay success (the keystone) and host success
  (`fn-snt-relation`): two records at txid 0, both at sequence 0. The replay
  answers `:fault`, and there are two `must-fail`s.
- Host success for `-configured-node-of-host-open`: the empty journals. The
  open is refused (`:history`), and the configured replay's node is the
  initial node. One `must-fail`.
- No `must-fail`: host success in
  `-exact-history-node-of-host-open`. A refused open's state is its
  reason. Its node, groups and capacity are all nil, and `fn-sf-replay-node`
  under a nil capacity is nil, so the conclusion holds without the
  hypothesis. The test book asserts this instance. The receipt corollary
  has no ground witness. The fixed-table keystone
  `fn-bpr-live-receipt-regenerated-after-restart` has none in the tree
  either.
