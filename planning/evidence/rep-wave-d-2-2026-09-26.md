`store checkpoint` completes on the N = 10,000 × 32 KiB store where the

## 1. The interface finding: the freeze as briefed is not closed

The brief's (i): `fn-record-payloadp` → `natp` with the handle bound in
`fn-own-relation`; the prepare seals from the buffer; the duplicate
decision over the arena; boundary 7 over the arena; the recovery decode
sealing. The wave-d census counted the direct readers of
`(fn-record-payload …)` and classified the store node's as "no function
here reads bytes". Read again from the code, the *transition* and the
*served machine* read payload bytes, and the acceptance state carries a
second payload field:

| Byte reader inside a transition or machine | Site | What it reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fn-sn-finish`, every article completion | `books/store-node.lisp` `fn-sn-finish`, the `fn-stx-verdict-of-octets` call | the finished record's payload, parsed for a signed statement; the verdict goes into `fn-sn-verdicts`. Replay re-runs it at open (`fn-replay-*`, `fn-cpr-replay`: 27 and 17 books name them). |
| `fn-sn-composite-delta` (finish-identity) | `books/store-node.lisp` | `(fn-stx-delta (fn-record-payload (fn-replay-composite-record event)) keyring)`; the composite's record is decoded from the event's octets (`books/replay.lisp` `fn-replay-composite-record`), so these bytes live in the event: arena-free under a codec that keeps the octet-list wire record. |

Consequences. The record's payload and the acceptance state's article and
pending payload become handles *together* (a list in either keeps the
sixteen bytes per octet). A handle in the record then forces one of two
things on `fn-sn-finish` (38 books name it), the replay, `fn-own-step` and
`fn-ocfg-step` (16 each) and the served machine (`fn-own-read` 24,
`fn-served-dispatch` 18, `fn-nntp-article-response` 12): the arena as a
read-only stobj formal through all of them (a free `fn-arena` on every
theorem statement over the transition, about a hundred books of moves), or
the byte-derived facts decided once and carried as parsed fields. And the
codec seam (`fn-record-encode`/`fn-record-decode-exact`; 37 books above the
seam name them) keeps its arity and its *exact* round trip only if its
domain stays the octet-list wire record: over handle records, round trip,
canonicality and injectivity hold only up to the arena (two handles with
equal bytes encode alike). None of this is a lane's call, and a lane that
started the 438-root recertification on the briefed interface would have
built a record the finish cannot complete.

## 2. The closed design, for PKT-293

Two views over one `fn-record-shapep` tuple and the same accessors:

- the **wire record**: today's `fn-record-p` domain (octet-list payload),
  the codec's domain, unchanged; every seam theorem keeps its statement;
- the **held record**: the stored and served record, payload = `natp`
  handle into `fn-arena`, plus the parsed fields the transition and the
  machine need, decided at intern from the bytes.

**Intern**, the one function that reads bytes to build a held record:
`(fn-arn-intern w keyring generation fn-arena) => (mv held fn-arena)` seals
`(fn-record-payload w)` (from the buffer at prepare through
`fn-arena-seal-buffer`; from the decoded wire record at replay through
`fn-arena-seal-list`) and computes from the bytes the statement verdict
(`fn-stx-verdict-of-octets`), the tombstone flag (`fn-rcl-tombstonep`), the
body line count (OVER) and the control target. The finish reads the held
record's verdict field instead of the bytes; the phase gate (no other
transaction between a prepare and its finish) makes keyring@prepare equal
keyring@finish, to be proved; replay interns each record as it applies it,
under the replayed keyring at that step, which is today's semantics.
**Materialize**, the abstraction: `(fn-arn-wire-record h fn-arena)` is the
held record with its payload replaced by `(fn-arena-payload
(fn-record-payload h) fn-arena)`. The freeze's theorems, each over the
host-called function: prepare, `(fn-arn-wire-record (fn-sn-article-record s
obs msgid (fn-arena-count fn-arena) …) (fn-arena-seal-buffer fn-octets
fn-arena)) = (fn-sn-article-record s obs msgid (fn-octets-list fn-octets)
…)`; the duplicate decision, `(fn-rclb-existing-action msgid fn-octets
groups s fn-arena) = (fn-rcl-existing-action msgid (fn-octets-list
fn-octets) groups s)` over the held article's bytes read by handle; the
codec (boundary 7), encode of the materialized record, and decode then
intern re-establishing `fn-arn-store-corr` (`fn-arn-store-corr-of-open`);
the served read, the reference effect `(:write-payload h kind)` whose wire
bytes equal the list model's stuffed block.

**Order:** intern with the parsed fields (the transition becomes
parametric in the payload) → the shape freeze (records-shape and
acceptance's article and pending → `natp`; 438 books include
records-shape and 407 acceptance; the 17 books that open
`fn-record-payloadp` and the 6 that use `(len (fn-record-payload …))` need
proof work, the rest recertify) → the codec and recovery over the arena →
the served reference effect. **Rejected:** `fn-arena` threaded through
`fn-sn-finish`, the replay and the served machine (a stobj formal on every
theorem over the transition); a union payload type (natp or octets:
distinct types); a list copy "for now".

## 3. What landed: `store checkpoint` over the octet buffer (PRF-133)

The cause was never the retained payload. `fn-scc-file-octets`
(`books/store-checkpoint-codec.lisp`) built the file as `fn-scc-renc`'s
reversed accumulator, its reversal, the chunks (`take`/`nthcdr`), the frames
(`append header chunk trailer`), their concatenation, and the host's byte
vector: five to six octet-list copies of a ~330 MB file at sixteen bytes
per octet, 33 GB at death.

`books/store-checkpoint-buffer.lisp` (prefix `fn-sccb-`, `docs/prefixes.md`):

| Theorem | Statement | Host line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fn-sccb-renc-is-program` | `(equal (fn-sccb-renc x n st) (append st (fn-scc-program x) (fn-scc-repeat (nfix n) op-cons)))`, no hypothesis: the program written forward into the buffer, tail-recursive on the cdr as `fn-scc-renc` is, so a history of any length costs no stack | inside `fn-sccb-plan` |
| `fn-sccb-treep-is-treep`, `fn-sccb-treep-encodes-octets` | `fn-sccb-treep` (every leaf's octets octets) is `fn-scc-treep`, and its program is an octet list | the plan's refusal |
| `fn-sccb-chunk-count-is-len-chunks`, `fn-sccb-plan-octets-of-frames` | the buffer's ranges are `fn-scc-chunks` of its value, and the frames over them, concatenated, are `fn-scc-frames` of those chunks | the plan's frames |
| **`fn-sccb-plan-is-file-octets`** (KEYSTONE) | `(implies (fn-sccb-treep c) (equal (fn-sccb-plan-octets (mv-nth 0 (fn-sccb-plan c seg st)) (mv-nth 1 (fn-sccb-plan c seg st))) (fn-scc-file-octets c seg)))`: the plan's octets over the buffer it leaves are the file octets the list codec specified | `host/store-node-host.lisp` `fn-store-sco-publish-plan` → `host/native/io.lisp` `fnn-command-state-checkpoint` (the `store checkpoint` verb), which assembles `fn-sccb-plan-octets` from the buffer's array (`fnn-plan-octets`) and writes it through the unchanged `fnn-state-checkpoint-write` |
| `fn-sccb-plan-refuses-what-the-codec-refuses`, `fn-sccb-plan-buffer-is-encode` | where the list codec answered `:unencodable` the plan does; the buffer left holds `fn-scc-encode` | the verb's refusal; a later reader by index |

Every executable function is guard-verified (asserted in the test book).
`fn-sccb-treep` is the honest encodability: `fn-scc-treep` admits a leaf or
string of any length, but a length is written as a digit count in one
octet, so a tree with a length needing 256 digits encoded, as a list, to a
list with a non-octet in it, which the host's byte vector could not hold (a
fault); the plan refuses it as `:unencodable`. Refused and faulted stay
distinct. The byte program `fn-bs-scp-program` and its cuts are as they were
(`native_program_check` green): the host's one write step now takes the
plan's octets. `+fnn-gc-nursery-octets+` sets the 64 MiB collection trigger
in `fnn-main`, so the operator verbs no longer replay a history under
SBCL's 1.6 GB default.

**Teeth** (`tests/acl2/store-checkpoint-buffer-tests.lisp`): the exec path
on a live local buffer, a ground checkpoint-shaped value (a string, the
count slot, a cons tree with an octets leaf, a keyword, a natural, a
negative, a character and nil, two octets leaves; program 56 octets) at
segment sizes 7 (eight frames), 0 and 1000 (one frame), 56 and 55 (a chunk
boundary at the end), and the empty value: the plan's octets against
`fn-scc-file-octets`, the buffer against `fn-scc-encode`, the segments read
back by `fn-scc-decode-segments`; the refusal of `(1/2)` on both sides.
Per hypothesis: the keystone without `fn-sccb-treep` (the plan's octets are
nil, the codec says `:unencodable`; `must-fail`); `fn-sccb-treep-encodes-octets`
without its hypothesis (the program of a rational carries nil, a ground
theorem by evaluation since the program is guarded); the writer lemmas
without `(true-listp fn-octets)` (an empty write on an improper value) and
without `(true-listp xs)` (a dotted tail); the encoder's equation on an
improper buffer value, which holds, the record that its hypothesis was
dropped after the weakened theorem was proved. Every `must-fail` carries
`:do-not-induct t`: the refutation is the witness, and a doomed search cost
the book 27 s before that.

What the rows say:

- **The publication no longer builds the file as lists.** At N = 10,000 ×
  32 KiB the verb completes in 154 s at a 22.5 GB maximum resident set,
  which is the full replay's own peak (22.5 GB at the control row above):
  the publication adds nothing measurable on top of the replayed history,
  where the list codec added five copies of the file and died at 32 GB.
  At the two smaller points the verb's maximum resident set halves (7.33 →
  3.11 GB, 5.37 → 2.74 GB) and the files are byte-for-byte the same size
  as the baseline image's (the keystone says the same octets; the reader
  opens them: the module's `checkpoint_open_equals_full_replay` on both
  images, and the two smaller reopens here).
- **The writer costs about 0.12 µs per octet through the buffer's export.**
  The N = 1,000 × 32 KiB verb is 8.8 s slower than the same-hour before
  (27.8 against 18.9 s) for a 70.9 M-octet file: one `fn-octets-append-octet`
  call per octet, a guarded, `:protect`ed abstract-stobj export, where
  the list codec consed. A bulk export (`fn-octets-append-list` with the
  concrete `fn-oct-write-list` as its `:exec`, one call per payload) is the
  fix; it is a change to `books/octets-stobj.lisp` and its closure, not
  made here. At N = 10,000 × 2 KiB the difference is within a second: the
  file is smaller and the replay dominates.
- **The file holds every payload twice.** 670 MB for 320 MB of payload:
  `fn-sco-freeze` carries the replayed store (its history with the
  payloads) and the event index (the records again). That is the checkpoint
  format as it is, not this lane's; it doubles what the reader must hold.
- **The node cannot reopen from the N = 10,000 × 32 KiB checkpoint it now
  publishes.** The owner's open reads the 670 MB file's segments as octet
  lists (`fnn-state-checkpoint-segments`, 10.7 GB of conses) and decodes
  the tree beside them (`fn-scc-decode-tree`, the two copies), and SBCL
  exhausts the 32,000 MB heap in `FNN-STATE-CHECKPOINT-LOAD` after 56 s: a
  fatal error, not a refusal, so the corrupt-checkpoint fallback to full
  replay does not run and the node is down until the operator removes
  `store-checkpoint.fnsc` (the full replay then opens in 164 s at 22.5 GB,
  the control row). Classification: implementation (representation), the
  reader side the baseline named as the second copy (§1.2 there), now
  decisive. **Until the decoder over the buffer lands, `store checkpoint`
  at this size produces a file the node cannot open**; the deployed scale
  profile's auto-publication at 2,048 records is far below it (a ~135 MB
  file at 32 KiB articles; the N = 10,000 × 2 KiB point, 52 MB, reopens),
  and no refusal is added, since a size threshold would be a constant that
  bounds data (D27). The decoder by index over the buffer is the same
  shape as this lane's writer and is the next increment of (iii).
- **The collection trigger has a cost on the open path.** The reopen from
  the checkpoint at N = 1,000 × 32 KiB is 12 s slower than the same-hour
  before (28.1 against 16.0 s; +2 s at N = 10,000 × 2 KiB) at the same
  residency: `fnn-main` now sets the 64 MiB nursery before the open, where
  the baseline's owner set it only in `fnn-owner-run`, after it, so the
  decode's 7 GB of allocation is collected a hundred times more often
  against 1.4 GB of live data. The trigger did what the brief asked of the
  offline verb (it is the verb's halved maximum resident set above), and
  the open path pays for it; setting it after the open, or a larger
  nursery during recovery, is a one-line choice for the coordinator, and
  the numbers here are the ones to choose with.
- Live heap after a full collection on the reopened owner is not
  measured (no profiling twin was built); VmRSS at LISTENING varies with
  where the collector was (5.01 GB against 2.73 GB at N = 10,000 × 2 KiB
  at the same VmHWM order), and is not a live-heap figure.

## 7. PKT-293, for ember: the freeze's shape

Trace: §1 (the transition and the served machine read payload bytes; the
acceptance state's second payload field), §2 (the two views, intern with
parsed fields, the order), the wave-d record §3 (the consumers) and PKT-167.
Constraint: a handle record needs either the arena on every transition
theorem or parsed fields decided at intern; the codec's exact round trip
survives only over the wire record. Default (recommended): §2's design and
order: intern with the parsed fields first (verdict, tombstone flag, body
line count, control target), the transition parametric in the payload; then
the shape freeze on both the record and the acceptance state; then the
codec and recovery over the arena; then the served reference effect. The
verdict decided at intern under keyring@prepare, with the phase-gate
theorem that it equals keyring@finish, and replay interning in order.
Rejected: `fn-arena` threaded through `fn-sn-finish`, the replay and the
served machine (about a hundred books of statement moves and a stobj formal
on every theorem over the transition); a union payload type; a list copy
"for now". Affected: the record's logical shape (not its wire encoding),
the acceptance state's article and pending, every store invariant that
says "the payload is an octet list", the P8/D21 books that state the
verdict is computed at finish, the checkpoint codec and every host entry
of the wave-d table. What continues without it: everything; the node runs
at sixteen bytes per octet, and now checkpoints at N = 10,000 × 32 KiB.

## 8. Not done, and why

- **The checkpoint reopen over the buffer, now urgent:** `fnn-state-checkpoint-segments`

  `fn-octets-append-list` export in `books/octets-stobj.lisp` with the
  concrete `fn-oct-write-list` as its `:exec`, one call per payload; the
