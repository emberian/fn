## What a reader now sees

An article page (tools/fn_web.py) shows five separate facts: the claimed
author; the authorship evidence carried; the node's historical verdict
(`HDR :fn-verified`, unchanged by any later key change); the node's current
enrollment of the verdict's principal (`HDR :fn-enrollment`, new, decided in
ACL2); and the reader's own independent verification with the reader's own
keyring (`--keyring FILE`, `tools/fn_verify.py check-article`), naming whose
keyring, or why it was not performed. In tin, `HDR :fn-enrollment <msgid>`
is one line of the node's grammar (specs/human-client.md "Reader metadata
lines", specs/peering.md section 8).
