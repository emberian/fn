## What this decided

- Client: `msgid_measure.Conn(buffered=True)` and `rep_measure.article` are reused as is. rep_measure.post raises SystemExit on any non-240 reply; the driver counts refusals by name (e.g. `440-posting-not-permitted-now-the`, MEM-011) and retries a refused POST command instead.
- Sampler: scale_curve's 20 ms RssAnon sampler is per-process-start; the driver samples VmRSS/RssAnon/VmHWM every 1 s from /proc/PID/status and takes smaps_rollup, /proc/PID/io and utime+stime at each phase edge.
- GC time: the production and developer images expose none; `tools/load/hooks/load-hook.lisp` is the mem7hook `*after-gc-hooks*` pattern (one line per GC: epoch, gc-run-time, dynamic usage), loaded before `acl2::sbcl-restart` by rewriting a copy of the launcher (mk.sh's method). The idle-GC-off arm (MEM-003 armA) is the same file under FN_LOAD_IDLE_GC=off.
