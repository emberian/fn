All on hbox (16 cores), under `SWARM_MEM_MAX=16G swarm-build`, tmpfs (/dev/shm), launched by `python3 -m tools.load.driver box`, one directory per run below (result.json, cells.jsonl, report.md). KiB figures are /proc VmRSS (N's "MB" in MEM-003 are the same KiB figures divided by 1000). Box load average 16 to 30 during every run (shared box), so nothing here is a latency judgement; memory rows are not load-sensitive.

## W2@10k (2026-10-07-w2-10k-d)

Developer image 0b4d3b183, filled preset (development with 100000 transactions, 256 MiB history), store of 10,000 x 2 KiB preloaded by POSTs (not from the stale fixture), heap decided by the image's probe (5917 MB), load 25 to 27: judged NOT-MEASURED (box loaded) by the rule; recorded ARTICLE p50/p99 ms R1 5.4/113, R4 68/231, R16 87/377, R64 99/398 at that load. The node admits about 30 connections: R64 had 34 connections refused with `400 too many connections` (counted by name). Rerun at load < 8 for the L-READ judgement.

## What the first W2 attempts found

The small preset cannot hold 10,000 articles (8 MiB history: 441 refusals after ~4000), `--profile development` takes 127 POSTs (max-transactions 128), and a decided heap must be re-probed after the store grows (a restart at the empty store's figure was refused: "the process heap does not hold the store's protected runtime"). The harness now has the `filled` and `scale-1m` presets, verifies a preload admitted every POST, and probes the heap at every start.
