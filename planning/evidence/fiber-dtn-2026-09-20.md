# Fiber dtn: v0.3, queued work to receipt across a real outage

What this strand of v0 has proved, what it has only tested, the pessimistic
numbers with the scope they cover, and what is open. Every number here is
cited to the file it came from; nothing is retyped from memory. Written by
the w9/release lane against `dev` at `52eb0db`.


## What is proved

- **The BP step preserves the node**: `fn-bp-step-preserves-node` and
  `fn-bp-trace-preserves-node`, with the bp-receiver invariants
  (`planning/requirements.json` REP-006 note).
- **Receipts distinguish transmission, retention and delivery** (RET-003,
  `implemented`, evidence `tests/evidence/2026-09-18-bp-composition-assurance.md`).
- **Durable scheduling with resource and fairness policy** (REP-005,
  `implemented`, evidence `tests/evidence/2026-09-18-bp-workflow.md`). All three
  scheduler roots certify; the `bdd59d2` gate reached them
  (`planning/deputies/BOARD.md`, w5/registries correction).
- **Exchange portable facts, not local identifiers** (REP-001) and
  **duplicate/reordered ingestion converges** (REP-002), both `implemented`.

## What is tested and not proved

- The bp workflow bridges and the receipt journal run under
  `tools/run_bp_ingress.py`, `tools/run_bp_receive.py` and
  `tools/receipt_journal.py`; their cases are in the Python suite counted in
  `planning/evidence/verdict-52eb0db-2026-09-20.md`.

## Open, with the evidence that says so

- **REP-006 names BP *and LTP*, and LTP is a feasibility study**
  (`planning/ltp-feasibility.md`), not an implementation. The requirement stays
  `specified` for that half alone; the BP half has its keystones
  (`planning/requirements.json` REP-006 note).
- **`fn-sched-conditional-progress-under-a-fairness` is conditional on a
  constrained assumption**, and both aging keystones were false as stated until
  `79e5227` (`planning/deputies/BOARD.md`, w5/registries).
- **`host/scheduler-host.lisp` builds a `:bad` decision record.**
  `fn-sched-host-decision-octets` (lines 82 to 85) hands `fn-sched-decision`
  string literals where the codec wants octets
  (`planning/deputies/BOARD.md`, w3-scheduler proof lane).
- **REP-004 (useful transfer without interactive inventory discovery)**: PRF-018
  has no events; inventories are not implemented.
- **The v0.3 gate condition -- an interrupted relay and contact-plan run with
  expiry and staging exhaustion -- has no evidence file.** The w9/dtn-e2e lane
  owns it.

## Pessimistic numbers, each with its scope

- None measured for this fiber. The scale gate measures the store and the

## Rules this record follows

- A keystone is cited only where a root that reaches it certified in a farm
  gate whose manifest is named above. A theorem admitted in a lane worktree
  and never gated is listed as open, not as proved.
- A pessimistic number carries its scope in the same sentence, and the scope
  names the host, the load and the shape of the input.
- A passing test is not a proof and a certificate is not an audit. Where the
  two disagree about a claim, the disagreement is written down rather than
  resolved in favour of the greener one.
