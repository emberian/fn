## The image

A dev-matching image could not be built from `6c0626c5` in scratch:
`proof_artifacts acquire --profile default` found no complete current
artifact set in `/tank/fn/certcache` for that source (the p2-wire, p6 and
p3-p5 merges changed books after the last cache fill), and certifying is a
farm run this lane was told not to make. The run therefore used the p2-wire
lane's developer image, the newest one carrying the W1 to W3 wire fixes
(refusal reasons on the wire), copied read-only with its source tree:

- source: p2-wire tree `REV` `b4dc77d7` (merged to dev as `4d1f6c79`),
  copied from `/tank/fn/scratch/p2-wire/tree` to `/tank/fn/scratch/m6-web/tree`
- `build/fn-host-developer.core` SHA-256
  `09ed86fa10c6998a20ff37caed446ab74d3fa93256e0ce9c20ac26898ce9f65a`
  (identical to p2-wire's `image.sha256`); the launcher was repointed at the
  copy's core, launcher SHA-256
  `5e1df5bb9b207b22ae7eefbf34b6b10b8b48cf7e7d6c88eb334794d263306ebb`
  (p2-wire's original `50092191...`)
- toolchain: w28 ACL2 `/tank/fn/toolchains/w28/acl2-literal-4g`,
  `FN_OPENSSL_PREFIX=/tank/fn/toolchains/openssl-3.5.8`, Python 3.12.7

The native image needs `FN_OPENSSL_PREFIX` in the environment of the process
that launches it: without it `store init` did not finish in 180 s.

What the four cases observed on the image:

- `test_login_over_tls_is_the_nodes_answer_and_the_command_line_exits_by_it`:
  an operator-run owner with `tls_cert`/`tls_key`, `[auth] required = true`,
  `protected_only = true` and one principal enrolled through `bin/fn principal
  set-password --posting`. The login check reached `281` over `TLSv1.x`; a
  wrong password was `refused` with the node's `481`; a certificate that is
  not the node's was `refused` before anything followed `382`. The one command
  (`fn_web.py --node ... --tls-cert ... --user ...`) exited 1 on the wrong
  password (stderr `refused 481 ...`, the password absent from both streams),
  3 on an unreachable port, 2 with no password and no terminal, and on the
  right password printed its identity banner and served the group list.
- `test_thread_reply_outcomes_verdict_unread_and_resume`, same owner: two
  posts and two nested replies through the pages; the reply form carried
  `Re: Thread root` and References `<root>` then `<root> <reply>`, and the
  stored article read back over NNTP carried them; the group page ordered
  root, reply, reply-to-reply, unrelated at indents 0/18/36/0 px. A post with
  `From: not a mailbox` was refused and the page showed the node's line
  `441 posting failed; From is not a valid mailbox list`, offered "Edit as a
  new post", and the node's high-water mark did not move. The node reported
  `absent no-field` for the unsigned posts, shown as the `absent` badge on the
  article and each row. Unread counts fell by one after opening one article,
  persisted to the marks file, and "Mark read through" cleared them across a
  web-client restart. Resume from (fn.agents, 1, root id) redirected to
  `start=2&end=41`; with a different Message-ID it answered 409 "not the same
  article" naming the id the node serves there; at the last article it said
  "Nothing newer". No page carried the password.
- `test_durable_draft_lost_post_reply_and_restart_keep_one_native_article`
  (existing, extended): with `FN_NATIVE_POST_FAULT=record-attempted:kill` the
  POST result is `uncertain`, the page says "Do not repost", shows the exact
  body, offers no edit, and `/compose?edit=` for that submission is refused
  (400); the rest of the existing case (one article in the store, no second
  POST after restart) still holds.
- `test_web_post_and_thread_read_use_the_native_owner` (existing): unchanged.

The fake-node module `tests/test_fn_web.py` (29 cases, 4 new for thread

## What this does not claim

- Not run against `/tank/fn/node` or with real credentials; that is the
  coordinator's after deploy.
- Not on a dev-`6c0626c5` image; on p2-wire's `b4dc77d7` image. The client
  touches only NNTP, and the protocol lines it relies on (`281`, `441` with a
  reason, `HDR :fn-verified` ranges, `OVER N`) are those of that image.
- No `verified` badge was observed on the image: the client does not sign, so
  every post it makes is `absent`. The `verified`/`unverified` rendering is
  covered only by the fake node.
- Uncertain was observed only via the modelled `record-attempted` kill on a
  plain owner, not on the TLS owner.
