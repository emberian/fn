B's local `consumer poll` instead returned a 30,338-byte legacy `fn-r` record, not the carried kind-4 `fn-e` event required by native `consumer-project` and Mini's observed-poll atom. Thus independent signature verification of fetched carrier is present, but B has no acceptance-time persisted historical verdict to bind to its consumer event. A Python interpretation or locally reconstructed `fn-e` would not establish the required Store provenance.
