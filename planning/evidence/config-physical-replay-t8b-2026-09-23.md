Configuration records carry the next unconsumed Store transaction ID. `fn-cpr-replay` merges those records at recovery by transaction ID, placing configuration before a Store event on a tie and ordering tied configuration records by their own sequence. It checks the configuration at the reservation total produced by the preceding Store events. Its successful-result theorem, `fn-cpr-replay-ok-is-configured`, establishes `fn-cnode-statep` of the result; it does not yet establish native recovery correspondence or live owner adoption.
