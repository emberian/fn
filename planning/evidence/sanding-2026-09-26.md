  re-send that meets the gate (`440` after the login lost its posting

## The proof (PRF-162)

`books/served-implicit-tls.lisp`, teeth `tests/acl2/served-implicit-tls-tests.lisp`:

- **`fn-served-implicit-tls-is-the-starttls-session`** (keystone). With
  `c0` the connection `fn-served-open-group-indexed` makes (what
  `fn-own-open` calls for the host's `fn-owner-open`), under
  `(fn-auth-configp acfg)` and `(fn-auth-config-tls-availablep acfg)`:
  STARTTLS dispatched on `c0` emits the `(:starttls)` handshake effect, and
  `fn-served-dispatch c0 (:tls-established)` has the same connection as
  `fn-served-dispatch` of `(:tls-established)` on the connection after
  STARTTLS. The equality alone holds without either hypothesis (the event
  sets the layer whatever preceded it); the hypotheses buy the handshake
  conjunct, i.e. that the STARTTLS path reaches the event at all.
- **`fn-served-implicit-tls-session-is-protected`**: the event on the opened
  connection emits nothing, records the TLS layer, owes no handshake.
- **`fn-native-operator-implicit-tls-listener-needs-its-certificate`**
  (books/native-operator.lisp, keystone): `fn-native-operator-result-run-implicit-tls-port`
  is non-nil only for an accepted `run` plan that is not `--once`, with
  certificate and key octets present, in 1..65535 and not the plaintext
  port. The loader admits `tls_port` only beside `tls_cert`/`tls_key` and on
  another port (`fn-ncfg-tls-port-okp`); `native-config-show`'s round trip
  (PRF-094) carries the new field.
- **`fn-native-operator-inspect-report-is-the-lookup`** (keystone): exit 0
  and `:accepted` exactly when the lookup is true, exit 1 and `:absent`
  otherwise, and the printed line is the verdict's.

Host lines. Implicit TLS: `host/native/owner.lisp` `fnn-owner-run` binds the
second listener when `fn-native-operator-host-result-run-implicit-tls-port`
(host/native/operator.lisp, passed through `fnn-control-owner-run-normalized`)
is non-nil and a TLS context was loaded; `fnn-owner-start-tls-accept` runs
its accept thread (a worker, joined at stop); `fnn-owner-serve-client` in
implicit mode completes `fnn-tls-accept` BEFORE `fn-owner-open` (a failed
handshake costs no connection and sends no greeting), then calls
`fn-owner-tls-established` (host/owner-host.lisp -> `fn-ocfg-read-step` ->
`fn-own-read-step` -> `fn-served-dispatch`), then sends the greeting over
TLS. Inspect: `fnn-operator-execute-inspect` (host/native/operator.lisp)
opens the store as `recover` does, asks `fnn-bridge-lookup-found-p`
(`fn-store-sn-lookup-foundp`) and prints `fn-native-operator-host-inspect-report`'s line.

Assurance chain (implicit TLS): native entry `fnn-owner-serve-client`
(implicit) -> executed ACL2 subject `fn-served-dispatch` over the connection
`fn-own-open` pinned -> no representation change (the event is a wire event
on the same served connection) -> maintained relation: the owner's
connection invariants (`fn-own-conn-boundedp`, checked by `fn-own-read-step`
for every event, as for STARTTLS) -> behavioural theorem
`fn-served-implicit-tls-is-the-starttls-session`, after which both
listeners' connections are one state under one `fn-served-step` ->
observed: SCN-092 below (no `STARTTLS` label, `381`, `502`) and tin.

Teeth. served-implicit-tls-tests: reachable witness under `[auth] required,
protected_only, TLS available` (both hypotheses asserted true, the
equality, the `(:starttls)` effect, then `502` to STARTTLS and `381`, not
`483`, to AUTHINFO USER on the common state, and `483` on the opened
connection before the event); hypothesis removal: TLS not available (config
still well formed; STARTTLS answers `580`, no handshake effect) and a
malformed policy (`tls-availablep` reads t; `configp` fails; `580`), each
with a `must-fail` of the keystone restated without that hypothesis;
protected witness. native-operator-tests: the implicit port 1563 with every
conjunct evaluated, `--once` and `status` give none, the theorem without
its hypothesis `must-fail`s, `tls_port` without a certificate is `:usage`;
`store inspect` parse (accepted, `:inspect` action, needs a store, msgid
octets; a word without brackets and a missing word are `:usage`), both
report lines, and a `must-fail` that the verdict ignores the lookup.
native-config(-show)-tests: tls_port accepted and rendered back; refused
without a certificate, on the plaintext port, and as 0.

## Not done, and why

- `store inspect` needs the owner stopped (the store's exclusive lock). A
  live answer through the owner's control socket is PKT-410.
- The inspect answer does not compare the stored article with the client's
  copy: `accepted` says a Message-ID is bound, not that it is the client's
  text. The comparison is D25's re-send, which the gate refuses here.
- tin's wire is not logged over TLS (the plaintext relay of reader-daily
  cannot sit inside TLS); the evidence is tin's screens, the owner's log
  lines and the read-back.
- RFC 8143 (which prefers implicit TLS) is not among the supplied RFCs;
  the listener is cited as a local policy over RFC 4642 section 1.

## Packets

- **PKT-409 — an implicit-TLS port in `mission`.** Trace: `mission` writes
  `tls_cert`/`tls_key` but no `tls_port`, so a mission node serves tin only
  after a hand edit. Default: `mission ... --tls-port P` writing the key.
  Rejected: a fixed default port (563 needs privilege; a guess collides).
  Affected: books/native-config-show.lisp mission rows. Continues without it.
- **PKT-410 — a live settling lookup.** Trace: `store inspect` is refused
  while the owner runs. Default: a control-socket request answered by the
  owner from its view (`fn-store-sn-lookup-foundp` over the committed
  state), same report function. Rejected: an unlocked store read (two
  readers of one journal). Affected: books/native-control.lisp frame codec.
- **PKT-411 — tin on a host without a domain.** Trace: above. Default:
  document it (done); no node change. The node is not at fault.
