**Frontier format 3 (PKT-271).** `fn-bs-frontier-encode-impl` writes the
narrow CBOR form for every `n <= *fn-cbor-max-uint*` and the wide form only
above 2^32-1.
- Until a store passes txid 2^32-1, the frontier bytes are the ones bbf52159
  reads. The rehearsal observed exactly that.
- So "after the first write the pre-upgrade snapshot is the only rollback"
  does not hold for this image. The binary rollback is lossless.
- docs/operator.md does not mention frontier formats. It needs no sentence
  unless a node nears 2^32 transactions, when the wide frame begins.
- Recommendation for PKT-271: close it as "no rollback consequence below
  2^32-1".

## PKT-321: the owner's fairness under a mixed load (M1)

- **Trace.** The mixed hour (`mixed.json.gz`) ran 3 tight-loop readers and 1
  POST every 0.5 s. From t = 384 s, control-socket requests queued behind the
  served path past the client's 10 s deadline, and each answered uncertain.
  Served reads were fast at the median, with minute-long outliers. Commits
  thinned to gaps of up to 212 s in the last 25 minutes.
- **Constraints.** Every outcome stayed classified: uncertain is never
  accepted, and the consumer and hybrid-author retries settle as duplicates.
  So the defect is availability and fairness, not correctness.
- **Default.** Deploy on the live node's present load. Before any claim of a
  mixed-workload envelope:
  - measure the owner's mutex hold times per request kind (mandate §15:
    mutex-held work, tail latency);
  - bound each served step's work, and give control requests a share;
  - rerun this hour against bbf52159 as the matched baseline, which was not
    run here.
- **Rejected alternative:** raising the control client's 10 s deadline. It
  hides the queueing and makes each uncertain answer slower.
- **Affected:** host/native/owner.lisp's scheduling of connections and the
  control socket. No format and no proof is changed.
- **What continues without it:** everything in this record. The limit shows
  only under sustained saturating reads.
