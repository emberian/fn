# Control C2 and C3: evidence (2026-09-25)

Packets C2 and C3 of [the control-message design](../design-2026-09-25-control-messages.md)
under D29 ([decisions](../decisions.md), 2026-09-25; the reviewer's precision in
[review-2026-09-25-gpt6-decisions.md §1](../review-2026-09-25-gpt6-decisions.md)).
Requirement NNT-011, proof target PRF-084, scenario SCN-044; the
specification is [peering §8](../../specs/peering.md#8-control-messages-filing-and-authority-implemented-cancel-decided-served-withdrawal-open-group-control-deferred).
Lane `lane/control-c2c3` from `dev` `c8e1e9c1`.

**What is done and what is not, in one place.** C2 is implemented end to
end: the slot, the delta kinds, the codec, replay, admission, the operator
verbs on the live path, and `fn-ctl-authorize`. C3's *decision* is
implemented and proved: the withdrawal record, its effect on the target,
the visible article list, the equivalence, the pinned-view and early-cancel
clauses, and the journal derivation that a later revoke cannot rewrite. C3's
*served withdrawal is not*: no reader port applies the filter, the host
never calls `fn-ctl-authorize` or `fn-ctl-cancel-plan`, a signed control
article is still refused `:control-signed` (so no cancel is ever verified),
and Supersedes is untouched. Nothing this lane added withdraws an article
on a running node. NNT-011 and SCN-044 therefore stay `specified` and
PRF-084 `in-progress`; by the rule "the theorem subject is the function the
host calls", the C3 keystones do not yet count for the requirement.

## C2: the slot and the verbs

- `books/config.lisp`: the value's eighth slot `fn-cfg-authorities` (:389);
  `fn-cfg-valuep` requires it to be a row list; every `fn-cfg-value-make`
  carries it through. Delta kinds `:grant-control` 11 and `:revoke-control`
  12 (:620-621 and the inverse table); constructors `fn-cfg-grant-control`
  (:762) and `fn-cfg-revoke-control`; `fn-cfg-principal-hexp` (:714),
  `fn-cfg-namespace-patternp` (:731); apply arms (:866, :874): a grant
  upserts the row `(NS P VERB 0)` on (NS, P), a revoke removes the pair;
  admission arms in `fn-cfg-delta-reason` (:947, :960). The configuration
  record codec is generic over kind codes, so the two kinds round-trip with
  no codec change (`fn-cfg-code-kind`/`fn-cfg-kind-code` extended).
- `books/config-invariants.lisp`: `fn-cfg-apply-delta-preserves-valuep`
  re-proved over the two new arms (two local lemmas).
- `books/native-admin.lisp`: `fn-native-admin-control-plan` (:127) for
  `control grant|revoke PRINCIPAL VERB NAMESPACE-PATTERN` and `control list`;
  a reserved namespace is refused `:reserved-group-name`, then
  `:namespace-pattern`, `:principal`, `:verb-not-grantable`;
  `fn-native-admin-plan-deltas` (:260) builds the deltas; `control list` is a
  query (`fn-native-admin-result-queryp`) rendered by
  `fn-native-admin-control-report` (:368) through
  `fn-native-admin-query-report` (:382).
- Host: `host/native-admin-host.lisp` `fn-native-admin-host-owner-reconfigure`
  hands `fn-native-admin-plan-deltas` to `fn-owner-reconfigure-deltas` (the
  live arm, unchanged), the offline arm routes the two kinds through
  `fn-store-cfg-peer-delta-record` (:43), and `fn-native-admin-host-query-report`
  (:24) is what `fnn-admin-query` (`host/native/admin.lisp:206`) now calls.

The two reconfiguration headlines,
`fn-ocl-no-reader-observes-a-half-change` and
`fn-ocl-crash-at-any-instant-recovers-the-live-generation`
(`books/config-crash-replay.lisp`, `books/config-owner-publish.lisp` and
their closure), are universally quantified over delta lists; they were
recertified over the eight-slot value and the two new kinds (manifests
below). No new crash cut: a grant is one configuration record.

Teeth (`tests/acl2/control-authority-tests.lisp`): per keystone a reachable
witness asserting the antecedent and the conclusion, and per hypothesis a
removal witness checking the retained hypotheses, the omitted one's failure
and the conclusion's failure: the carried verdict (declined `:carried`);
the cross-post outside the grant (`:outside-namespace`, no covering row);
the wrong principal (`:no-grant`; neither disjunct holds); a revoke at txid
3 before the cancel at 5 (the record changes) against one at 9 (it does
not); the view pinned before the cancel (the target served) against the
advanced view; the early cancel (the first view with both serves neither
the target nor anything but C and the other article); T-then-C against
C-then-T. The operator plan witnesses (`control grant|revoke` deltas; a
reserved namespace refused by name) are in the same book.

The requested "recovery between the two arrivals" tooth is the pinned-view
and journal pair: records are a function of the journals alone, so a view
built after a restart between C and T holds C's record and serves T never.
There is no native kill witness, because nothing on a running node applies
the record yet.

## Findings

1. **The served withdrawal is not a lane-sized change.** The committed view's
   archive is pinned by `fn-own-relation` (`books/owner-invariants.lisp`
   :135-178) to `fn-own-prefix-archive`, the acceptance projection of the
   replay; K1 and 27 textual uses across 8 owner books depend on it. Filtering
   at `fn-own-refresh` needs the record list carried in owner state (or a
   ninth view field), the prefix-archive restated with
   `fn-ctl-visible-articles`, the grants at each cancel's txid available to
   the relation (`fn-sn-config-history` is in the store), and
   `fn-midx-refresh`'s incremental premise relaxed when a previously visible
   article disappears. The spike's route (filtering in the host at install)
   would leave K1 false of the running owner; it is not taken.
2. **`:control-signed` blocks every verified cancel.** C1's open item is
   C3's first prerequisite: `books/hybrid-store.lisp` binds a signed
   article's record to its source's `Newsgroups`; the binding must name the
   filing group, with the hybrid-store invariants and replay recertified.
3. **The decided record is a derivation, not a Store record.** The design
   asked for a withdrawal record kind in a composite with the cancel's
   acceptance. This lane binds the decision to durable inputs instead:
   `fn-ctl-journal-withdrawals` is a pure function of the configuration
   journal and the accepted cancels (txid, Message-ID, stored verdict,
   classification), decided under the configuration in force at the cancel's
   txid. Nothing new is written, so there is no new crash cut; the cost is a
   recovery fold over the cancels, and that the record's content is only as
   durable as the verdict pairs (historical, never rewritten).
4. The live arm reports a refused reconfiguration (the second revoke,
   `:no-such-grant`) without the reason; the offline arm and the plan name
   theirs. Naming it on the live reply is a small follow-up.
5. D27: every function in `books/control-authority.lisp` is over strings,
   lists of rows and article records; `fn-ctl-visible-articles` walks the
   record list per article (records times articles). When the filter lands
   at `fn-own-refresh` it must be incremental in the new records, like
   `fn-midx-refresh`, or it becomes whole-archive work per refresh.
6. `tests/test_native_operator_verbs.py` has one failure and one error on
   `dev` before this lane (a `peer-report` defun it looks for in
   `native-admin.lisp`, and a non-ASCII byte in `native-control.lisp`); this
   lane changed only the query-bridge assertion.
