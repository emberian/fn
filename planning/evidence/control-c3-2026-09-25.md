## Step 2: the signed record binding names the filing group

`books/hybrid-store.lisp` `fn-hsig-source-filed-groups SOURCE FIELDS`: the
filing group `(fn-ctl-filing-group VERB)` when the exact signed source
classifies `(:control VERB ARGS)`, `:malformed` (no group list) when it
classifies `:malformed`, and the source's `Newsgroups` otherwise. It replaces
the three `(equal groups (cadr fields))` tests: the legacy constructor
`fn-hsig-authorized-submission-event`, the native constructor
`fn-hsig-authorized-carried-submission-event-base`, and the replay binding
`fn-hsig-carried-record-metadatap` (a conjunct of
`fn-hsig-article-event-snapshot-bindsp-v1` and
`fn-hsig-article-event-carried-bindsp`, which `books/replay.lisp`
`fn-replay-identity-step` calls at :391 and :401). The D23 carried
constructor `fn-pa-carried-event` (`books/peer-authored-accept.lisp`) binds
the same way.

`books/peer-authored-accept.lisp` `fn-pa-filing-plan` no longer refuses a
carrier-bearing control article `:control-signed`; it files it in its
filing group or refuses `:control-not-filed`, as for an unsigned one.
`fn-ctl-control-article-is-filed-only-in-control` lost its `:control-signed`
disjunct; `fn-ctl-unsigned-control-filing-by-definition` became
`fn-ctl-control-filing-by-definition` without the carrier hypothesis.

Host: `host/native/hybrid-control.lisp` `fnn-hybrid-control-author` (the
local signed-author path) now takes the filing plan's groups instead of
faulting when they differ from `Newsgroups`; `host/native/owner.lisp`
`fnn-owner-attempt-transit` already passed the filed groups to the signed
constructors (served POST, bound submission, NNTP and BP transit).

| Theorem (books/hybrid-store.lisp) | Statement |

| `fn-hsig-signed-control-record-lists-its-filing-group` | `fn-hsig-carried-record-metadatap SOURCE RECEIVED RECORD` and SOURCE classified `(:control VERB ARGS)` imply `(fn-record-groups RECORD) = (list (fn-ctl-filing-group VERB))` |

Teeth (`tests/acl2/hybrid-store-tests.lisp`, end): a real carrier over a

## Step 1 kernel: the visible list, incrementally (books/control-visible.lisp)

`fn-ctl-visible-add A OLD-VISIBLE OLD WS VERDICTS` (one new article) and
`fn-ctl-visible-extend DELTA OLD-VISIBLE OLD WS VERDICTS` (a batch, newest
first, the shape of the acceptance archive between two refreshes).

| Theorem | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| `fn-ctl-visible-add-is-visible` | `OLD-VISIBLE = (fn-ctl-visible-articles OLD WS VERDICTS)` implies `(fn-ctl-visible-add A OLD-VISIBLE OLD WS VERDICTS) = (fn-ctl-visible-articles (cons A OLD) WS VERDICTS)` |
| `fn-ctl-visible-extend-is-visible` | the same hypothesis implies `(fn-ctl-visible-extend DELTA ...) = (fn-ctl-visible-articles (append DELTA OLD) WS VERDICTS)` |

Work: an ordinary article costs one pass over the records WS (is it a cause,
is it a target) and no pass over the archive; a target costs a probe of the
article list per record naming it; an executed cancel costs one pass over the
visible list of N articles times |WS|. Pessimistic figure: N x |WS| per
executed cancel, |WS| per other article. The cause probe is a list walk in
the kernel; the owner wiring is to answer it from the Message-ID trie. No
caller yet: `fn-own-refresh` does not call it.

Teeth (`tests/acl2/control-visible-tests.lisp`): the cancel after its
targets (C over O A T drops T by authority and A by author), the target
after its cancel (T over C O is never added), an ordinary article (consed,
`fn-ctl-causes-p` false), a batch from the empty archive; for each keystone's
one hypothesis a carried list that is not the definition's (the raw archive
still holding T; a stray O) is not repaired, and the conclusion fails.

## What the owner wiring needs (the continuation's brief)

1. The view and every connection serve `(fn-ctl-visible-state A WS V)`: the
   acceptance state with its article list filtered (numbers and nexts
   unchanged, so no number is reused and statep is kept: a sublist).
2. WS as a function of durable inputs over the pinned prefix: the cancels
   are articles of A (filed in `control.cancel`), their verdicts are in V,
   and the grants come from the configuration in force at each cancel's
   txid (`fn-ctl-config-at`, the store's configuration journal). K1 then
   reads: the conn archive equals the visible state of the prefix archive
   under WS(prefix, V, configs). Two facts the relation does not carry today
   are needed: the pinned verdicts V are the store's verdicts at the pin
   (the view's `verdicts` field is unconstrained by `fn-own-view-okp`), and
   configuration records appended later carry txids above every pinned
   cancel's (so `fn-ctl-configs-through-of-later-append` keeps WS fixed).
3. `fn-own-refresh`: `fn-ctl-visible-extend` over the acceptance delta (the
   new articles consed since the view), falling back to the full definition
   only on a discontinuous archive (start, reopen), like `fn-midx-refresh`;
   the Message-ID trie and group buckets built over the visible list (the
   trie extends on an ordinary article and rebuilds on an executed cancel).
   A lemma that records caused by new articles leave the old visible list
   unchanged (the list form of `fn-ctl-pinned-view-keeps-its-archive`).
4. The 27 uses of `fn-own-prefix-archive` across 8 owner books keep their
   text if the restatement is a new function around it; the growth lemmas
   need the two facts of item 2.
Spike/control's host-side filter is the negative example (deputy).

## Findings
1. The owner wiring is not lane-sized with a three-run budget: it changes the
   relation's content (items 2 and 4 above), not only its text.
2. The binding classifies the signed source and the filing plan the received
   carrier; where they disagree the constructors build no event (refused
   `:event`), never a misfiled record. A proof that the carrier render
   preserves the Control field would close that gap.
3. `books/bp-native-app.lisp` / `bp-native-app-fast.lisp` still build a BP
   native author's groups from `Newsgroups`; a signed control article there
   is refused at the binding, not filed. Not changed.
4. `:control-signed` stays in the served-reason tables (`nntp-post.lisp`,
   `*fn-pa-served-reasons*`) though nothing produces it now.
