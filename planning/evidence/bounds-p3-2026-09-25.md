## What changed

- **The checkpoint value** (`books/store-checkpoint-open.lisp`, prefix
  `fn-sco-`). `fn-sco-capture configs P` is
  `(:fn-store-checkpoint P cpr identity consumer topic event-index)`: the
  record list P and the accumulator of every fold `fn-cpo-open-observed`
  runs, after P. The configuration fold is `fn-sco-cpr-prefix`, a copy of
  `fn-cpr-loop` that pauses when the events run out. It records the node
  and both sequence counters, and the configurations still to come are
  those after the counter. The checkpoint holds the exact state and removes
  nothing, so D13 is not a precondition.
- **The record kind and codec** (`books/store-checkpoint-codec.lisp`,
  prefix `fn-scc-`). The existing TREE codec admits naturals below 2^32 and
  four symbols, and the Store state exceeds both, so this is a new codec.
  - The payload is a postfix program for a stack machine. An atom is one
    instruction; a cons is its car's program, its cdr's program, then CONS.
    The atoms are nil, naturals (a length octet then little-endian octets,
    below 2^2040), negative integers, characters, strings, symbols of the
    KEYWORD, ACL2 and COMMON-LISP packages by name, and non-empty octet
    lists.
  - The decoder `fn-scc-run` is a guard-verified loop with an explicit
    stack. Its depth does not grow with the data, and it never calls the
    Lisp reader. The encoder `fn-scc-renc` recurses only on car and loops
    along each list spine.
  - The payload is cut into segments of at most R octets (the profile's
    max-record-octets). Each segment has a 37-octet header (magic FNSC,
    schema 2, index, count, length and sequence S, each u64), the chunk,
    and a 32-octet trailer. The trailer is `fn-frame-trailer` over the
    previous trailer, the header and the chunk, so ACL2 computes it.
  - The file is `store-checkpoint.fnsc` in the store root.
- **The verb** `operator store checkpoint`, parsed in
  `books/native-operator.lisp` and run by
  `fnn-command-state-checkpoint` (host/native/io.lisp:1763) under the store
  lock. It extends the checkpoint the open used over the suffix
  (`fn-sco-extend`), or captures the whole history when none verified, and
  writes it through the byte program.
- **The byte program** `fn-bs-scp-program`
  (`books/byte-store-state-checkpoint-program.lisp`) runs create, cut
  `state-checkpoint-created`, write-all, cut `-written`, fsync-file, cut
  `-staged-durable`, rename to `:root store-checkpoint.fnsc`, cut
  `-replaced`, fsync-dir `:root`, cut `-durable`. The native writer is
  `fnn-state-checkpoint-write` (io.lisp:1738), and its five cuts are in
  `tests/campaign/native_cuts.py` (`STATE_CHECKPOINT_CUTS`: old, old, old,
  either, new).
- **Open** (`fnn-recover`, io.lisp):
  1. Read the checkpoint by ranges (`fnn-state-checkpoint-segments`,
     io.lisp:1578): one no-follow fd, a header, then the rest of that
     segment, each read at most one segment.
  2. Decode it (`fn-scc-decode-segments`).
  3. `fn-sco-select` decides with the observed committed count and K.
  4. On `:checkpoint`, read only the transaction files whose sequence is at
     least S, then call `fn-store-sn-recover-from-checkpoint`
     (host/store-node-host.lisp:251), which calls `fn-sco-open` at
     host/store-node-host.lisp:264.
  5. Otherwise run today's `fn-store-sn-recover`. `status` and `recover`
     print `open=checkpoint:S suffix=k` or `open=full-replay reason=R`.
  If `fn-sco-open` refuses, the host resets and replays in full with
  reason `checkpoint-open-refused`. It never serves a refused checkpoint
  open.
- **Fallback, not refusal.** A missing or corrupt checkpoint, one ahead of
  the history, or a suffix above K falls back to full replay, which the
  design recommends. Nothing here needs a refusal: the checkpoint is
  derived, and replay stays authoritative. With suffix > K the full replay
  is more work than the checkpoint would be. It is chosen because K is the
  operator's bound on what open replays after a checkpoint, and a
  checkpoint that leaves more is stale by that bound.

## Theorems (ACL2 8.7, all certified; manifests below)

- **Keystone, `fn-sn-recover-from-checkpoint-equals-full-recover`**:
  `(equal (fn-sco-open (fn-sco-capture configs prefix) configs frontier suffix)
  (fn-cpo-open-observed configs frontier (append prefix suffix)))`.
  - It has no hypothesis: a history that the recognizer refuses is refused
    by both sides.
  - Its subject is `fn-sco-open`, called at host/store-node-host.lisp:264.
    The full open `fn-cpo-open-observed` is what `fn-store-sn-recover`
    calls.
- **One append lemma per fold of `fn-cpo-open-observed`.** It has six
  passes, not eight: `fn-sf-recover` and `fn-sf-replay-node` belong to
  `fn-sn-recover`, which the host's open does not call.
  - `fn-cpr-loop-append`: the configuration and node fold over P ++ Q is the
    fold paused after P, then resumed over Q from the unconsumed
    configurations.
  - `fn-sco-cpr-prefix-append`.
  - `fn-replay-identity-append`, whose hypothesis is that P is Store events.
  - `fn-cpe-projection-replay-append` (true-list P, natural start). It
    generalizes the existing `-append-one`.
  - `fn-th-prefix-project-append`.
  - `fn-cei-build-append`.
  - The history recognizer is not split. It runs over the carried record
    list: one linear pass, no replay.
- **Supporting theorems:**
  - `fn-sco-extend-of-capture`: the extension of the capture of P over Q is
    the capture of P ++ Q. Hypotheses: P is Store events; Q is a true list.
  - `fn-sco-finalize-of-capture`: under a true-list history, finishing the
    capture of the whole history is `fn-cpo-open-observed`.
  - `fn-sco-capture-of-later-configs`: a configuration whose txid is above
    every covered record does not change the capture. Such a configuration
    is one published after the checkpoint, since configuration and Store
    share one txid frontier. So the keystone applies with the configuration
    history read at open.
  - `fn-sco-select-bounds-the-suffix`: a served checkpoint has status ok,
    `S <= count` and `count - S <= K`.
- **Codec.** `fn-scc-decode-segments-of-segments`: for every tree whose
  program length + 1 and record count are below 2^64 (the u64 header
  width), the segments decode to the value, for any segment size. It is
  built on `fn-scc-run-of-program` and `fn-scc-decode-tree-of-encode`
  (`fn-scc-renc-is-program`: the host's encoder produces the specification
  program).
- **Crash.** `fn-bs-scp-program-crash-is-old-or-new`: at every cut, a
  crash leaves the name bound to the old file with its old octets, absent
  (first publish), or bound to the new inode with exactly the new octets.
  Hypotheses: a quiet store, a fresh stage name, non-empty octets. Open then
  reads the old checkpoint, the new one, or none. Each is refused (full
  replay) or is the capture of a committed prefix, and the keystone applies.
- **Range reads.** `fn-bs-read-ranges-concatenate` (A-HOST-EXCLUSIVE-READ):
  two consecutive ranges, the second read in a later state, are one range
  of the first state's content.
- **`A-HOST-EXCLUSIVE-READ`** is an encapsulate in `books/assumptions.lisp`
  (`fn-assume-host-exclusive-read`, constraint
  `fn-assume-host-exclusive-read-keeps-content`). The range theorem names it
  as its hypothesis.

## Teeth (tests/acl2/store-checkpoint-open-tests, byte-store-state-checkpoint-program-tests)

- **Witness.** config-observed-tests' image: two retention events and a
  configuration at txid 7 after both, split after the first event.
  - The full open succeeds.
  - The checkpoint open equals it.
  - The configuration fold paused with one configuration consumed.
  - The extension equals the capture of the whole history.
  - `fn-sco-replay-result` equals `fn-cpr-replay`.
- **One `must-fail` per slot.** A checkpoint without its consumer, topic,
  event-index, identity, configuration or records slot does not open to the
  full state. So does the capture of a different prefix.
- **One `must-fail` per hypothesis:**
  - an improper suffix for `fn-sco-extend-of-capture`;
  - an improper history for `fn-sco-finalize-of-capture`;
  - a later configuration at txid 0 for `fn-sco-capture-of-later-configs`,
    with its positive case beside it;
  - a range read in a changed state for `fn-bs-read-ranges-concatenate`;
  - a busy store for `fn-bs-scp-program-crash-is-old-or-new`.
- **Select:** served within K. Absent, corrupt, ahead-of-history (S > the
  committed count) and suffix-exceeds-k each give a full replay with that
  reason.
- **Codec, over the witness's checkpoint at 64-octet segments** (more than
  two segments):
  - it is a tree, and it round-trips;
  - the extent of the first header is the first segment's length;
  - reorder, truncation (the last segment dropped), splice (another
    checkpoint's tail) and one flipped octet are each refused;
  - a value with a rational is not encoded (`must-fail`).
- **Byte program:** a reachable run from an old checkpoint and from none.
  At the rename cut both crash images are old-or-new.
- **Hypotheses with no `must-fail`:** the u64 widths of the codec round
  trip, and fn-scc-treep in the codec round trip beyond the rational case.
  A 2^64-octet witness cannot be evaluated.
