The E2 passes exercise production bootstrap, register, position, equal-position
ack, unregister/re-register epoch refusal, independent Store scope refusal,
unrelated article preservation, full replay, and a killed owner after durable
register but before reply (client exit 3, resolved by reopened position).
The ordinary hbox invocation skipped the different-UID `SO_PEERCRED` case
because its runner was not root. A separate, noninteractive `sudo -n` run of
that single test on the **same image** [passed 1/1](native-e2-bp-4f66-logs/test-native-consumer-e2-different-uid.log)
in 0.371 s: a synthetic socket was made reachable at the filesystem level,
`setpriv` changed the client to UID 65534, the owner refused its `position`
without an output file, and the owner UID still obtained the position.
Thus all three E2 cases passed across two invocations, not in one suite run.
Poll, advancing ack,
signed-source references, and consumer inbox processing were not in this
image.

The BP positives reached durable FNBS custody and application handoff, then
reported `BP node delivery request-refused`. This prevented receipt/outbox
and later fault-cut assertions; only absent-trust custody refusal and
ambiguous kind-five non-delivery passed. A focused diagnostic launched the
**same developer core** with a temporary read-only `fnn-owner-core` logging
hook and one synthetic request (not a new image build). Its
[trace](native-e2-bp-4f66-logs/diag-bp-case.log) shows that
`fn-owner-bp-session-principal` received raw announced EID octets and
returned `(:refused :channel)`. The durable held ingress consequently had
principal `nil`, generation 0, and the outer
`fn-owner-bp-request-trustedp` returned `nil`; the inner serialized recheck
and Store application plan were never reached. In
`host/native/bp-service.lisp`, `fnn-bps-tcpcl-ingress` computes parsed
`peer-eid` but passes raw `announced` to that ACL2 function. This is a
caller-contract defect; the original nine failures remain failures until a
source-matched repaired image passes. The diagnostic hook observed decisions
and did not publish or alter a Store result.
