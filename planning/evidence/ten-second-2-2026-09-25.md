## Finding for the farm (not fixed here)

The certificate cache stores `.cert`/`.port` pairs only (`tools/certs.py`).
A book installed from the cache therefore has no `.fasl`. ACL2 prints
"Unable to load compiled file" and loads that book and every book below it
uncompiled. In run 060646Z, 112 of the 223 books in the gate had a `.fasl`.
Includes such as `bp-report-guards` (5.1 s) and `bp-fnbs-replay-append`
(4.9 s) are the largest single cost in the books above, and they are paid
uncompiled. Caching the compiled file with the pair would shorten every
include-heavy book. Such a change belongs to the farm.

These runs certify books at these bytes. They do not certify an integrated
image.
