## 1. Routed queued jobs (PKT-055; bp-routing finding 1; spike deferral 1)

**Queue time.** `books/bp-route-jobs.lisp` `fn-bprt-job-route DEST TABLE NODE
KEEPALIVE SEGMENT TRANSFER` answers the loopback contact route of the boundary
`fn-bprt-outbound-choice` names, with this node's session parameters, or nil.
The host (`fnn-bps-queue-route`, host/native/bp-service.lisp) calls it at the
three queue sites:
- `fnn-bpnode-queue-outbox` (receipts) and `fnn-bpnode-queue-report`
  (reports), host/native/bp-node.lisp, over the owner's table
  (`fnn-bpnode-route-by-owner`, read each pass);
- `fnn-command-bpo-owner-request` (the request carrier),
  host/native/bp-obligation.lisp, over the Store's table.

No route: nothing is queued, no sequence is reserved, and the host logs
`BP queue route destination=… decision=… (not queued; the obligation stays)`.
The receipt stays owed in FNRJ and a later pass queues it; the report intent
stays; the request's durable attempt keeps the pin and the verb exits 2
(`BP obligation request carrier no-route`). The route a job is queued with is
its durable route (the `:queued` record).

**Contact time.** `books/bp-node-contact-driver.lisp` `fn-bpnp-contact-next
ST PEER ROUTING OFFERED` is the one question the host asks before each offer
(`fnn-bpc-drive-contact`, now in bp-service.lisp; every base contact goes
through it: `fnn-bps-attempt-ready` for `bp-service run/resume` and
`bp-obligation request`, `bp-contact tick`, and `fnn-bpnode-send-receipts` in
`bp-node serve`/dispatch). It answers `(:offer (:base (:contact PEER t))
OFFERED' EID)`, `(:held KEY :no-route | :no-live-hop | :route-changed)` or
`(:close)`. The host passes EID to the TCPCL session machine as the expected
node ID (`fnn-bps-send-effect`; before, the expected peer was nil).

**The durable contact-time route (spike deferral 1a).** The deferral names a
record that fixes the address an attempt was made on. Here the address is the
durable `:queued` record's route, and the driver offers a job only when the
contact-time decision names exactly that route; a table change after queueing
holds the job `:route-changed`, reported each contact, until the table routes
it back. No attempt is ever made to an address a durable record does not
hold. What stays open is a durable **re-route** record (a lower-machine
record kind, with codec and replay arm) so that such a job moves to the new
hop instead of waiting; that touches `bp-node-machine` and every BP book
above it, beyond this lane's run budget.

## 2. Once per contact (PKT-054, PKT-140)

The loop's decision is ACL2's. The host threads the OFFERED the driver
answers and stops at the first answer that is not an offer; it no longer
reads the transfer outcome to decide.

## 3. The gated session (PKT-056; bp-routing findings 2 and 3)

- `fn-bpnp-start-one` takes ORDERED, the rows it may offer.
- `fn-bpnp-routed-start` scans `fn-bpnp-routed-rows` of the held rows (those
  `fn-bprt-offer-decision` offers on this session). When no routed row is
  ready and the scan over every row is, it reports that row's decision; every
  row stays held.
- A `:session` open without VIA installs the session and offers nothing.
  `:resume` takes the same optional VIA field (`fn-bpnp-resume-via`) and goes
  through `fn-bpnp-routed-start`; without VIA it offers nothing. The host
  sends neither ungated form.

## 4. PKT-064: the with-issued waits slot is still open (narrower than filed)

`fn-bpnp-with-issued` (books/bp-node-progress.lisp:86) rebuilds the
foundation state through `fn-bpnf-with-issued` → `fn-bpnf-state-with-arrival`,
an 11-element list (slots 0 to 10), then writes slots 12 to 15 back with
`update-nth`, which pads slot 11 with nil. Slot 11 is `fn-bpnp-waits`: the
per-key volatile `:mru`, `:credit` and BP-R17 `(:busy m budget)` waits. So
`fn-bpnp-with-issued` clears every row's waits.

What is already right: every durable-success arm rebuilds with
`fn-bpnp-with-waits … (fn-bpnp-waits st)` (attempt persist :867, forward-result
persist :987, busy delivery and deferral :1229, :1345), and the fence and
recovery paths clear volatile state by design. What is still open: the
`:refused` persist results of the attempt (:879) and forward-result (:996)
arms answer `fn-bpnp-with-issued st nil` and drop every wait, so after a
refused kind-8 or kind-9 publication a busy-deferred local row can be offered
before its backoff reading (the durable busy count still bounds its
retries; MRU and credit waits are re-derived by the next scan). Not closed
here: the repair is `fn-bpnp-with-waits` around those two answers, and the
keep lemmas of both arms in bp-node-progress-premises, -guards, -bridge and
bp-node-machine-gaps (`bpgap-with-issued-fields`) open that shape; it needs
its own step with the witness (a busy-deferred row, then a refused kind-8
publication, then a progress tick before the backoff reading, which must not
deliver).
