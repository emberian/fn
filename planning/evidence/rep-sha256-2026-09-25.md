# SHA-256 over a word stobj (D27 boundary 3), 2026-09-25

Lane `rep-sha256`, branch `lane/rep-sha256` from dev `70759bfb`. The list
model of FIPS 180-4 in `books/sha256.lisp` was 31 to 34% of the POST path's
CPU before the intent-carried lane and 22 to 25% after it
(`rep-intent-2026-09-25.md`): four conses per message octet (the padding
copy, the per-block slices, the schedule consed and reversed twice) and
generic integer arithmetic on undeclared words. This lane executes the same
digest over a single-threaded object and attaches it to the digest seams
under a correspondence theorem; no statement of an existing theorem moved.

## What changed

- `books/sha256-stobj.lisp` (new). The stobj `fn-shs` holds the schedule
  W[0..63] and the hash state H[0..7] as `(unsigned-byte 32)` arrays (288
  bytes). The message is read in place: an octet list by `car`/`cdr` as the
  sixteen words of each block are loaded (`fn-shs-load-list-block`), a
  string by `char` at an index (`fn-shs-load-str-block`); the padding is
  synthesised from the length (`fn-shs-tail-byte`), never built. The word
  primitives are `mbe`s whose `:logic` is the list model's own term and
  whose `:exec` is the same arithmetic on declared words reduced with `mod`
  by 2^32 (SBCL compiles that to a mask on a non-negative fixnum); the
  rounds carry the eight registers as typed arguments. Two entries:
  `fn-sha256-stobj` (any object read as octets; `fn-sha256`'s signature, so
  it attaches) and `fn-sha256-of-string`. Everything is guard-verified.
- `books/crypto-attach.lisp`: `(defattach fn-digest fn-sha256-stobj)` and
  `(defattach fn-frame-digest fn-sha256-stobj)` replace the two lines that
  bound `fn-sha256`. The three constraint discharges are re-proved for the
  stobj entry from the correspondence; no constraint moved.
- `host/native/io.lisp`: the host's own SHA-256 (`fnn-sha256`, its round
  constants, `fnn-rotr`, `fnn-add32`) is deleted; it survived only for the
  diagnostic `sha256` verb, a second implementation of a decision ACL2 owns.
  The verb now hands ACL2 the file as a string (`fnn-octet-string`, a byte
  array to a `(simple-array character (*))` of the same codes) for
  `fn-sha256-of-string`, with no octet list in between.
  `tools/fn_native.py sha256-selftest` is unchanged and is now a check of
  ACL2's digest through the host against hashlib.
- `tests/acl2/sha256-stobj-tests.lisp` (new), `docs/prefixes.md` (`fn-shs-`),
  `planning/proofs.json` (PRF-035 events and evidence).

## Theorems (books/sha256-stobj.lisp; all subjects guard-verified)

- `fn-sha256-stobj-is-sha256`: `(equal (fn-sha256-stobj m) (fn-sha256 m))`
  for every object `m`, no hypothesis. The keystone; an enabled rewrite,
  which is what lets `crypto-attach` discharge the seams' constraints for
  the stobj entry by the list model's own discharges.
- `fn-sha256-stobj-is-sha256-of-octets`: on `fn-sha256-octet-listp m`,
  `(equal (fn-sha256-stobj m) (fn-sha256-of-octets m))`; the instance of
  sha256's `fn-sha256-is-of-octets-on-octets`, which carries that hypothesis
  (the two sides agree on every object; the hypothesis is proof-support).
- `fn-sha256-of-string-is-sha256-of-octets`: for `stringp s`,
  `(equal (fn-sha256-of-string s) (fn-sha256-of-octets (fn-shs-string-octets s)))`,
  the string's character codes in order; the test book proves
  `fn-shs-string-octets` is records-shape's `fn-record-string-octets`.

The proof is the compression function's correspondence, block by block,
each a local lemma: the padded octet at every index
(`fn-shs-pb-is-nthx-of-pad`, against `fn-sha256-pad`), the two readers
against that reading (`fn-shs-str-byte-is-pb`, `fn-shs-list-byte-is-pb`),
the sixteen loaded words (`fn-shs-load-str-block-loads-words16`,
`fn-shs-load-list-block-loads-words16`, against `fn-sha256-words16` of the
model's slice), the extended schedule against the model's reversed
accumulator (`fn-shs-extend-yields-schedule`, through W[t] as the
standard's recurrence and `fn-sha256-schedule-aux` walking it in reverse),
the sixty-four rounds (`fn-shs-rounds-is-rounds`), the state update
(`fn-shs-h-add-h` against `fn-sha256-add8`), one block
(`fn-shs-compress-loaded-is-compress`), all blocks
(`fn-shs-blocks-str-is-blocks`, `fn-shs-blocks-list-is-blocks`) and the
octets out (`fn-shs-h-octets-is-words-octets`). It is a named
correspondence and not an `mbe` because the two computations have
different signatures (a stobj threads through one of them); the word
primitives, whose signatures agree, are `mbe`s.

The padded length is the list model's own expression, `n + 9 + ((55 - n)
mod 64)`; the block count is what `floor-mod-elim` makes of it,
`1 - floor((55 - n)/64)`, so "the padded length is 64 blocks" is one linear
fact (`fn-shs-pad-len-is-blocks`) and the loop needs no division.

Not proved, and unchanged: collision and preimage resistance (A-CRYPTO).
The attachment introduces no axiom.

## Host lines

- `host/native/io.lisp`, the `sha256` verb (search `fn-sha256-of-string`):
  `(fnn-core 'fn-sha256-of-string (fnn-octet-string (fnn-read-regular-bounded ...)))`.
  The file's octets reach the digest as a string, read in place at an
  index; no octet list.
- Every other host digest call is unchanged and still hands ACL2 an octet
  list, because the functions it calls build their preimage as one:
  `fnn-trailer` (`fn-frame-trailer` over the protected prefix),
  `fnn-subject-id` (`fn-store-subject-id-of-payload`), `fnn-obligation-id`
  (`fn-store-obligation-id-of`), and the owner's carried intent identity.
  Their digests now run on the stobj through the attachment, and the list
  they hand over is consumed by `cdr` as it is read, not copied or sliced.
  A digest straight from a payload buffer with no list at all is
  boundaries 6 to 9 of the representation design (the frame over the
  buffer), which this lane's `fn-sha256-of-string` is the entry for.

## Teeth (tests/acl2/sha256-stobj-tests.lisp)

## Findings

- Constant-index `nth` and `update-nth` open into car/cdr/cons forms and
  case-split; a stobj proof that lets them costs thousands of subgoals
  (the first attempt's `fn-shs-p` preservation lemma). The book keeps them
  closed from the stobj on and bridges array reads to the model's `nthx`
  with one rule restricted to reads of an array field.
- `ihs/logops-lemmas` is not certified on the farm's ACL2, so the
  executable branches reduce with `mod` rather than a mask; SBCL compiles
  `(mod x 4294967296)` on a non-negative fixnum to the same instruction.
- ACL2 unifies `(+ 1 j)` with a constant, so a "snoc" rule about
  `(firstn (+ 1 j) xs)` unrolls every `(firstn 16 ...)`; the rule is
  restricted to a symbolic count.
- The host had three SHA-256s before the one-owner lane and one after it
  (the diagnostic verb); now it has none.
