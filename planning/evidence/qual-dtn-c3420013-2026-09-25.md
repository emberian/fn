## What M4's exit clause can now carry

> On frozen image c3420013 (DTN developer core `345357d2…`, through pinned
> dtn7-rs 0.21.0 with zero, one and two relays and in the four-node
> topology), a request whose first hop is SIGKILLed mid-transfer is reported
> **uncertain**. It is re-offered from A's durable job and accepted on
> resume. B takes it into custody once, and B's application decides it once,
> separately from transport acceptance. The returned receipt releases exactly
> the requested obligation only when the delivering neighbour is B itself or
> A's return boundary lists B with `releases-for`; without that row it is
> `receipt-refused` and the obligation stays pinned. A never-requested
> obligation stays pinned. A carrier B does not list is refused
> `source-not-carried` with no receipt. A SIGKILL inside native request
> publication leaves the work pinned, both with the Python-bridge request and
> with native `bp-obligation request` authoring.

It still lacks:
- **Issuer authentication.** The release rests on the listed-relay
  delegation. Signed receipts are not on this image, and its boundary
  grammar refuses the signer rows.
- **Native recovery of a fenced attempt.** After a kill at ATTEMPT DURABLE
  the re-request is refused and the work stays pinned with no verb to
  resolve it. `bp-obligation recover` (BP-R17) is post-cut.
- **The `bp-node resume` verb and busy-delivery deferral** are post-cut.
- **Receipts are forwarded by `bp-contact tick`**, not sent by the node
  itself. There is no ACL2 outbound neighbour or routing decision.
- **Production coverage.** The labs run only on the developer DTN image,
  because their setup uses `store post`. The production DTN image's evidence
  is its module cases above and the interrupted contact.
- **Deployment.** The DTN profile is not deployed. The live node runs the
  default image.
- **Scope of the deaths.** All deaths are process deaths on loopback, not
  power loss.
