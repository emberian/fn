### Migrate to required tonight, or stay unmarked

The step works on this image. `status` shows `history-marker=required`, and
`rollback-check` refuses the kept config. The deputy decides.

- **Stay unmarked tonight (format 8, `config.json.format-7` kept).**
  - Rollback is lossless. The kept config restored under c3420013 was
    rehearsed rc 0 and served, and `rollback-check` answered `sound` over
    the whole history, so any articles accepted after the deploy survive
    the rollback.
  - The marker is still written at every commit and caught up at open.
    While it is present, a lost newest record is refused.
  - The risk: a store whose marker is deleted together with the files it
    covers is admitted (the stated limit of unmarked).
- **Required tonight.**
  - A missing marker or a lost newest record is refused at open (rehearsed:
    `marker-missing`, `history-short-of-marker`). The price is
    availability: a node that meets either stays down (exit 4, and
    systemd's `StartLimitBurst` gives up) until an operator acts.
  - The only rollback to c3420013 is the pre-migration snapshot (rehearsed
    rc 0). **It loses every article accepted after the snapshot.**
  - U1b: a hand copy of the kept config over a required store still opens
    under c3420013 and drops the requirement. `rollback-check` now refuses
    it, so the rollback is safe only if the operator runs that check first.
- **This lane recommends unmarked tonight, and required after a day of
  service**, taken with a fresh snapshot. That keeps a lossless rollback
  through the first day on the new image. Nothing found here argues against
  `required` itself.
