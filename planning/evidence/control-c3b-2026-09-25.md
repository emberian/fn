## Step 1: the owner wiring

### The design

- `books/control-visible.lisp` `fn-ctl-visible-state A WS VERDICTS`: the
  acceptance state A with its article list replaced by
  `fn-ctl-visible-articles`; groups, watermarks, next txid, pending and fence
  are A's, so no local number is reused.
- The refresh kernel: `fn-ctl-refresh-withdrawals NEW OLD WS VERDICTS CFG`
  (when NEW is OLD with one article consed, the record `fn-ctl-cancel-plan`
  decides for it if it is a cancel, prepended to WS; a discontinuity rebuilds
  the records of every article) and `fn-ctl-refresh-visible NEW OLD
  OLD-VISIBLE WS OLD-VERDICTS VERDICTS` (`fn-ctl-visible-add` when NEW is one
  article over OLD and the verdict list grew by at most that article's
  verdict; the whole-archive definition only on a discontinuity: start,
  reopen). Cost of the cons case: |WS| plus, for an executed cancel, one
  pass over the visible list; the tests are `equal` on shared structure (as
  `fn-midx-refresh`).
- `books/owner.lisp`: the view record is `(version frontier archive verdicts
  trie buckets withdrawals raw)`. `fn-own-refresh` computes the records and
  the visible list with the kernel, publishes `fn-ctl-visible-state-of` the
  acceptance state, and keys the trie (`fn-midx-refresh`) and the group
  buckets to the visible list. Every connection opened or advanced copies
  that archive, so every reader is served the visible state.
  `fn-own-refresh-config`: the configuration a record is decided under is
  `fn-ctl-config-at` the archive's next txid over the store's configuration
  journal (`fn-sn-config-history`).
- `books/owner-invariants.lisp`, K1 restated:
  `fn-own-view-okp` now says the view's archive is `fn-ctl-visible-state` of
  its prefix archive under the records and verdicts the view carries, and
  its raw list is the prefix's article list; `fn-own-conn-okp` says a
  connection's archive is a withdrawal projection of its pinned prefix
  (`fn-ctl-projectionp`: the prefix's state with a subsequence of its
  articles). The four served-step K1 theorems
  (`fn-own-read-is-served-step-on-pinned-prefix`, `-after-any-trace`,
  `fn-own-reader-sees-pinned-prefix-replay`, `-after-any-trace`) now name the
  prefix with the connection's article list; `fn-own-conn-serves-a-projection`
  says that list is a subsequence of the prefix's and nothing else differs.

### Theorems

| Theorem | Statement |
| --- | --- |
| `fn-ctl-refresh-visible-is-visible` (keystone) | `OLD-VISIBLE = fn-ctl-visible-articles OLD WS OLD-VERDICTS` and NEW's Message-IDs distinct imply `fn-ctl-refresh-visible NEW OLD OLD-VISIBLE WS2 OLD-VERDICTS VERDICTS = fn-ctl-visible-articles NEW WS2 VERDICTS`, WS2 = `fn-ctl-refresh-withdrawals NEW OLD WS VERDICTS CFG` |

| `fn-own-conn-serves-a-projection` | under the relation, a found connection's archive is its prefix's state with a subsequence of the prefix's articles |

Why the served keystone changed its conclusion: once a cancel executes, its
target leaves the served list while keeping its number in the raw list, so
the former equality over the served list is false (the holder becomes
nil). The property PRF-002 names ("never reuses an allocated number for
another article") is the raw equality plus the served "same or nothing";
both are proved. New local lemmas: `fn-own-freshp-of-tail`,
`fn-own-holder-of-fresh-subseq` (in a fresh list a subsequence's holder is
the list's), `fn-own-relation-view-is-a-subsequence-of-raw`.
Teeth (`tests/acl2/owner-numbering-tests.lisp`): the served conclusion
function is the "same or nothing" form; the raw conclusion is asserted on
the witness (numbers 1 and 2) with the relation must-fail (wound-back node:
raw number 1 becomes the injected article) and the held-before must-fail
(number 3). The fixture has no cancel, so the "nothing" arm has no witness
in this book (the control-visible teeth show a withdrawn target).

## Not done, and why
- Step 2: 423 `withdrawn` by number (today a withdrawn number answers the
  plain 423 of an absent number, since the group buckets are keyed to the
  visible list), `HDR :fn-control`, Supersedes, `fn_verify`'s report.
- The durable binding at recovery. The first refresh after start rebuilds
  the records under the configuration at the archive's next txid, not
  `fn-ctl-journal-withdrawals` at each cancel's own txid; the two agree
  unless a configuration record is appended between a cancel's commit and
  the refresh that publishes it (for example a revoke). The fix needs each
  cancel's Store txid at the refresh (the article record does not carry
  it) and the two relation facts control-c3 named (the pinned verdicts are
  the store's at the pin; later configuration records have larger txids).
  Neither is carried; the view's WS and verdicts are carried, and K1 is
  exact relative to them.
- A connection does not carry its records, so its K1 is the projection
  form (a subsequence), exact only through the view it pinned.
- Peer history: the owner's IHAVE/CHECK test (`fn-pix-history-hasp`) reads
  the view trie with the view's (visible) article list and the node's
  (raw) list; once any target is withdrawn the two differ and the test
  answers by its scan fallback (correct: a withdrawn target is still held;
  not incremental). A raw trie beside the visible one would restore the
  fast path.
- Two-node native cases (authority, no grant on two nodes, the kill between
  arrivals).
