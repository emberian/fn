# P2 wire half: W1, W2, W3 fixed and observed on a developer image — 2026-09-24

Lane `p2-wire` (branch `lane/p2-wire`). This record covers the three NNTP wire
defects the [1a9dd747 campaign](campaign-1a9dd747-2026-09-24.md) found. It
shows the fixes on a developer/production image pair built from this branch,
observed at the wire by `tests/campaign/native_nntp_post_probe.py`. It is a
runtime record plus the certification runs of the changed books. It does not
make the cut campaign pass on a production image (see "What this does not
show").

## The fixes

| defect | fix |
| --- | --- |
| **W1**: an uncertain outcome reached the poster as a bare close | `fnn-owner-stop-service-locked` (`host/native/owner.lisp`) takes the answering connection's socket and does not shut it down. `fnn-owner-handle-chunk` passes its own socket, so the worker sends the ACL2-rendered `441 posting failed; the outcome is uncertain, do not repost` and then closes. The fence is unchanged: `STOPPING` is set under the owner mutex, every other client and the listener are shut, and the process exits 3. |
| **W2**: an EIO at a `finish-*` cut answered a durable article `441 ... refused` | Host: both `fnn-at` calls in `fnn-finish` (`host/native/io.lisp`) now sit inside handlers that fence and raise indeterminate. `fnn-owner-attempt` and `fnn-owner-attempt-transit` share `fnn-owner-attempt-handlers`, which maps an OS error no Store step classified to a fence and `:uncertain`, never `:refused`. Model: `fn-own-outcome-completion` (`books/owner.lisp`) renders a refusal only while no completion has been consumed after the take. `fn-own-consumed-completion-is-240-or-uncertain` (`books/owner-invariants.lisp`) states that after a consumed completion the reply `fn-own-outcome` renders is the 240 for `:durable` and the uncertain line for every other word. The control projection treats a `:duplicate` after a consumed completion as uncertain too. The article completion word is now `fn-own-finish`'s (`host/owner-host.lisp` `fn-owner-finish-submission`, the finish callback of `fnn-owner-attempt`), not the host's phase comparison. |
| **W3**: every store refusal was one `441` line | The host relays the refusal kind from the ACL2 step that refused: `:duplicate` and `:conflict` (`fn-owner-existing-action`, i.e. `fn-sn-existing-action`), `:malformed` (`fn-owner-prepare`'s `:invalid`), `:unaffordable` (`fn-store-publication-admissibility`, or the max-transactions capacity), `:storage-failed` (the new `fnn-store-io-refusal`, raised only at the two known pre-publication failure sites whose reservation ACL2 consumed), and `:refused` for the rest. `fn-post-store-refusal-line` (`books/nntp-post.lisp`) gives each kind its own line. `fn-post-outcome-store-refusal-kinds-are-distinct` and `fn-post-outcome-store-refusal-is-not-uncertain` state that the lines are distinct and that none is the uncertain line. The words are a local policy choice pending ember's decision. |

The owner logs of the W1 rows no longer show `send-reply: [Errno 32] Broken
pipe`. The log for `finish-*:eio` says `uncertain post path=served`, where it
used to say `refused post`.

**Repost of a present article (W3):** the answer is `this article is already
stored here` or `a different article with this Message-ID is stored here`. Which
one comes back varies row by row between the two runs. An NNTP repost is
re-injected with a new `Injection-Date`, so its octets equal the stored ones
only when the clock reading lands in the same second. The same retry therefore
answers duplicate or conflict by timing. This bears on ember's wording decision:
a client cannot act on that distinction for a POST retry. The operator entry is
unaffected, because it retries the stored injection
(`fn-own-operator-retry-resubmits-the-stored-injection`).

## Sub-cases where the uncertain reply is still not sent

- **Uncertainty raised as a condition.** Sometimes uncertainty surfaces as an
  `fnn-store-indeterminate` condition that escapes `fnn-owner-handle-chunk`,
  rather than as the Store word `:uncertain`. Examples are a failed
  `fn-owner-submission-resolution` or feed flush, and an indeterminate from the
  serialized boundary itself. No ACL2 outcome was rendered in that case, so no
  reply exists to send. `fnn-owner-serve-client`'s handler fences and the client
  sees a close. Sending the uncertain line there would be a host-authored reply
  line, which the one-owner rule forbids. The fix belongs in the model: a named
  outcome for "journal uncertain".
- **An uncertainty decided on another connection.** Another connection's outcome
  shuts this one without a reply. That connection had no submission in flight,
  so it is owed no POST reply.

## What this does not show

- **Production image.** The production image still refuses every cut selector,
  by design. The EIO rows ran on the developer image built from the same source
  and books. The two production controls pass.
- **No ACL2 differential.** The byte model's differential did not run. Nor did
  the operator campaign or its 20-cut table on this image.
- **Host computations that remain.**
  - The `:unaffordable` capacity comparison against `max-transactions` is a
    host comparison.
  - `fn-owner-finish`'s phase comparison still decides the word for retention,
    identity and config completions, and `fn-store-sn-finish` still decides it
    for `store post`. None of these has an article submission for
    `fn-own-finish` to name.
- **Wording.** The refusal words are pending ember's decision.
