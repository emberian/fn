## What was NOT exercised

- the gate stopped early: --reuse but there is no tree at $HOME/fn-deploy/52eb0db

Standing gaps of the gate itself, independent of this run:

- A wall time is not a complexity bound. The exponents quoted below are fits
  over a handful of doublings on one host, not theorems about the books.
- One host, one load window. The pre-realignment table this compares against
  was measured on a different box; only the like-for-like phase re-runs its
  method here, and even that is a different machine from its original.
- The series posts through `post_article`, the function the CLI and the served
  POST call. It does not exercise POST over a socket at scale, nor two
  writers, nor any crash point: the deploy and two-node gates own those.
- Articles are one shape (two groups, incompressible body, ordinary headers).
  The hostile-input axes -- folded headers, maximum Message-IDs -- are the
  handoff's grid and are not re-run here.
- The certificates were not re-established here; see the certificate row.
