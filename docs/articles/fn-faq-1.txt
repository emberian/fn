Summary: The words fn uses, and its three answers.

fn uses Usenet's words, plus a few of its own.

*The words:*
- Node: one fn server. Store: the folder holding what it accepted.
- Article: header lines, a blank line, a body.
- Group: a named place for articles, like local.general.
- Message-ID: an article's name, like <abc@example.org>. The same
  on every node. Article numbers are each node's own.
- Peer: a node yours swaps articles with. Feed: that flow.
- Cancel: a request to withdraw an article.
- Principal: a key-based identity. A signed article names one.

*The three answers:*
- accepted: done and saved.
- refused: not done, nothing changed, the reason named.
- uncertain: fn cannot tell whether it was saved. Never repeat it
  blindly (part 5).

*Why it matters:* Received, accepted, forwarded, delivered and read
are different events. fn never reports one as another.
