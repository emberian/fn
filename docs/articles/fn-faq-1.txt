Summary: The words fn uses, and its three answers.

*The words:*
- Node: one fn server. Store: what it has saved.
- Group: a named place for articles, like local.general.
- Message-ID: an article's name, the same on every node.
- Peer: another node yours swaps articles with.
- Cancel: taking back your own article.

*The three answers:*
- accepted: done and saved.
- refused: not done, nothing changed, and fn says why.
- uncertain: fn cannot tell whether it was saved. Do not post it
  again as new; part 5 says what to do.

Sent, saved, delivered and read are different events; fn never
reports one as another.
