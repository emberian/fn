Summary: Newsreaders, the web reader, and cancelling your own post.

Any newsreader with TLS works, and so does a browser (part 11). A
login before TLS is refused.

*A login:* Redeem the invitation code your operator gave you, once:

    fn redeem news.example.org CODE carol --cafile cert.pem

*tin:* Use port 563. Put "HOST PASSWORD LOGIN" in ~/.newsauth, mode
0600, then:

    tin -T -r -A -p 563 -g fn.fg-goose.online

*Cancel:* Your reader's cancel takes back your own post everywhere.

*Commands:* what fn answers, from its protocol table. A * needs a login
where the node requires one (480 otherwise).

- CAPABILITIES (RFC 3977 5.2): What this node offers you now, as a list
  of labels.
- HELP (RFC 3977 7.2): The commands this node answers, one per line.
- MODE (RFC 3977 5.3; RFC 4644 2.3 (MODE STREAM)): MODE READER says
  whether you may post; peers use MODE STREAM.
- QUIT (RFC 3977 5.4): Ends the connection.
- GROUP * (RFC 3977 6.1.1): Selects a group; answers its count and first
  and last numbers.
- LISTGROUP * (RFC 3977 6.1.2): Selects a group and lists its article
  numbers, optionally a range.
- LAST * (RFC 3977 6.1.3): Moves to the previous article in the group.
- NEXT * (RFC 3977 6.1.4): Moves to the next article in the group.
- ARTICLE * (RFC 3977 6.2.1): Sends a whole article, by number, by
  Message-ID, or the current one.
- HEAD * (RFC 3977 6.2.2): Sends an article's header.
- BODY * (RFC 3977 6.2.3): Sends an article's body.
- STAT * (RFC 3977 6.2.4): Says whether an article exists, and moves to
  it by number.
- OVER * (RFC 3977 8.3): Overview lines (subject, author, date, ids,
  size) for a range.
- XOVER * (RFC 2980 2.8): OVER's older spelling.
- HDR * (RFC 3977 8.5): One header field for a range; also :fn-verified,
  :fn-control and :fn-enrollment.
- XHDR * (RFC 2980 2.6): HDR's older spelling.
- XPAT * (RFC 2980 2.9): XHDR filtered by a wildmat on the field's
  content.
- LIST * (RFC 3977 7.6; RFC 6048): Lists groups and server data; the
  second word names which.
- NEWGROUPS * (RFC 3977 7.3): Groups created since a date and time
  (UTC).
- NEWNEWS * (RFC 3977 7.4): Message-IDs of articles accepted since a
  date, in matching groups.
- DATE (RFC 3977 7.1): The node's clock, in UTC.
- POST * (RFC 3977 6.3.1; RFC 5537 3.5): Sends an article; 340 asks for
  it, 240 means it is stored.
- IHAVE (RFC 3977 6.3.2): A peer offers an article by Message-ID (peers
  only).
- CHECK (RFC 4644 2.3): A streaming peer asks whether an article is
  wanted (peers only).
- TAKETHIS (RFC 4644 2.4): A streaming peer sends an article (peers
  only).
- AUTHINFO (RFC 4643 2.3, 2.4): Logs in: AUTHINFO SASL SCRAM-SHA-256 (or
  PLAIN over TLS), or AUTHINFO USER name, then AUTHINFO PASS password.
- STARTTLS (RFC 4642 2.2): Turns on TLS on this connection before you
  log in.
- COMPRESS * (RFC 8054 2.2): Compresses this connection with DEFLATE
  (RFC 8054), after you log in.
- XREDEEM (fn extension, PRF-164): Redeems an invitation code for a
  login.
- XFNCATCHUP * (fn extension, PRF-325): A peer's batched catch-up over
  the articles it may read (peers only).
- XFN-ZARTICLE * (fn extension, NNT-055): Sends an article's payload as
  it is stored when you hold its dictionary (fn peers).
