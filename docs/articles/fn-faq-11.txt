Summary: The node's own web page: turning it on, inviting friends, and
 what it does and does not do.

A node can serve its own web page, so a friend needs only a browser.

*For a friend:* Open the address you were given, press "Make your
account", type the invitation code, choose a name and a password.
The same name and password work in tin. Light, dark or automatic
colours. Phones work.

*After you post:* "Posted!" means saved. "The server said no" means
nothing was published, and why. "We couldn't tell" means look at the
group before sending again.

*For the operator:* turn it on as root, then invite:

    sh /opt/fn/install.sh --reader
    fn operator /var/lib/fn/fn.toml account invite

docs/web.md covers the proxy and the settings.
