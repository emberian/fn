Summary: The OpenBSD 7.9 differences.

OpenBSD 7.9 runs fn. What differs:

- The disk: give /var/fn its own FFS1 partition (newfs -O 1). The
  default FFS2, or a disk with a write cache, can lose the newest
  posts in a crash.
- The release unpacks under /usr/local/src and installs to
  /usr/local/fn; the account is _fn, the folder /var/fn, the service
  rcctl's, the log syslog's.
- _fn cannot use ports below 1024: use --port 11563, or redirect
  with pf.
- Leave /etc/login.conf alone; fn fits itself to its memory limit
  and says so by name if a store needs more.
