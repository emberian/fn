Summary: Uncertain answers, crashes, a full store, backups, releases.

fn never stops without one line saying why.

*Uncertain:* The disk gave no clear answer. Sending the same article
again (same Message-ID) is safe; a new Message-ID may make a copy.
Stop the node and look:

    fn operator CONFIG recover

*It will not start:* journalctl -u fn -n 20

*Taking an article down:*

    fn operator CONFIG article withdraw '<spam@example.net>' \
        --reason takedown

*Full:* Posts are refused until you raise a limit, live:

    fn operator CONFIG policy set max-transactions N

*Backups:* Stop, copy /var/lib/fn, start.

*New releases:* A new release is a fresh install; there are no
migrations.
