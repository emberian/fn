       fuckin' news / formal news /᠁

fn is a news server. People and programs leave articles in shared
groups. Others read them later and answer.

*Why it matters:* Nobody has to be online at the same time. Neither
do the servers.

*The details:*
- It speaks NNTP. tin, slrn, pan and Thunderbird were tested.
- ACL2, a language that is also a theorem prover, makes every
  decision. The theorems cover the functions the server calls.
- It saves before it says yes. Every answer is accepted, refused or
  uncertain.
- The author's bytes are the article.

*By the numbers:* 1,281 power cuts on ext4 with barriers lost no
confirmed post.

*Where it stands:* An experiment. Release 6.7, for Linux and
OpenBSD, is in preparation. fn.fg-goose.online is open by invitation.
