## 3. Where the lanes wandered
- Into fn1's surface. RELEASE-PLAN §1 enumerates fn1's 31 NNTP commands, the 17-gate cut script, the interim tables, and the seats optimized for "zero disagreements with H's fn1 model". That is the right oracle for the NNTP convention and the wrong compass for the product: the dream's clients speak the consumer port and BP, and NNTP is "one convention over the store".
- Into the ACL2 target's taxes. The 32-value limit twice, the assembly books growing with every kind (eight books over the minute today), four whole gates for one landing, certify-time splits: necessary hygiene, but days of it, and each split is a symptom of the machine form's shape (the assembly re-proves every kind's plumbing in one place).
- Into evidence before the thing to be assured exists. The Q1 classification, four contract rows and the four-way controls are good work and were the review's own rulings; but the dream ordered semantics, then dynamics and performance, then assurance, and the tree now has more test lines than book lines before a POST is durable.
- Into per-octet correctness at the cost of the envelope. Every byte on the article path goes through a checked export, which is why the simulator catches so much and why POST will not meet its floor; the dream wanted both, by handles over ranges with the checks at the handle.

## 4. The meat most missing from the bones (ten, each with its owner and whether a form exists)
1. The consumer port: append / read / wait / ack over the one engine, with Mini and helm as the first clients and CNS as the wire (E; the grammar form expresses the wire; a port form (defport, WIDEST) is missing).
2. The live plane: live streams and ephemeral keys on the same scheduler with typed gaps (D/E; the edge-row class and ring are expressible in defcolumns; the subscription edge is missing).
3. The envelope fields the widest role asked for now: a revision stamp and an occurrence id on every event (B's layouts; deflayout expresses it; it is cheap today and a rewrite of every layout tomorrow).
4. Range copies by handle as the default article path, and a FLOOR run of POST and ARTICLE on the socket (A's pool: fn2-pool-copy-across un-demoted; the check moves to the handle).
5. BP and peering designed against the one engine, as record kinds and ports, before any transport code (E4/E5; the design, not the code, is what is owed now).
6. Grant: capabilities with issuer, holder, scope over Spans, caveats that narrow, revocation (D7's principals exist; the form is missing).
7. Retention and obligations as record kinds with lifetimes and removing capabilities (B/C; STO-010; the record kind form expresses it).
8. The DSL succinctness pass: arm bodies in the restricted arm language with the plumbing generated, measured as lines per machine; the machine form should emit its own section book per kind so the assembly never re-proves other kinds (A; the form exists and needs to grow).
9. Reading an article back: the cursor machine over C3's reads and the article's stored location (D6 + S-ARTICLE-LOCATION; in flight).
10. The transport demonstration's first rung: a world (a commit domain) as a row, fork by reference, a cut (WIDEST §2; B/C; the forms exist for the rows, the design is unwritten).

## 5. Reorientation (proposed; ember amends)
- Keep: the spine to a durable POST and a readable article (B-S2, D5 2/n, D6) because "serve" means both; the simulator and the checkers as the gate; the shared-tree method with its landing rules; the evidence rows that exist.
- Stop, until a POST is durable and an article reads back: new evidence machinery (the judge accepts eight kinds; enough); fn1-surface breadth in D beyond what a consumer and a peer need (XPAT, HDR, the LIST variants, LISTGROUP ranges stay interim); the fn1 cut-script matrix work in H; splitting books by hand (the form emits per-kind books instead).
- Start now, in parallel with the spine: (a) the revision stamp and occurrence id in the event envelope before more layouts freeze; (b) the consumer port's design as a port over the engine (CNS wire, cursor/ack, the inbox selection per INBOX §4-5), with Mini's processing transaction as the first fixture; (c) the FLOOR run and the range-copy path; (d) the live plane's edge class; (e) the BP/peering design against the engine; (f) the DSL pass on the machine form.
- Where spine-first bends: M2 is not "fn1's surface on fn2"; M2 is "a consumer appends, reads, waits and acks on the laptop, and an NNTP reader sees the same article". E starts its design now, not after D8.
- Three designs to revisit before more code goes on them: the machine form (hand arms and one shared assembly: the shape that taxes every landing); the pool's export model (per-octet checks vs handles over ranges: the shape that fails the envelope); the surface order (the release plan's §5 serializes E after D's fn1 breadth: invert it).
