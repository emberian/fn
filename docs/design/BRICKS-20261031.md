## 4. The approach (unchanged since 10-31; WORKFLOW-20261031 and FN2-SEAT-COMMON carry it)
A slice is named by the verbs or cuts it flips and accepted by a number on H's line or a fixture asserting real
octets. Vocabulary before bodies: a missing word goes to the brick's owner as an exact form. Statement-first,
then built in the same seat. M2's spine first, breadth behind it. Seats are long-lived and own their layer.
