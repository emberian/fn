# fn2's development flow (main, 2026-10-31, rewritten whole after ember: "these gatescripts are bullshit ... revisit our entire development flow and methodology to reduce redundant and useless and bureaucratic work")

Ember's sentences this flow is built from (EMBER.md, read whole tonight): "can't we do everything we need in the repl while we are swarming?"; "how often do we actually need to qualify an image though? ... only when we want to run the actual dynamic validation"; "we need to be building things so we can do the least amount of work to iterate on exactly and only what is red and not be revalidating things that are slow"; "we do not need to measure before and after each fix"; "stop serializing into slow tests and just 'have faith and move forward'"; "we find bugs by debugging, tracing, and source inspection in this house"; "'evidence archive' why are doing all this rituals about 'evidence.' deep down you know it's just cargo culted bullshit"; "we're not trying to do ledger beaurocracy here we're trying to just make good software"; "push before verify"; "fixing forward is the way forward"; "the gate is the tour"; "any hour-long wait is a defect".

## 1. What a change has to establish, and nothing else
1. **It loads.** The books you changed certify (seconds each; `ld` in the REPL is faster and is where you work). Certify is information, never a ceremony.
2. **The node still behaves.** The simulator's scenarios for what you touched pass in the REPL on the tiny profile (seconds to a minute), and H's nntp model is the oracle for anything that answers a client.
3. **You read what you wrote.** Tracing and source inspection, not reruns.
That is the whole acceptance for a commit. Push. A red that reaches dev is fixed forward by its owner; nothing is reverted, nothing is bisected, nobody waits.

## 2. What is deleted
- **Per-commit whole-tree gates**, in every form: the two-closure landing gate, the "foundation commit certifies the whole closure" rule, the moved-tip and landing-order rules, main's batch gate (written and deleted tonight), the slot-pool choreography around them. A whole closure is run by the nightly, and by a seat only when it changed a form and wants to know, in the background, while it keeps working.
- **The byte-identical A4 baseline comparison.** It cost two closures and two driver sweeps per landing to compare 113 summary blocks against a baseline every seat recomputed. The scenarios' checkers are the regression test; the drivers run nightly.
- **OWED.md regeneration and the export as a commit step.** The ledger is regenerated by the nightly; a commit never carries a regenerated OWED.md. The export's include list is edited when a book is added, and that is all.
- **Per-landing evidence sweeps** (sim-layer-d-evidence at 7,200 s, sim-layer-b's sweep, the Q1 controls harness, the crash-model differential). They are nightly rows. The tiny-profile scenarios in the REPL are the per-change check.
- **"Gate step" reporting.** READY says the sha and one line of what it does. No gate tables in commit messages, no certify-time tables in continuations unless a book crossed a minute (then the number and the fix).

## 3. The three loops
- **Inner (seconds):** a live ACL2 session per seat with its books loaded; edit, `ld`, run the fixture or scenario, read the result. No certify to learn what one definition will say.
- **Commit (minutes):** certify the changed books; push by path; READY. Several times an hour is normal.
- **Nightly (unattended, hours, one script on forge, nobody waits):** the whole closure at dev's tip; every scenario at the concrete profile; A's drivers; H's whole nntp family against the simulator and the socket; the owed export with OWED.md committed by the nightly itself. Reds are posted per file to the owning seat in the morning; the last green nightly sha is what a demonstration or a measurement runs on. Dev being red somewhere overnight is normal.

## 4. Seats
One seat per layer, long-lived, owning its layer's design and order (SEAT-COMMON "You own your layer"). A seat keeps its context by discipline (tail logs, never read a whole certify log or a book over 2,000 lines whole, tee long outputs) and at ~400K asks main for the distill and continues from its own transcript; it never hands off from a notebook. Main carries cross-layer seams and standards, reads the originals itself, rules in the design files as present state, and runs no gates.

## 5. The shape the form rewrite gives this
When a kind certifies alone and the assembly is a registry (MACHINE-FORM), the commit loop's certify is the kind's own book and the nightly's closure stops growing with kinds. Until then the nightly is simply slow, and nobody waits on it either.
