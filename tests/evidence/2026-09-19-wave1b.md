## What this record establishes

  registry events cite theorems that exist, are not shape-suspect, and live in
  certified roots.
- Two portability defects were found and fixed on the way: Linux delivers a
  reset instead of the final 501 when the reader closes with unread input, and
  the corruption matrix's expected diagnostics predated the ACL2-owned frame
  classes (truncated, over-length, over-limit, integrity are now distinct).
- `books/article-properties` is deterministic at 318,552,821 prover steps on
  every quiet run; its failure on the loaded laptop (wave 1a) did not recur on
  any remote gate.
