## Boundaries still open

- The physical adapter is tested Python/program-mode host code. Its write and
  barrier sequence is not yet related by a proved refinement to the isolated-slot
  journal model. OS/filesystem/hardware durability and write isolation remain
  assumptions. Process tests do not qualify power loss.
- Checksums detect damage under their primitive assumptions, not hostile forgery
  or rollback to an older wholly valid store. There is no independent freshness
  anchor. SHA-256 here is a local experimental checksum/content label, not a
  selected native signing or identity profile.
- Replay validity does not prove that every acknowledged physical history
  survives every allowed crash. Typed fault results carry diagnostic prefixes;
  their validity is not permission to resume from them.
- Record round trip does not establish record-level accepted-input canonicality.
  Native messages, signature preimages, and portable batch formats remain open.
- Reservations use abstract units; the adapter separately bounds 128 records,
  32,768 payload octets per article, 65,538 encoded octets per record, and
  8,388,864 total recovery record octets. This is not proved physical-space
  accounting. Staging orphan reclamation and compaction remain future work.
- The CLI stores opaque bytes and requires explicit identity/groups. It does
  not implement full article injection, signed gateway provenance, conflict
  evidence storage, authorized release, or network POST. The reader holds a
  static shared-lock snapshot; there is no concurrent live posting service.
- Raw-execution guard verification, whole-system traces, checkpoint/compaction,
  native signatures, and deployment/flight qualification are unfinished.
  Interpreted bridge helpers, Python, ACL2/SBCL, and installed community books
  remain within the stated trust boundary. This batch adds the local use of
  `arithmetic/top` alongside `ihs/quotient-remainder-lemmas`; the manifest audits
  local sources, not the complete installed system-book dependency closure.

No complete broad requirement or proof target is marked finished merely because
these component results and tests pass.
