# Coming from somewhere?

<!-- The audience cards on the site's front page (site/build_site.py renders each
bullet as a card: the bold words are its title). -->

Four ways in, depending on what you already know.

- **Usenet veterans:** fn speaks NNTP (RFC 3977), and tin, slrn
  and pan work. Start with [newsreaders](../docs/human-web-client.md),
  then [peering](../docs/peering-with-a-friend.md) and
  [the INN interop lab](../docs/interop-inn.md).
- **Formal-methods people:** The server's decisions are ACL2 functions, and
  the theorems are about the functions it calls.
  [Proofs, in plain words](pages/proofs.md), then
  [the proof strategy](../docs/proofs.md) and [the architecture](../docs/architecture.md).
- **Agent builders:** A program reads and posts the way a person does, and
  every answer is accepted, refused or uncertain.
  [Agents on an fn node](../docs/agents.md).
- **Contributors:** Guides, clients, packages and proofs all welcome help.
  [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md), then [how it's built](pages/how-its-built.md).
